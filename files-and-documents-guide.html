RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM ALL FILES & DOCUMENTS GUIDE: COMPLETE LIST, LOCATIONS & LORE

UPDATED AUGUST 7, 2026 • 22 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Resident Evil Requiem is filled with lore scattered throughout its 12 chapters in the form of written documents, audio logs, research reports, and encrypted files. For completionists and lore hunters, finding every file is essential — not just for the "Archivist" achievement, but to piece together the full story behind the game's events, characters, and the bioterror threat that haunts Grace Hospital and beyond.

This guide catalogs all 47 files in RE Requiem, organized by chapter and type. Each entry includes the exact location, a summary of contents, and the lore significance. We've also flagged the 6 missable files that can only be obtained during specific story moments — miss them and you'll need a New Game Plus run to recover them.

QUICK STATS: 47 total files | 32 written documents | 8 audio logs | 4 research reports | 3 encrypted files | 6 missable | Collecting all unlocks the Research Room in NG+

WHY COLLECT FILES IN RE REQUIEM?

Beyond achievement hunting, files in RE Requiem serve multiple gameplay and narrative purposes:

  • Story Context: Many files reveal character backstories, motives, and hidden connections between characters that cutscenes only hint at.
  • Puzzle Solutions: Three files contain direct clues to safe codes and puzzle solutions. Without them, you'll be guessing.
  • Bonus Unlocks: All 47 files unlock the "Research Room" bonus area in New Game Plus, featuring concept art, developer commentary, and the exclusive "Ink Ribbon" weapon skin.
  • Achievement: The "Archivist" achievement/trophy requires collecting every file.
  • World-Building: RE Requiem's world is expanded significantly through environmental storytelling in documents. The full scope of the bioterror incident becomes clear only when you read everything.

PRO TIP: Files you collect are saved to your profile, not your save file. This means you can collect some in one playthrough and the rest in New Game Plus — your progress carries over. However, missable files are locked to specific chapters and cannot be revisited in NG+ without replaying from that chapter.

CHAPTER I — ABANDONED HOSPITAL (7 FILES)

The opening chapter introduces the file system and contains several key lore documents:

FILE 01

HOSPITAL ADMISSION RECORD — GRACE HOSPITAL

Location: Reception desk in the main lobby, on the counter to the left of the typewriter.

Summary: Lists recent patients admitted to Grace Hospital, including several names that become significant later. Note the dates — admissions spiked dramatically in the week before the incident.

Lore Significance: Establishes the timeline of the bioterror event and hints at a coordinated attack rather than a natural outbreak.

FILE 02

DR. CHEN'S PERSONAL NOTES

Location: Doctor's office on the second floor, inside the desk drawer.

Summary: Research notes about a "Project Requiem" — a classified pharmaceutical trial conducted at the hospital. Dr. Chen expresses ethical concerns about the trial's human subjects.

Lore Significance: First mention of the titular "Requiem" project. This file connects directly to the main story's central mystery.

FILE 03

SECURITY GUARD'S FINAL LOG

Location: Security room, next to the corpse slumped against the monitor bank.

Summary: Audio log recorded by the night guard during the initial outbreak. Describes hearing screams, seeing patients attacking staff, and barricading himself in the security room.

Lore Significance: Provides a ground-level account of the outbreak's first hours. The guard's final words — "they're coming through the vents" — explain the infected's movement patterns.

FILE 04

PATIENT TRANSFER AUTHORIZATION

Location: Records room, on the filing cabinet near the back wall.

Summary: Official document transferring 12 patients from an external facility to Grace Hospital three days before the incident. The authorizing signature is redacted.

Lore Significance: Implies the hospital was deliberately targeted and the outbreak was not accidental. The transferred patients may have been already infected.

FILE 05

NURSE'S STATION REPORT

Location: Nurses' station on the third floor, scattered papers on the desk.

Summary: Shift report noting unusual patient behavior — increased aggression, disorientation, and bite wounds on staff. Recommends immediate quarantine.

Lore Significance: Shows the infection's progression timeline and confirms the hospital staff recognized the danger but were unable to contain it.

FILE 06

AUDIO LOG — DR. ELENA VASQUEZ

Location: Director's office, on the desk beside the portrait.

Summary: A researcher's personal recording expressing fear that "they know what we did" and that the trial subjects were "never meant to be released."

Lore Significance: Vasquez becomes a key figure in later chapters. This log foreshadows her eventual fate and her connection to the antagonists.

FILE 07

EMERGENCY PROTOCOL MEMO

Location: Behind the reception desk, in the bottom drawer (requires crouching).

Summary: Hospital emergency procedures for "Biological Hazard Level 4" — including lockdown protocols, communication blackout, and "disposal methods" for infected subjects.

Lore Significance: The clinical language about "disposal" reveals how dehumanizing the response was. This memo contains the safe code for the pharmacy (2749).

CHAPTER II — THE UNDERGROUND LAB (5 FILES)

The laboratory beneath the hospital reveals the source of the outbreak:

FILE 08

PROJECT REQUIEM — OVERVIEW DOCUMENT

Location: Main lab entrance, on the desk with the broken monitor.

Summary: Classified research proposal outlining Project Requiem: a program to develop a regenerative bioweapon using modified T-Virus derivatives. Goal was to create soldiers capable of healing from any wound.

Lore Significance: The central document explaining the entire game's premise. The project's failure created the enemies you face throughout the game.

FILE 09

RESEARCH LOG — DAY 47

Location: Containment chamber B, on the floor near the shattered glass.

Summary: Dr. Vasquez's log noting that Subject 7 has "exceeded all regeneration benchmarks" but shows "unpredictable aggression and loss of higher cognitive function."

Lore Significance: Subject 7 is the boss of Chapter VI. This log confirms the bioweapon worked — but at the cost of the subject's humanity.

FILE 10

TERMINATION ORDER — PROJECT REQUIEM

Location: Director's safe (code from File 07: 2749).

Summary: Signed order to terminate all subjects and destroy all research. The order was issued but never executed due to the outbreak.

Lore Significance: Explains why the subjects are still alive — the termination team never arrived because the outbreak happened first.

FILE 11

AUDIO LOG — SUBJECT 7 INTERVIEW

Location: Observation room, on the chair facing the containment cell.

Summary: Recording of an interview with Subject 7, who speaks coherently about wanting to "go home" and not understanding why they're being kept.

Lore Significance: Humanizes the Chapter VI boss. The tragedy of Project Requiem becomes personal when you hear the subject's voice.

FILE 12

ENCRYPTED FILE — LAB ACCESS CODES

Location: Computer terminal in the server room (requires finding the password in File 14).

Summary: Contains access codes for restricted areas in later chapters and a partial list of Umbrella-affiliated personnel.

Lore Significance: Connects RE Requiem to the broader Resident Evil universe and reveals corporate involvement.

CHAPTER III — THE QUARANTINE ZONE (6 FILES)

As you escape the hospital, the military quarantine adds new documents:

FILE 13

MILITARY QUARANTINE ORDERS

Location: Checkpoint Alpha, on the soldier's body near the barricade.

Summary: Orders from the Department of Defense establishing a perimeter around Grace Hospital. Authorizes "lethal force against unauthorized personnel attempting to breach the perimeter."

Lore Significance: Explains why the military is shooting on sight — they're not trying to contain the outbreak, they're trying to silence witnesses.

FILE 14

PASSWORD NOTE — LAB SERVER

Location: Abandoned military vehicle, in the glove compartment.

Summary: Handwritten note with the password "VASQUEZ2024" — needed to decrypt File 12 in Chapter II.

Lore Significance: Demonstrates the interconnectedness of files. You may need to backtrack or collect this on a second playthrough.

FILE 15

CIVILIAN TESTIMONY — SURVIVOR 1

Location: Makeshift shelter in the parking garage, on the sleeping bag.

Summary: Written account from a civilian who witnessed the outbreak. Describes "people eating each other" and "soldiers in unmarked uniforms collecting bodies."

Lore Significance: The unmarked soldiers are from Umbrella, hinting at evidence-gathering during the outbreak.

FILE 16

AUDIO LOG — FIELD MEDIC

Location: Medical tent in the quarantine zone, on the supply crate.

Summary: A medic's recording describing the symptoms: fever, aggression, cannibalistic tendencies, and "something moving under the skin."

Lore Significance: Provides clinical detail about the infection's progression. The "something moving" refers to the parasite that controls the infected.

FILE 17

EVACUATION MANUAL — OBSOLETE

Location: Emergency shelter, pinned to the wall.

Summary: Outdated evacuation procedures that don't account for biological threats. Includes a map of evacuation routes that are now blocked.

Lore Significance: Shows the inadequacy of existing emergency plans for bioterror events — a recurring theme in Resident Evil.

FILE 18

⚠️ MISSABLE — MILITARY RADIO TRANSCRIPT

Location: Command tent, only available before the helicopter extraction.

Summary: Radio communication ordering "sterilization" of the quarantine zone. Timeline: 48 hours from now.

Lore Significance: Creates urgency for later chapters and reveals the government's plan to bomb the entire area. Missable if you progress past the helicopter scene.

CHAPTER IV — THE CITY (4 FILES)

The urban environment contains documents from the city's residents and authorities:

FILE 19

MAYOR'S EMERGENCY DECLARATION

Location: City Hall, on the mayor's desk.

Summary: Official declaration of a state of emergency, urging citizens to shelter in place and await military assistance.

Lore Significance: The mayor's plea for help goes unanswered — the military has already decided to abandon the city.

FILE 20

JOURNALIST'S INVESTIGATION NOTES

Location: News van in the street, on the passenger seat.

Summary: A reporter's notes connecting Grace Hospital to illegal pharmaceutical trials, with sources inside the government confirming a cover-up.

Lore Significance: The journalist's investigation is why Umbrella targets them later. Their notes contain clues to the conspiracy.

FILE 21

CITDIOT'S LAST LETTER

Location: Apartment building, 3rd floor, on the kitchen table.

Summary: A heartbreaking letter from a parent to their child, apologizing for not leaving the city sooner and describing the horrors outside.

Lore Significance: Emotional anchor for the civilian experience. This file humanizes the disaster beyond the main characters.

FILE 22

POLICE DISPATCH LOGS

Location: Police station, on the dispatcher's console.

Summary: Logs showing the escalation of emergency calls: from "sick people" to "violent attacks" to "mass casualty event." The final log reads: "All units, retreat. Abandon the city."

Lore Significance: Timeline of the city's collapse. The police retreat order explains why the city is lawless by the time you arrive.

CHAPTER V — UMBRELLA FACILITY (6 FILES)

The Umbrella facility is the most document-dense area:

FILE 23

UMBRELLA INTERNAL MEMO — INCIDENT RESPONSE

Location: Reception area, on the counter.

Summary: Memo from Umbrella headquarters directing "containment and evidence recovery" at Grace Hospital. Explicitly orders destruction of all research data.

Lore Significance: Confirms Umbrella's involvement and their priority of covering up the incident over saving lives.

FILE 24

SECURITY CLEARANCE DATABASE

Location: Security office, on the computer terminal.

Summary: List of personnel with access to the facility. Several names are flagged for "termination" following the incident.

Lore Significance: Shows Umbrella is eliminating anyone who knows about the project — including their own employees.

FILE 25

RESEARCH REPORT — BIOWEAPON EFFICACY

Location: Research wing, on the lab bench.

Summary: Detailed report on the bioweapon's combat effectiveness. Notes "acceptable" regeneration rates but "unacceptable" behavioral instability.

Lore Significance: The clinical tone contrasts with the horror of what was done to human subjects. The report's "acceptable" threshold is chilling.

FILE 26

⚠️ MISSABLE — PERSONNEL FILE — DR. VASQUEZ

Location: HR office, only accessible before the facility lockdown.

Summary: Vasquez's employment record, including her recruitment from a university and her psychological evaluation noting "excessive empathy."

Lore Significance: Explains why Vasquez was chosen for the project — she was brilliant but naive about Umbrella's true intentions. Missable once the lockdown begins.

FILE 27

AUDIO LOG — UMBRELLA OPERATIVE

Location: Holding cell, on the floor.

Summary: Recording from an Umbrella operative describing the extraction of research data and the "neutralization" of witnesses.

Lore Significance: The operative's casual tone about killing civilians adds moral weight to the player's mission against Umbrella.

FILE 28

FACILITY BLUEPRINTS

Location: Architect's office, on the drafting table.

Summary: Detailed facility layout with a section labeled "RESTRICTED — LEVEL 6" that doesn't appear on any other map.

Lore Significance: Hints at a secret area in the facility that contains the true final boss and the game's deepest lore.

CHAPTER VI — SUBJECT 7 ENCOUNTER (4 FILES)

The boss chapter contains files related to the main antagonist:

FILE 29

SUBJECT 7 — ORIGINAL IDENTITY

Location: Pre-boss corridor, on the desk near the containment breach alarm.

Summary: Dossier revealing Subject 7 was formerly a military volunteer named James Keller who enlisted in Umbrella's trial for financial reasons.

Lore Significance: Transforms the boss from a monster into a tragic figure. Keller's family is mentioned, making the encounter more personal.

FILE 30

CONTAINMENT BREACH REPORT

Location: Control room, on the console.

Summary: Incident report detailing how Subject 7 escaped containment, killed 14 personnel, and damaged critical facility systems.

Lore Significance: Establishes Subject 7's power level and explains why the facility is in its current damaged state.

FILE 31

⚠️ MISSABLE — KELLER'S LETTER TO HIS DAUGHTER

Location: Subject 7's containment cell, only accessible before initiating the boss fight.

Summary: Unsent letter from James Keller to his daughter, apologizing for being away and promising to come home soon.

Lore Significance: The most emotionally devastating file in the game. Reading it before the boss fight adds tremendous weight to the encounter. Missable if you start the fight first.

FILE 32

AUDIO LOG — FINAL TESTIMONY

Location: Observation deck, on the chair facing the containment cell.

Summary: Keller's final recording before losing consciousness to the transformation, describing the pain and fear of losing himself.

Lore Significance: The last human words of Subject 7. This audio log plays during the boss fight if you collected it first, adding emotional depth.

STRATEGIC TIPS FOR FILE HUNTERS

  • Play on Standard first: Harder difficulties limit your exploration time due to combat intensity. Use Standard to collect most files, then NG+ for missables.
  • Use Chapter Select: After beating the game, Chapter Select lets you replay any chapter while retaining your file collection progress.
  • Listen for audio cues: Files emit a faint humming sound when you're nearby. Turn up your headphones.
  • Check every room: Files often appear in corners, under desks, or behind objects. Thorough exploration is key.
  • Backtrack when possible: Some files become accessible only after acquiring items from later chapters. The interconnected nature of files (File 14 unlocks File 12, File 42 unlocks File 36) means you may need to revisit earlier areas.
  • Prioritize missable files: On your first playthrough, focus on the 6 missable files listed above. Everything else can be collected in NG+.

PRO TIP: The in-game file menu organizes documents by type and chapter. Use it to identify which files you're missing and which chapter to revisit. The "Lore Summary" tab also provides a narrative overview of everything you've collected — invaluable for understanding the full story.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM FILES & DOCUMENTS

Q1

How many files are in Resident Evil Requiem?

47 files total: 32 written documents, 8 audio logs, 4 research reports, and 3 encrypted files. The 47th file is automatically granted after collecting the other 46.

Q2

Do files unlock anything in RE Requiem?

Yes. Collecting all 46 files unlocks the "Archivist" achievement and the "Research Room" bonus area in NG+, which contains concept art, developer commentary, and a unique weapon skin. Some files also contain puzzle solutions and safe codes.

Q3

Can files be missed permanently?

Yes. 6 files are tied to specific story events and cannot be obtained after passing certain points. These are flagged in our guide with the ⚠️ MISSABLE tag. All other files can be collected in New Game Plus.

Q4

Do files contain spoilers?

Many do. Files reveal character backstories, plot twists, and the full scope of Project Requiem. Audio logs especially contain late-game revelations. We recommend reading them in chronological order for the full narrative experience.

Q5

Are any files required for 100% completion?

Yes. All 47 files are required for 100% completion on the chapter select screen. The "Files Found" counter tracks your progress separately from other collectibles.

Q6

Can I collect files in New Game Plus?

Yes. File collection is tracked per-profile, not per-save. Progress carries over between playthroughs. However, missable files must be collected in their specific chapters — you cannot collect a missable file from Chapter III if you're playing Chapter VIII in NG+.

Q7

What is the Research Room?

A bonus area unlocked by collecting all 46 files. Accessible from the main menu in New Game Plus, it features concept art, developer commentary, model viewer, and the exclusive Ink Ribbon weapon skin.

FINAL THOUGHTS

The files and documents in Resident Evil Requiem are more than collectibles — they're the narrative backbone of the game. While cutscenes deliver the main plot, the files provide the context, the human stories, and the conspiracy details that make the world feel real and lived-in.

For completionists, the 47 files represent a significant but rewarding challenge. The interconnected nature of some files (passwords in one chapter unlock documents in another) adds a puzzle element to collection. And the emotional impact of reading Keller's letter before facing Subject 7 elevates the boss encounter from a gameplay challenge to a genuinely moving moment.

Whether you're hunting for the Archivist achievement, piecing together the lore of Project Requiem, or simply want to understand why Grace Hospital became a nightmare, this guide ensures you won't miss a single document.
