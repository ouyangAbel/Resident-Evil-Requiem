LOADOUT GUIDE

RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM: BEST LOADOUT & BUILD GUIDE

JULY 29, 2026 • 14 MIN READ
Share

Choosing the right loadout in Resident Evil Requiem can mean the difference between a flawless run and a frustrating reload. Unlike previous entries in the series, RE Requiem gives you unprecedented flexibility in how you equip Grace — from weapon combinations and accessory loadouts to item management and upgrade priorities. Whether you're pushing for a speedrun time, braving Professional difficulty, or exploring every corner for 100% completion, this guide covers the optimal builds for every playstyle.

We've tested dozens of configurations across multiple difficulty levels and playthroughs to bring you the definitive loadout guide. Each build is tailored to a specific approach, with clear explanations of why each choice works, so you can adapt on the fly when a chapter throws you a curveball.

Key Insight: RE Requiem's loadout system rewards specialization over generalization. Unlike RE4's briefcase, there's no penalty for carrying "too many" weapons — but accessory slots are extremely limited. The real build ceiling comes from accessory synergies, not weapon count.

UNDERSTANDING THE LOADOUT SYSTEM

Before diving into specific builds, let's break down how loadouts work in RE Requiem. Grace has four weapon slots (Primary, Secondary, Sidearm, Melee), two accessory slots, and an item pouch with six compartments. The Merchant can expand your pouch to eight slots for 50,000 Lei, which is almost always worth the investment by Chapter 3.

Accessories are where the real power lies. Each one occupies one of two slots and provides a passive effect — some stack, some don't. The Cat Ears accessory (infinite sub-weapon ammo) is so dominant that many builds are designed around it. Other top-tier accessories include the Leihoo Talisman (slow HP regen), Gold Egg (2x Lei drops), and the Ring of the Damned (+20% damage but +15% damage taken).

BUILD 1: THE SURVIVALIST

Best for: First-time players, Professional difficulty, resource-heavy chapters

The Survivalist is the most versatile build in the game — and the one we recommend for your first playthrough. It prioritizes sustainability over raw damage, ensuring you always have answers for whatever RE Requiem throws at you.

Survivalist Loadout
SUSTAIN & CONQUER
W-870 Shotgun | LE5 Handgun | Knife | 2x First Aid Med

Tank hits, control crowds, and outlast every encounter. The shotgun's knockback keeps enemies at bay while the LE5 handles precise weakpoint damage. Always carry maximum healing items.

Weapon Choices

  • Primary — W-870 Shotgun: The best all-around weapon through Chapter 8. Its crowd control is unmatched, and the fully upgraded version one-shots most standard enemies at close range. Upgrade path: Damage → Reload Speed → Capacity.
  • Secondary — LE5 Handgun: Fast, accurate, and ammo-efficient. The LE5 is your workhorse for distant enemies and weakpoint targeting. Upgrade path: Critical Rate → Damage → Penetration.
  • Melee — Knife: Essential for finishing downed enemies and saving ammo. The knife in RE Requiem has a lunge attack that covers surprising distance — master it for crowd management.

Accessory Picks

  • Leihoo Talisman: Slow health regeneration means you use fewer healing items over time — the best defensive accessory in the game.
  • Ring of the Valley: Reduces damage taken by 10% with no downside. Pairs perfectly with the Talisman for a tanky setup.

Item Loadout

  • 2x First Aid Med (healing)
  • 1x Mixed Herb (G+R) for emergency full heal
  • 1x Grenade (crowd panic button)
  • 1x Flash Grenade (boss stun)
  • 1x Gunpowder (ammo crafting on the go)

Pro Tip: On Professional difficulty, swap the LE5 for the Broken Butterfly revolver once you reach Chapter 6. The massive damage per shot justifies the slower fire rate when every bullet counts.

BUILD 2: THE PREDATOR

Best for: Aggressive players, boss rushes, Standard difficulty speed clears

If you'd rather obliterate enemies before they can touch you, the Predator build turns Grace into a walking arsenal. This loadout sacrifices defensive utility for overwhelming offensive power — perfect for players who've memorized enemy patterns and want to minimize time-to-kill.

Predator Loadout
PURE OFFENSE
CQ Assault Rifle | Broken Butterfly | Knade (Grenade Launcher) | Flash Grenades

Eliminate threats before they reach you. The assault rifle handles groups while the Broken Butterfly punches through armor. The Knade clears rooms in seconds. Glass cannon — but what a cannon.

Weapon Choices

  • Primary — CQ Assault Rifle: Unlocked in Chapter 5, this full-auto beast mows down groups. Best used in short bursts to conserve ammo. Upgrade path: Damage → Fire Rate → Recoil Control.
  • Secondary — Broken Butterfly: The highest single-shot damage of any handgun. Perfect for boss weakpoints and armored enemies. Upgrade path: Damage → Critical Rate → Stun.
  • Special — Knade: A grenade launcher that turns crafting materials into explosive payoff. Each shot deals massive AoE damage — save it for crowds and mini-bosses.

Accessory Picks

  • Ring of the Damned: +20% damage output. When you're killing enemies in 1-2 hits anyway, the 15% extra damage taken is a trade worth making.
  • Eagle Eye Charm: Increases critical hit rate by 15%. With the Broken Butterfly's already high crit rate, this pushes you into one-shot territory on many enemies.

BUILD 3: THE PHANTOM

Best for: Stealth enthusiasts, ammo conservation, NG+ exploration

RE Requiem rewards stealth far more than any previous Resident Evil game. Many encounters can be entirely bypassed, and enemies that are unaware take triple damage from stealth attacks. The Phantom build maximizes these mechanics for a near-invisible playthrough.

Phantom Loadout
UNSEEN AND UNSTOPPABLE
Suppressed LE5 | Knife | Throwing Knife x10 | Smoke Grenades

Avoid detection entirely or strike from the shadows for massive bonus damage. The suppressed LE5 handles emergencies without alerting the whole room. Smoke grenades provide escape options when things go loud.

Stealth Mechanics

Enemies in RE Requiem have a detection meter that fills when you enter their vision cone. Sprinting, gunfire, and flashlight use accelerate detection. Crouching and moving slowly keeps you hidden even at moderate distances. Stealth attacks (from behind or above) deal 3x damage — enough to one-shot most standard enemies with the knife.

Accessory Picks

  • Soft-Sole Boots: Eliminates footstep noise. Enemies can still see you, but they can no longer hear you approaching from behind.
  • Shadow Veil: Reduces detection range by 40%. Combined with crouching, you can walk past most enemies without triggering awareness.

Stealth Mastery: Chapters 3, 7, and 9 have the most bypassable encounters. A skilled stealth player can complete Chapter 7 without firing a single shot. Save your ammo for unavoidable boss fights.

BUILD 4: THE SPEEDRUNNER

Best for: Any% speedruns, S+ rank attempts, time attack mode

Speedrunning RE Requiem requires a completely different philosophy. Every second counts, which means skipping fights, exploiting movement tech, and using the most broken combinations the game offers. This is the build used by the current Any% world record holder.

Speedrunner Loadout
TIME IS AMMUNITION
Cat Ears | Sam Edge (Infinite Ammo) | Knife | Nothing Else

The Cat Ears + infinite ammo weapon combo removes all resource constraints. Carry nothing that slows you down. Every enemy is optional — only fight when the route demands it.

The Cat Ears Strategy

The Cat Ears accessory grants infinite ammo to your sub-weapon (throwing knives, grenades, grenade launcher). When combined with the infinite ammo reward weapon from the Merchant (unlocked after Professional completion), you have unlimited ammunition for both your primary and melee options. This frees your entire item pouch for movement and utility items.

Movement Tech Integration

  • Slide-Canceling: Sliding into a direction change maintains momentum. Chain slides to move 30% faster than sprinting.
  • Knife Lunge Skips: The knife's lunge attack has a forward burst that can skip past trigger zones. Essential for the Chapter 9 sequence break.
  • Door Cancel: Opening doors while sliding preserves speed. Never stop moving through doorways.

PRO TIP #1

Upgrade Priority Matters More Than Build

A fully upgraded W-870 outperforms a stock CQ Assault Rifle. Focus upgrade materials on your primary weapon before spreading resources. The merchant's +40% damage upgrade is the single biggest power spike in the game — prioritize it above all else.

BEST LOADOUTS BY CHAPTER PHASE

RE Requiem's difficulty curve isn't linear — certain chapters are massive difficulty spikes that demand specific adjustments. Here's how to adapt your build across the game's three acts:

Chapters 1–4: The Estate Arc

Enemy density is low and resources are plentiful. Run any build you like — this is the time to experiment. We recommend starting Survivalist and switching to Predator once you're comfortable with enemy patterns. The W-870 obtained in Chapter 2 will carry you through the entire arc.

Chapters 5–8: The Hospital Arc

Difficulty jumps significantly. Professional players should have the Leihoo Talisman equipped by Chapter 5. Enemies hit harder, appear in larger groups, and the Amalgam enemies (introduced in Chapter 6) require precise weakpoint targeting. Switch to the LE5 or Broken Butterfly for these encounters — shotgun spread is too inaccurate for Amalgam cores.

Chapters 9–12: The Endgame Arc

Full specialization time. By now you should have your primary weapon fully upgraded and your accessory slots optimized. The final chapters introduce the toughest enemies in the game — Shadow Amalgams and the Forgotten King. Run your strongest build without compromise. If you've been saving the Knade or infinite ammo weapons, deploy them here.

PRO TIP #2

The Gold Egg Economy

Equip the Gold Egg accessory (+50% Lei drops) during Chapters 3–6 when you're still accumulating wealth. By Chapter 7, swap it out for a combat accessory — the Lei difference is negligible once you know farm spots. The farming guide covers the best Lei-per-minute locations.

COMMON LOADOUT MISTAKES

After hundreds of hours across all difficulties, we've identified the most effective configurations — but also the traps that catch players off-guard:

  • Over-investing in melee: The knife is essential, but melee-focused builds fall apart on Professional where enemies stagger-lock you. Always keep a ranged option.
  • Ignoring accessory synergy: Two S-tier accessories don't always combine well. Cat Ears + Gold Egg is a waste — you won't need infinite grenades AND maximum Lei. Build around a single strategy.
  • Carrying too many healing items: Three First Aid Meds is the sweet spot. Beyond that, you're wasting pouch space that could hold ammo or utility. Trust the Leihoo Talisman to cover sustained healing.
  • Not switching weapons per chapter: The W-870 is dominant early but falls off late game. Don't get comfortable — swap to the CQ or Broken Butterfly when enemy armor values increase.

The Golden Rule: Your loadout should match your comfort level, not a meta list. If you're dying constantly, switch to Survivalist even on Standard. If everything feels too slow, try Predator. RE Requiem is about fun first — optimize second.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the best loadout for Professional difficulty?

For Professional difficulty, the Survivalist build is the safest choice: W-870 Shotgun (upgraded), LE5 Handgun, full healing items, and the Leihoo Talisman + Ring of the Valley accessory combo. Prioritize defense and crowd control over raw damage output.

Which build is fastest for speedrunning RE Requiem?

The Speedrunner build with Cat Ears and an infinite ammo weapon (Sam Edge) is the meta for Any% speedruns. Skip all non-essential fights, use slide-canceling for movement, and carry only utility items. Current world record is under 2 hours using this setup.

Should I use the same loadout for the entire game or switch per chapter?

A hybrid approach works best: run a versatile Survivalist build for Chapters 1–4, then transition to your preferred specialized build by Chapter 5. By Chapter 8, fully commit to your build based on your playstyle and the challenges ahead.

What are the best accessories to equip in Resident Evil Requiem?

The S-tier accessories are Cat Ears (infinite sub-weapon ammo) and Leihoo Talisman (slow HP regen). For offense, Ring of the Damned (+20% damage) and Eagle Eye Charm (+15% crit rate) are excellent. For stealth, Shadow Veil and Soft-Sole Boots are mandatory.
