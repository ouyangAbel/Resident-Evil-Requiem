MAP SYSTEM OVERVIEW & MECHANICS

RE Requiem uses a layered map system displayed when you open the pause menu. The map shows your current area with color-coded markers: blue for your position, yellow for objectives, red for locked doors/interactables, and green for unlocked shortcuts. Areas you've visited but not fully explored display with a faded "incomplete" border until you enter every room in that zone.

Map Legend Key: The in-game map uses specific icons — a key symbol means a key item is required, a skull indicates high enemy density, a shopping cart marks Merchant proximity, and a document icon signals readable lore. Tap any icon on the map screen to see a tooltip description. On Hardcore difficulty, enemy density markers are removed, raising the exploration challenge significantly.

FAST TRAVEL: ALL TYPEWRITER LOCATIONS

Fast travel activates once you interact with any typewriter. You can then warp between any unlocked typewriter directly from the map screen. There are 9 typewriters across the game — one per chapter plus two bonus placements in larger chapters. Using fast travel does not consume resources and does not advance in-game time or enemy respawns.

Typewriter Save Tip

Each typewriter has exactly one save slot. On Standard difficulty, saves are unlimited. On Hardcore, each typewriter accepts only 3 saves total before locking — use them strategically. On Nightmare, saving consumes a Ink Ribbon found in the world (roughly 15 available per playthrough).

HIDDEN & SECRET AREAS

Beyond the chapter-specific hidden zones, RE Requiem contains several cross-challenge secrets that reward curiosity:

The Developer Room

Accessible only in New Game Plus after obtaining all weapon upgrades. The Developer Room appears behind a previously impassable door in the Laboratory chapter. Contains joke weapons, developer messages, and the coveted Cat Ears accessory (infinite ammo). This is the game's ultimate exploration reward.

Secret Ending Lore Fragments

Six hidden Lore Fragments are scattered across the game in out-of-the-way locations. Collecting all six is required for the secret ending. Fragment locations: Estate attic (I), Garden fountain (II), Hospital rooftop (IV), Lab Archivist's Office (VI), Forgotten City Sunken Library (IX), and Dimensional Rift edge (X). Each appears as a faint blue glow only visible when nearby.

Merchant Secret Inventory

The Merchant unlocks a secret inventory tier after you sell him all 12 unique treasure items. This exclusive stock includes fully upgraded weapon parts and the exclusive "Treasure Hunter" accessory (increases item drop rate). Treasure items are found exclusively in hidden areas and off-path rooms.

EXPLORATION TIPS & TRICKS

AUDIO CUES

Listen for Hidden Secrets

The game uses distinct audio signals for nearby secrets: a faint humming near breakable walls, a whisper near crawlspaces, and a chime for nearby collectibles. Play with headphones to catch these cues. On Standard difficulty, an accessibility option can display visual markers for audio cues.

BACKTRACKING

Revisit Areas with New Key Items

Many early-game rooms have locked containers requiring key items found in later chapters. The Attic in Chapter I requires the City Seal from Chapter IX. The Hospital Basement safe requires a key from Chapter VII. Use Chapter Select post-game to systematically clean up every locked container.

MAP COMPLETION

Track Your Completion Percentage

The pause screen displays an area completion percentage. Rooms show as "incomplete" with a faded outline until you've entered and revealed all map details. For 100% map completion (required for the Cartographer trophy), you must physically enter every room in every chapter, including optional boss arenas and crawlspaces.

RESOURCE FARMING

Farming Routes Using Shortcuts

Once shortcuts are unlocked, efficient farming routes emerge. The Estate Garden-to-Cellar loop (Chapters I-II-III) is the best early-game route: respawn herbs, collect Merchant treasure, repeat. The Forgotten City catacomb loop is the best late-game route for Lei farming. Typewriter fast travel enables unlimited repetitions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many explorable areas are in Resident Evil Requiem?

Over 40 distinct explorable areas across 12 chapters. The exact count depends on whether you count sub-rooms within larger zones. The Forgotten City alone contains 15+ connected rooms and corridors.

Does RE Requiem have fast travel?

Yes. Typewriters serve as fast travel points. Once activated, you can warp between any unlocked typewriter from the pause map screen. There are 9 typewriters total (including bonus placements in Chapters IX and XI).

Are there hidden shortcuts?

14 unlockable shortcuts exist — padlock doors, breakable walls, ladder descents, and elevator activations. Unlocking all is required for the Pathfinder trophy. They persist across all future playthroughs.

What is the largest area in RE Requiem?

The Forgotten City in Chapter IX is the largest, with over 15 interconnected rooms across three vertical layers: surface streets, underground catacombs, and the optional Temple of Echoes.

Can you revisit previous areas?

Yes. Fast travel lets you return to any previously visited area at any time. Post-game Chapter Select provides full access. The only exception: Dimensional Rift areas (Chapter X) become permanently inaccessible after the chapter ends.

Do enemies respawn when you fast travel?

Most enemies do not respawn after being killed. However, certain high-value enemies (Lycans in gardens, Hospital Stalkers) respawn each time you fast travel to their area. This is intentional for resource farming — respawned enemies drop Lei and occasionally ammo.
