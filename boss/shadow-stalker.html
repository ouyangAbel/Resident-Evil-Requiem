STEALTH MINI-BOSS

Shadow Stalker

Chapter VIII: The Inverted Hall • DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆ • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS

The Shadow Stalker is a stealth-type mini-boss encountered in Chapter VIII: The Inverted Hall. Unlike the game's other bosses that reward aggressive play, the Shadow Stalker demands patience, positioning, and environmental awareness. This fight is as much a test of observation as it is of combat skill.

ARENA OVERVIEW

  • The Inverted Main Hall is a large rectangular room with the ceiling collapsed inward, creating an uneven floor with debris piles. The room has six pillars arranged in two rows, which the Shadow Stalker uses for cover during its invisibility phases.
  • The room is dimly lit, with the only light source coming from a flickering emergency light at the far end. The darkness is intentional — it forces you to rely on the faint red glow cue.
  • The floor is a checkerboard of dark and light tiles. The red glow is easier to see against dark tiles, which is a deliberate design choice by the developers.

PHASE 1: VISIBLE AND INVISIBLE CYCLING (100%-40% HP)

  • The Shadow Stalker begins the fight visible, crouched near the center of the room. It will immediately phase to invisible and reposition — do not waste ammo on the opening animation.
  • The boss alternates between visible (attacking) and invisible (repositioning) states every 8–10 seconds. This cycle is consistent throughout Phase 1.
  • When invisible, track it by the faint red glow that emanates from its core. The glow is most visible against dark floor tiles — look down at ground level rather than scanning at eye height.
  • When it materializes, it attacks immediately. The attack pattern is a three-claw swipe combo with a brief pause between the second and third swipe. Block or dodge the first two swipes, then punish with two close-range shotgun blasts.
  • Each materialization window lasts about 4 seconds. Two shotgun blasts are enough to force it to retreat. Do not chase — let it phase out and reposition.
  • Conserve ammo during Phase 1. The fight requires roughly 12 shotgun shells total on Standard difficulty. Do not use the handgun — it deals insufficient damage per materialization window.
  • The Shadow Stalker has a special attack where it phases to invisible, then materializes directly behind you for a backstab. The telegraph is a distinct audio cue — a whispering sound. Immediately turn around and fire.

PHASE 2: AGGRESSIVE REPOSITIONING (40%-0% HP)

  • At 40% HP, the Shadow Stalker becomes more aggressive. Its visible windows shrink to roughly 3 seconds, and it teleports more frequently.
  • The backstab attack becomes the primary threat in Phase 2. It now teleports behind you immediately after phasing out, with no audio cue. Keep your back to a wall at all times to prevent flanking.
  • The pillars in the room are your best friend in Phase 2. Position yourself with a pillar at your back, reducing the Stalker's flanking angles.
  • When the Stalker materializes within 2 meters, it uses a grab attack instead of the claw combo. The grab telegraph is both arms reaching forward. Dodge to either side and fire during the recovery.
  • Continue the same cycle: wait for materialization, dodge the initial attack, two shotgun blasts, reposition. Do not deviate from this pattern.
  • Below 10% HP, the Stalker stops phasing out entirely and becomes fully visible, desperately attacking. This is the time to unload everything. Switch to the handgun if you run out of shells.

RECOMMENDED LOADOUT

  • W-870 Shotgun (10+ shells) — Primary weapon, two blasts per materialization
  • Handgun (backup) — Finishing weapon for the final desperation phase
  • 1 Herb Combo — Healing for unavoidable claw damage

COMMON MISTAKES

  • Scanning at eye height for the red glow. Look down at the floor — it is much easier to track.
  • Wasting shotgun ammo on invisible phases. Only fire when it materializes.
  • Standing in open areas during Phase 2. Always have a wall or pillar at your back.
  • Chasing the Stalker when it phases out. Let it come to you.
  • Using the rifle or magnum. The shotgun's spread makes it ideal for the short materialization windows.
