HARDEST BOSS IN THE GAME

The Amalgam

Chapter XI: The Final Descent • DIFFICULTY: ★★★★★ • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS

The Amalgam in Chapter XI is universally regarded as the hardest boss in Resident Evil Requiem. This horrific fusion of every major enemy type from the game cycles through three distinct forms and then combines all their attack patterns in a brutal Final Form. The fight demands proficiency with every weapon in your arsenal, perfect resource management, and the ability to adapt strategies on the fly. Chapter XI has the tightest resource economy in the game — you cannot afford to waste a single round.

PREPARATION CHECKLIST

  • Enter Chapter XI with maximum supplies. Backtrack if necessary to find hidden ammo caches.
  • The Semi-Auto Rifle should be fully upgraded if possible. The damage difference between upgraded and stock is significant during the Lycan Form.
  • Craft at least 3 herb combos and purchase 2 First Aid Meds. The Amalgam fight can last 15–20 minutes on your first attempt.
  • Save all Broken Butterfly Magnum rounds for the Final Form (25%–0% HP). The magnum deals roughly 1,800 damage per shot in the final phase.
  • Hidden ammo caches exist behind destructible crates in the eastern corridor of Chapter XI. Do not skip these.

ARENA OVERVIEW

  • The Central Chamber is a three-tiered arena. The boss occupies the middle tier, with the upper gallery providing a safe sniping position during the Lycan Form.
  • Four stone pillars in the arena provide cover. These are essential for the Abomination Form phase — do not let the boss destroy all of them.
  • Explosive canisters spawn at the arena edges during the Final Form. They respawn every 30 seconds.
  • The upper gallery can be accessed via two staircases. The Shadow Form's red glow is easier to spot from above.

LYCAN FORM (100%-70% HP)

  • The fight begins with the Amalgam in its Lycan Form. This is the fastest form, with lunging claw attacks and rapid movement.
  • Use the upper gallery for this phase. The Semi-Auto Rifle at range while backpedaling is the safest approach.
  • The Lycan Form's lunge attack covers roughly 8 meters. Dodge to the side — not backward — as the tracking is forward-biased.
  • Three to four rifle headshots stagger the Lycan Form for roughly 3 seconds. Use this window to fire additional shots.
  • This is the easiest form of the fight. Maintain distance, fire precision shots, and conserve ammo. You need roughly 15 rifle rounds to push through this phase.
  • The Lycan Form cannot reach the upper gallery. Use this to your advantage for safe damage.

ABOMINATION FORM (70%-45% HP)

  • At 70% HP, the Amalgam shifts to its Abomination Form. The body expands, tentacles sprout from its torso, and movement slows.
  • This form has devastating tentacle attacks. A single tentacle sweep covers nearly 180 degrees and reaches roughly 5 meters.
  • Use the stone pillars for cover. When a tentacle wraps around a pillar, it gets stuck for 3 seconds. This is your primary damage window.
  • During the stuck animation, switch to the W-870 Shotgun and fire 2–3 close-range shots at the tentacle base. Each shot deals roughly 500 damage.
  • The Abomination Form also has a grab attack where a tentacle extends and pulls you toward the boss. The telegraph is a high-pitched shriek. Dodge sideways.
  • Do not fight in the open during this phase. The tentacle sweep can hit you from unexpected angles when you are not behind cover.
  • Phase transition requires roughly 8–10 shotgun shells worth of damage.

SHADOW FORM (45%-25% HP)

  • At 45% HP, the Amalgam shifts to its Shadow Form. It shrinks, becomes partially invisible, and moves rapidly around the arena.
  • Like the Shadow Stalker in Chapter VIII, this form alternates between visible and invisible states. However, the cycle is faster and more aggressive.
  • Fire the shotgun when it materializes. Two shots stagger it, four shots force it to phase out. The stagger window lasts roughly 2 seconds.
  • Track the red glow during invisibility. The glow is more active in this fight than the Chapter VIII encounter — it pulses faster.
  • Position yourself near explosive canisters if any remain. The canisters deal approximately 8% HP damage when detonated near the Shadow Form.
  • Do not fire at empty space. Every wasted shell matters in the resource economy of this fight.
  • Phase transition requires roughly 8–10 shotgun shells of damage.

FINAL FORM (25%-0% HP): ALL THREE COMBINED

  • At 25% HP, the Amalgam roars and enters its Final Form. All three body types merge into a single chaotic entity that cycles through all attack patterns every 15–20 seconds.
  • Explosive canisters now respawn every 30 seconds. These are your PRIMARY damage source for this phase.
  • Lure the Amalgam near a canister and detonate it. Each canister explosion deals roughly 8% of its total HP — roughly 2,240 damage.
  • During Lycan-pattern windows (visible red eyes), maintain distance and use the rifle. You have roughly 5 seconds before it switches.
  • During Abomination-pattern windows (tentacles extending), get behind pillars and fire shotgun when tentacles get stuck.
  • During Shadow-pattern windows (fading visibility), fire shotgun when it materializes to force quick phase-out.
  • Use Broken Butterfly Magnum rounds during the Lycan-pattern window for maximum burst damage. Each magnum shot deals roughly 1,800 damage.
  • This phase is a marathon. Focus on canister damage and supplement with weapon fire during safe windows. Do not get greedy.
  • Below 5% HP, a QTE prompt appears. Successfully completing it triggers a cinematic finish.

RECOMMENDED LOADOUT

  • Semi-Auto Rifle (fully upgraded, 30+ rounds) — Lycan Form and Final Form ranged damage
  • W-870 Shotgun (15+ shells) — Abomination Form and Shadow Form stagger windows
  • Broken Butterfly Magnum (4+ rounds) — Final Form burst damage only
  • Handgun (20+ rounds backup) — Supplementary and last resort
  • 3 Herb Combos — The fight is a resource war
  • 2 First Aid Meds — Emergency full heals

CRITICAL TIPS

  • The canister respawn timer (30 seconds) is consistent. Count in your head to know when new canisters appear.
  • The upper gallery is unsafe during Final Form — the Amalgam can reach it with tentacle attacks.
  • If you enter with less than 30 rifle rounds or 15 shotgun shells, consider backtracking for more supplies.
  • The Amalgam is vulnerable to the same elemental damage types as the base enemies: fire damage deals 1.5x to Abomination Form, shock damage deals 1.5x to Shadow Form.
