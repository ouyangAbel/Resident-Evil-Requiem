FIRST BOSS ENCOUNTER

The Bloated Lycan

Chapter III: The Forest Depths • DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆ • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS

The Bloated Lycan serves as your first boss encounter in Resident Evil Requiem, occurring near the end of Chapter III: The Forest Depths. This grotesque, oversized Lycan has been corrupted by the T-Virus, resulting in a bloated, tumorous form that belies surprising speed. While it is the easiest boss in the game, new players often struggle with ammo management and positioning during this fight.

PREPARATION

  • Before triggering the boss fight, thoroughly explore the underground tunnel area. There are three ammo caches hidden behind destructible crates in the eastern corridor.
  • Stockpile at least 15 handgun rounds and 6 shotgun shells before the encounter. The merchant in the safe room before the tunnel sells handgun ammo at 800 Lei per box.
  • Craft a Green+Red Herb combo from herbs found in the tunnel. The Bloated Lycan's attacks inflict moderate damage that accumulates quickly if you get cornered.

PHASE 1: RANGED EXCHANGE (100%-50% HP)

  • The fight begins with the Lycan emerging from a pile of corpses at the far end of the tunnel. It immediately charges — do not panic. Sidestep and create distance to roughly 5–8 meters.
  • At medium range, the Lycan uses two main attacks: a claw swipe (close range, quick recovery) and acid vomit (medium range, 2-second telegraph, longer recovery).
  • The acid vomit is your primary damage window. When the Lycan raises both arms and its chest bulges, it is about to vomit acid. This telegraph lasts about 1.5 seconds. Dodge sideways — not backward — as the vomit covers a cone in front of it.
  • After the vomit, the Lycan recovers for roughly 2 seconds. During this window, fire 3–4 precise headshots with the SG-09 R Handgun. Each headshot deals ~150 damage on Standard difficulty.
  • The tunnel contains two explosive canisters near the west wall. Kite the Lycan close to a canister and shoot it. One canister explosion deals roughly 30% of the Lycan's total HP. Two canisters alone can nearly end Phase 1.
  • Do not waste shotgun shells during Phase 1. The handgun is more efficient at medium range, and you will need the shotgun for Phase 2 stagger windows.

PHASE 2: AGGRESSIVE LUNGE (50%-0% HP)

  • At 50% HP, the Lycan roars and its eyes glow red. It gains a new charging lunge attack and all existing attacks speed up by roughly 30%.
  • The lunge telegraph is a crouch-and-growl animation that lasts about 1 second. The lunge covers roughly 10 meters. Dodge to either side — backward dodge often fails due to the lunge's forward tracking.
  • After a missed lunge, the Lycan is stuck in the floor for roughly 2.5 seconds. Switch to the W-870 Shotgun and fire 2–3 point-blank blasts. Each shotgun blast at close range staggers the Lycan slightly, buying you extra time.
  • If you saved both canisters, kite the Lycan to the second canister and detonate it. Combined with shotgun damage from lunge punishes, this should bring the Lycan below 20% HP.
  • Below 10% HP, the Lycan enters a brief rage state with no cooldown between attacks. Do not engage directly. Run loops around the tunnel pillars and fire handgun shots when safe. The rage lasts about 30 seconds, after which the Lycan slows down and becomes vulnerable to the final few shots.
  • On Standard difficulty, the fight requires approximately 20 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun blasts for the finishing sequence.

RECOMMENDED LOADOUT

  • SG-09 R Handgun (15+ rounds) — Primary weapon for Phase 1 precision shots
  • W-870 Shotgun (6+ shells) — Phase 2 stagger punishes after lunge misses
  • 1 Green+Red Herb combo — Healing for accumulated damage
  • Optional: 1 Flash Grenade — Cancels rage state if available

COMMON MISTAKES

  • Wasting shotgun shells during Phase 1. The handgun is perfectly adequate for the opening phase.
  • Dodging backward from the acid vomit. Sideways dodge is consistently successful; backward dodge often clips you with the splash radius.
  • Failing to use the explosive canisters. Two canister explosions deal over 60% of the Lycan's total HP — this is free damage.
  • Getting cornered against tunnel walls. Keep the open center of the tunnel at your back.
  • Using healing items during the rage state. Wait for the rage to subside, then heal in safety.

POST-FIGHT

  • After defeating the Bloated Lycan, search the corpse pile where it emerged. There is a hidden Luggage Tag key item that unlocks a storage box in Chapter V.
  • Proceed through the newly opened gate to reach the Chapter III safe room and save point. The merchant here sells the first weapon upgrade kit.
