FIRST MAJOR BOSS

The Abomination

Chapter VI: The Labyrinth Below • DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆ • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS

The Abomination in Chapter VI is the game's first major skill check. This grotesque fusion of multiple test subjects awaits you in the underground laboratory, and its three-phase fight demands weapon switching, resource management, and pattern recognition. Players who relied solely on the handgun through the early game will find this fight significantly more challenging.

ARENA OVERVIEW

  • The boss arena is a large circular laboratory with four stone pillars forming a square in the center. The pillars provide cover from the Abomination's shockwave attack but can be destroyed after taking enough damage.
  • Four explosive canisters are positioned at the cardinal points of the arena. These do not respawn but can be repositioned by shooting the floor near them (the explosion knocks them a short distance).
  • The floor has drainage grates that glow red during certain attacks, indicating safe zones. During Phase 3, some grates electrify — avoid standing on them.

PHASE 1: WEAK POINTS (100%-65% HP)

  • The Abomination spawns at the center of the lab, suspended from the ceiling by fleshy tendrils. It drops to the floor and immediately attacks. There is no pre-fight dialogue or item window.
  • Phase 1 features two glowing orange weak points on the Abomination's torso, one on each side. These are the only vulnerable spots during this phase.
  • Use the W-870 Shotgun at close-medium range. Each weak point absorbs roughly 3–4 shotgun blasts before bursting. When a weak point bursts, the boss staggers briefly.
  • When both weak points are destroyed simultaneously (or within roughly 3 seconds of each other), the Abomination enters a 5-second stagger state. During this window, switch to any weapon and unload on the exposed core — the Semi-Auto Rifle's precision shots deal the most damage here.
  • The boss attacks with wide arm sweeps during Phase 1. The sweep has a 180-degree arc and covers roughly 3 meters. Back up when you see the arms start to swing.
  • Destroying the first weak point triggers a minor attack speed increase. Destroying the second triggers the transition to Phase 2.

PHASE 2: EXTRA ARMS AND SHOCKWAVE (65%-30% HP)

  • The Abomination roars, tears additional arm-like appendages from its torso, and begins a new attack pattern. The weak points from Phase 1 close permanently.
  • A glowing orange core becomes visible in the center of its chest. This core is the primary target for Phase 2.
  • The new attacks include a ground slam that generates a radial shockwave. The slam telegraph is the boss raising both sets of arms and bringing them down simultaneously. You have roughly 1.5 seconds to react.
  • Dodge sideways to avoid the shockwave. Behind a pillar also works, but the pillar takes damage and can eventually break.
  • After the shockwave, the Abomination is immobile for 3 seconds. During this window, switch to the Semi-Auto Rifle and fire 2–3 precision shots at the exposed core. Each shot deals roughly 400 damage on Standard.
  • The boss also gains a tentacle grab attack during Phase 2. A single tentacle extends toward you — it has a noticeable wind-up and travels slowly. Simply strafe left or right to avoid it.
  • Phase 2 is about patience. Do not rush damage — wait for shockwave windows and punish consistently.

PHASE 3: ENRAGE (30%-0% HP)

  • At 30% HP, the Abomination's movement speed increases significantly and it gains a roar that briefly stuns you if you are within 3 meters.
  • Explosive canisters now respawn at the cardinal points of the arena every 45 seconds. Use these as your primary damage source.
  • Lure the Abomination near a canister and shoot it. One canister explosion deals roughly 15% of its remaining HP. Two canisters plus sustained rifle fire typically ends the fight.
  • The boss also gains a rush attack — it charges across the arena, damaging anything in its path. The telegraph is a loud shriek and the boss lowering its body. Dodge to the side.
  • Below 10% HP, the boss becomes enraged with no cooldown between attacks. Use saved shotgun shells for emergency staggers and finish with rifle fire.

RECOMMENDED LOADOUT

  • W-870 Shotgun (12+ shells) — Primary for Phase 1 weak points, emergency stagger
  • Semi-Auto Rifle (15+ rounds) — Phase 2–3 precision core shots
  • Handgun (20+ rounds backup) — Supplementary damage when repositioning
  • 2 Herb Combos — The Abomination hits hard in Phase 2–3
  • 1 First Aid Med — Emergency full heal

COMMON MISTAKES

  • Standing still during Phase 2 shockwaves. The shockwave covers the entire arena floor.
  • Wasting the 5-second dual weak point stagger on handgun fire instead of switching to the rifle.
  • Destroying all four pillars early. Save at least two for Phase 2–3 cover.
  • Forgetting that canisters respawn in Phase 3. Use them liberally.
  • Healing during the roar stun. Wait until the stun ends to heal safely.
