OPTIONAL SUPERBOSS

The Forgotten King

Chapter IX: City of Echoes • DIFFICULTY: ★★★★☆ • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS

The Forgotten King is the game's optional superboss, hidden in the throne room of the forgotten city beneath Rookwood Estate. Defeating this towering armored figure rewards you with the Broken Butterfly Magnum — widely considered the most powerful handgun in the game. While entirely optional, the Magnum makes the Amalgam and Final Boss significantly more manageable. This boss is a serious resource investment, so weigh your current supplies before committing.

HOW TO ACCESS

  • In Chapter IX: City of Echoes, after crossing the collapsed bridge, you will find a side passage to the left of the main path. This passage is partially hidden behind rubble — look for a faint glow emanating from a crack in the wall.
  • The passage leads to a set of descending stairs that spiral down to the throne room entrance. There is a merchant checkpoint before the boss door where you can stock up.
  • The merchant sells the following before the fight: Semi-Auto Rifle ammo (1,200 Lei per box), W-870 Shotgun shells (800 Lei per box), 1 First Aid Med (3,000 Lei).
  • There is no save point between the merchant and the boss door. If you die, you respawn at the last save point in Chapter IX main area.

ARENA OVERVIEW

  • The throne room is a massive circular chamber with a high vaulted ceiling. Four stone braziers burn with blue flame at the cardinal points, providing illumination.
  • The floor is cracked marble with patches of unstable ground that shift beneath your feet. Moving on unstable ground slows you by roughly 20%.
  • There are no explosive canisters in this arena. You must defeat the Forgotten King through weapon damage alone.
  • The throne itself is destructible. Destroying it reveals a small alcove containing 5 Broken Butterfly Magnum rounds — a significant find.

PHASE 1: HALBERD SWEEPS (100%-60% HP)

  • The Forgotten King rises from his throne and immediately attacks with wide horizontal halberd sweeps. The sweep covers roughly a 270-degree arc in front of him and reaches about 4 meters.
  • Your primary strategy in Phase 1 is to stay behind him. The King's back is unarmored — a glowing red weak point is visible between his shoulder blades at all times.
  • His turning speed is deliberately slow. When you see him begin to turn, circle around to stay behind him. This sounds simple but requires practice to execute consistently.
  • The halberd attacks include: horizontal sweep (medium speed, wide arc), overhead slam (slow, long recovery — best punish window), and quick thrust (fast, short range, little recovery).
  • The overhead slam is your best damage window. The King raises the halberd with both hands for roughly 2 seconds, then slams it down. Dodge behind him during the wind-up and fire 2–3 Semi-Auto Rifle shots at the weak point.
  • Each weak point shot deals roughly 300 damage on Standard difficulty. Phase 1 requires roughly 15–18 precision shots to progress.
  • Do not use the shotgun during Phase 1 at close range. The King's sweep can hit you during the shotgun's slow reload animation.

PHASE 2: SHOCKWAVE SLAM (60%-25% HP)

  • At 60% HP, the King plants his halberd into the ground and roars. The throne room shakes and Phase 2 begins with a radial shockwave.
  • The shockwave attack telegraph is unmistakable: the King drives the halberd's butt into the ground with both hands. You have roughly 1.5 seconds to react.
  • Back away to mid-range (roughly 8–10 meters) when you see the plant animation. The shockwave covers the entire arena floor radially.
  • There is a safe zone directly behind the King at close range (within 2 meters). If you cannot escape to mid-range, dash behind him to avoid the shockwave.
  • After the shockwave, the King's recovery animation lasts roughly 4 seconds. During this window, switch to the Semi-Auto Rifle and fire 3–4 shots at the weak point.
  • The King gains two new attacks in Phase 2: a shield bash (quick, moderate damage, short stun) and a ground pound that creates a localized tremor around him.
  • The shield bash is the only attack that can hit behind him. It has a quick telegraph — he pulls his shield arm back — and covers a 180-degree arc behind him. Stay close to his back but offset slightly to one side.

PHASE 3: ENRAGE (25%-0% HP)

  • At 25% HP, the King's armor cracks and his movements become significantly faster. The blue braziers in the room flare bright white.
  • All existing attacks speed up by roughly 40%. The overhead slam now has a shorter telegraph (roughly 1 second instead of 2).
  • The King also gains a chained swing combo: three horizontal sweeps followed by an overhead slam. This combo has no recovery between the sweeps — only the final slam leaves an opening.
  • Wait for the overhead slam at the end of the combo. Dodge behind him as usual and fire 2–3 shots.
  • If you have been saving magnum ammo, now is the time to use it. The Broken Butterfly Magnum deals roughly 1,500 damage per shot to the weak point. Four to five magnum shots can end Phase 3 quickly.
  • At 5% HP, the King collapses to one knee for roughly 10 seconds. During this window, any damage will finish him. Switch to the handgun to save ammo if needed.

REWARDS

  • Broken Butterfly Magnum — One of the strongest weapons in the game. High damage per shot but slow fire rate and rare ammo.
  • Forgotten Crest key item — Required for unlocking the secret ending (along with all other Lore Fragments).
  • King's Halberd key item — Sold to the merchant in Chapter X for 10,000 Lei.
  • 5 Magnum Rounds — Found in the alcove behind the destructible throne.
  • Approximately 12,000 Lei from treasure drops scattered around the throne room.

RECOMMENDED LOADOUT

  • Semi-Auto Rifle (20+ rounds) — Primary weapon for consistent Phase 1–2 damage
  • W-870 Shotgun (8+ shells) — Emergency stagger if cornered
  • Broken Butterfly Magnum (if available from NG+) — Phase 3 burst damage
  • 2 Herb Combos — The King hits hard even on Standard
  • 1 First Aid Med — Emergency heal for Phase 3

STRATEGIC NOTE

  • Defeat the Forgotten King BEFORE proceeding to Chapter X. This allows the merchant to upgrade the Broken Butterfly Magnum during Chapter X, significantly increasing its damage output for the Amalgam and Final Boss encounters.
  • If your resources are too low, it is acceptable to skip the Forgotten King on your first playthrough and return in New Game Plus. The King's HP scales with difficulty, and on NG+ with upgraded weapons, this fight is substantially easier.
