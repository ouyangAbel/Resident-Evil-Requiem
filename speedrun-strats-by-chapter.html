Speedrunning Categories: Which One Is Right for You?

Before diving into routes, it is important to understand the three main speedrunning categories for RE Requiem. Each category appeals to different types of runners and demands different skill sets.

Any% (No Major Glitches) — Complete the game as fast as possible using intended mechanics. Skips rely on clever use of movement, trigger manipulation, and weapon swaps. This is the most popular category and the primary focus of this guide. Typical time: 1:45–2:15.
100% (All Collectibles) — Collect every item, document, and treasure while completing the game. Routes are optimized to minimize backtracking and maximize collection efficiency. Typical time: 3:15–3:45.
Glitchless — A stricter variant of Any% that forbids certain advanced techniques like trigger clipping and quicksave buffering. This category rewards pure movement mastery and routing efficiency. Typical time: 2:00–2:30.

Universal Speedrun Techniques

Every RE Requiem speedrun relies on a set of core techniques that apply across all chapters. Master these fundamentals before attempting chapter-specific skips.

Slide Canceling

Cancel the slide animation by sprinting immediately after initiating a slide. This maintains momentum and shaves seconds off every traversal section. The timing window is generous — roughly 8 frames — making it one of the most accessible techniques for new runners.

Trigger Skipping

Manipulate enemy activation triggers by positioning at specific angles. Many scripted encounters can be bypassed entirely by hugging walls or using precise movement. Each trigger has a specific activation zone; learning these zones is essential for optimal routing.

Quick-Swap Buffering

Swap weapons during cutscenes or door transitions to skip equip animations. This is essential for maintaining pace during combat-heavy chapters and is used in nearly every boss fight for maximum DPS uptime.

Optimal Pathfinding

Learn the exact movement lines through each room. Cutting corners tightly and avoiding unnecessary geometry interaction saves enormous amounts of time over a full run. Top runners practice each room individually to find the fastest path.

Chapter-by-Chapter Speedrun Breakdown

This section details the optimal route, key skips, and time-saving strategies for every chapter in the game. Times listed are for Any% category on Standard difficulty.

Chapter 1: The Awakening — Target: 8:30

The opening chapter serves as both tutorial and route optimization playground. Top runners skip the optional exploration of the east wing entirely, sprinting directly through the main hall. The Bloated Lycan encounter can be bypassed by sliding under the debris pile on the left side of the corridor — a skip that saves approximately 45 seconds.

Pro Tip: Ignore the herb pickup in the bathroom. It is never needed in an Any% run, and grabbing it costs 3 seconds of movement time. The small health gain is irrelevant since you should not be taking damage.

Optimal route: Spawn → Main Hall (sprint) → Debris Slide Skip → Dining Room → Trigger Cutscene → Chapter Complete.

Chapter 2: Echoes of the Estate — Target: 12:45

This sprawling chapter contains the first major skip opportunity. In the library section, instead of solving the bookshelf puzzle, runners use a precise jump from the second-floor balcony to the chandelier, dropping into the locked study below. This skip eliminates roughly 90 seconds of puzzle-solving.

The garden maze can be navigated 30% faster by following the left-wall rule consistently. Do not bother with the optional treasure rooms — they add minimal time but cost significant movement efficiency.

Pro Tip: The chandelier jump requires a specific running start from the bookshelf side of the balcony. Practice the jump 10-15 times before attempting it in a full run. The timing is consistent once you find the right angle.

Chapter 3: Below the Surface — Target: 10:15

The underground sections demand efficient combat routing. The three Molded encounters in the sewer section can be skipped entirely by using a well-timed sprint-and-slide pattern through the drainage tunnel. The Abomination fight is unavoidable but can be ended in under 20 seconds with a specific shotgun blast pattern to its weak point during its charge animation.

Chapter 4: The Crimson Wing — Target: 14:20

Grace Hospital introduces vertical routing challenges. Top runners take the elevator shaft route instead of the stairs, using a ledge-grab technique to bypass two entire floors. The hallway of mannequins is a major time sink — the skip here involves activating the fire alarm to open the emergency exit early, cutting 60 seconds off the standard route.

Key Skip: The fire alarm skip requires you to interact with the alarm panel before the scripted event triggers. If you are too late, the alarm will not work and you will have to take the long route. Position yourself near the panel as soon as you enter the hallway.

Chapter 5: Betrayal in the Dark — Target: 9:50

Stealth sections are notoriously difficult to optimize. The key strategy here is memorizing enemy patrol patterns and using the darkness mechanic to your advantage. The Shadow Stalker first encounter can be skipped by interacting with the fuse box in a specific sequence, triggering a door unlock that bypasses the entire stealth section.

Chapter 6: The Living Laboratory — Target: 16:30

The lab chapter is where world records are won or lost. The critical skip involves the specimen containment area — by using a grenade to destroy the control panel at a precise angle, you can force-open all containment doors simultaneously, allowing a straight sprint through what would otherwise be a puzzle-heavy section. Time save: approximately 2 minutes.

Chapter 7: The Requiem Begins — Target: 7:15

This transitional chapter is short but packed with optimization opportunities. The rooftops section benefits heavily from slide-canceling, and the scripted fall sequence can be skipped by performing a specific ledge interaction that triggers the next cutscene early. Every runner should practice this skip extensively — it saves 30 seconds and is consistent once mastered.

Chapter 8: Dimensions Apart — Target: 18:45

The dimension-hopping mechanics introduce unique routing possibilities. The key skip here involves using the dimension-shift ability at a specific angle near the cliff edge, which clips you through a wall and skips an entire puzzle room. This is one of the more difficult skips in the game but saves a massive 75 seconds.

Pro Tip: The dimension-shift wall clip requires precise positioning. Stand on the cracked stone block near the edge, face 15 degrees left of the cliff face, and activate the shift. The timing window is tight but consistent with practice.

Chapter 9: The Forgotten City — Target: 22:10

The largest open area in the game demands route optimization on a macro scale. Top runners follow a strict collection path that picks up only the mandatory items while avoiding all optional encounters. The Forgotten King boss fight can be ended in 15 seconds by exploiting its grab animation — a risky but consistent strategy at high level.

Chapter 10: Convergence — Target: 13:25

The penultimate chapter combines everything you have learned. The multi-floor descent is optimized by using slide-canceling on every staircase and skipping every optional dialogue. The Amalgam boss fight has a damage glitch that top runners use to end the fight in under 30 seconds — a precise shot to its core during the vulnerability window.

Chapter 11: The Final Descent — Target: 11:50

The final gauntlet before the ending. The critical optimization here is the Developer Room skip — by taking a specific path through the lab, you can bypass the entire optional section while still triggering the story progression. Every second counts in this chapter, as it directly impacts your final time.

Chapter 12: Requiem — Target: 15:40

The final boss rush. Top runners use a specific weapon loadout (shotgun, handgun, and infinite knife) and follow a phase-by-phase strategy that minimizes downtime. The final phase can be skipped entirely by dealing enough damage during the second phase vulnerability window — a strategy that requires precise execution but saves 45 seconds.

Tools & Practice Methods

Successful speedrunning requires dedicated practice and the right tools. Here is what every RE Requiem speedrunner needs:

  • Chapter Select: Use chapter select to isolate difficult sections. Practice each skip individually before attempting full runs.
  • In-Game Timer: The in-game timer is the official timing method. Start timing on the "New Game" confirmation screen.
  • Auto-Splitter Mod: Available on the RE Requiem speedrun Discord. Automatically splits your run by chapter for detailed segment analysis.
  • Input Display: Overlay your inputs to identify inefficiencies in your movement and timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest route in Resident Evil Requiem?

The fastest Any% route uses the library chandelier jump skip in Chapter 2, the lab containment panel skip in Chapter 6, and the dimension-shift wall clip in Chapter 8. These three skips alone save over 4 minutes of gameplay. Combined with optimal boss strategies and movement tech, sub-1:50 is achievable with clean execution.

How long is a typical RE Requiem speedrun?

A typical Any% speedrun of RE Requiem takes between 1 hour 45 minutes and 2 hours 15 minutes depending on execution. The world record as of August 2026 is 1:47:32. A 100% run typically takes 3 hours 15 minutes to 3 hours 45 minutes.

What are the most important skips to learn first?

The most important skips to learn first are the Bloated Lycan debris slide in Chapter 1 (easy, saves 45 seconds), the library chandelier jump in Chapter 2 (medium, saves 90 seconds), and the lab containment panel skip in Chapter 6 (medium, saves 2 minutes). These three skips offer the best time investment to difficulty ratio.

Do you need all collectibles for a speedrun?

No. In the Any% category, you only need to reach the end of the game as fast as possible. Collectibles, optional bosses, and side missions are skipped entirely. Only in the 100% category are all collectibles required.

What difficulty is best for speedrunning RE Requiem?

Standard difficulty is best for speedrunning because enemies have less health and many encounters can be bypassed entirely. Hardcore and Professional difficulties increase enemy health and aggression, making skips more difficult and combat slower.
