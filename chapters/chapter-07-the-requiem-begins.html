CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter VII marks the beginning of Act III and a radical shift in setting and tone. The underground laboratory gives way to a decaying cathedral town that exists in a dimension overlapping with reality. The atmosphere transitions from scientific horror to supernatural dread as Leon finds himself in a world that should not exist.

This chapter introduces the Semi-Auto Rifle, which becomes your primary weapon for the precision combat encounters ahead. It also introduces Lore Fragments — collectibles required for unlocking the secret ending in Chapter XII.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

The Cathedral Town

  1. Emerging from the Laboratory: The elevator opens into what appears to be a cave system, but the walls are lined with cathedral architecture — stone arches and stained glass embedded in natural rock. Step forward to enter the town proper.
  2. Town Square (Save Room): A cobblestone square with a dried fountain at the center. The sky is a perpetual twilight purple. Search the fountain for Handgun Ammo (x8). The church bell tower is visible in the distance.
  3. Abandoned Shops: Three shopfronts line the square. The weapon merchant's shop contains the Semi-Auto Rifle on a display rack. The apothecary has Green Herb (x2) and a Blue Herb. The bookshop contains "History of the Cathedral Town" — essential lore context.
  4. Bell Tower: Climb the stairs on the east side of the square. The bell tower door requires the Cathedral Key, found in the town hall.

Town Hall

  1. Town Hall Entrance: The double doors at the north end of the square. Inside, a grand foyer with portraits of robed figures. Four Cultists guard the area — they wield ceremonial daggers and are faster than infected but have less HP (2 rifle shots).
  2. Mayor's Office: Upstairs, behind a locked door (key is on a Cultist leader in the banquet hall). Inside, search the desk for the Cathedral Key and "Mayor's Final Proclamation." The proclamation hints at the Stained Glass Puzzle solution.
  3. Banquet Hall: Large room with a long table. A Cultist leader and two followers attack. The leader takes 4 shotgun blasts. Defeat them for the Mayor's Office Key.

The Cathedral

  1. Cathedral Entrance (Save Room): Use the Cathedral Key on the main doors. The interior is vast, with towering pillars and a rose window at the far end. The atmosphere is oppressive, filled with incense smoke.
  2. Stained Glass Puzzle: The rose window has four empty panels (see solution below). Solving it opens the path to the altar.
  3. Altar: Three Altar Stones are required. Stone #1 is on the altar itself. Stone #2 is in a side chapel (search the confessional booth). Stone #3 is behind a false wall in the choir room (shoot the cracked section).
  4. Dimensional Rift: Place all three Altar Stones on the altar. A shimmering rift opens behind the altar — the path to Chapter VIII.

STAINED GLASS PUZZLE SOLUTION

The rose window has four panels: Angel, Chalice, Skull, and Cross. The clue is in the "Mayor's Final Proclamation": "The angel watches over the faithful, the chalice holds their hopes, the skull marks their end, and the cross leads them to salvation. From left to right, as the sun rises."

Solution: Arrange the panels from left to right: ANGEL → CHALICE → SKULL → CROSS. The panels click into place when correctly positioned. The altar path opens.

LORE FRAGMENTS & COLLECTIBLES

Chapter VII contains 2 of the 6 Lore Fragments needed for the secret ending:

  • Lore Fragment #1: In the bell tower, hidden behind a loose stone near the bell mechanism. Examine the stone wall near the rope.
  • Lore Fragment #2: In the cathedral choir room, inside a hollow hymn book on the music stand.

Note: Collect all 6 Lore Fragments across Chapters VII-XII to unlock the secret ending path. Missing even one locks you into the standard ending. See our All Endings Explained guide for details.
