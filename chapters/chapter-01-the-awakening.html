CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter I: The Awakening serves as the introductory chapter of Resident Evil Requiem. You begin as Leon S. Kennedy, arriving at the foreboding Rookwood Estate following a distress call from a fellow agent. This chapter establishes the core survival horror mechanics — exploration, resource management, puzzle-solving, and the first taste of combat against the infected estate staff.

The chapter is deliberately paced, allowing new players to acclimatize to the controls and environment before the horror escalates in later chapters. Veterans of the Resident Evil series will recognize familiar gameplay rhythms while encountering new twists unique to Requiem.

PREPARATION CHECKLIST

  • No prior preparation needed — this is the first chapter. Everything you need is provided through exploration.
  • Pay close attention to the tutorial prompts. They explain dodge mechanics, healing, and the inventory system.
  • Interact with every examine prompt. Several early files contain critical lore and safe codes used in later chapters.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Arrival at Rookwood Estate

The chapter opens with a cinematic of Leon driving through a thunderstorm toward the Rookwood Estate gates. After the cinematic, you gain control in the estate's front courtyard.

  1. Front Courtyard: Walk forward toward the main entrance. Examine the locked gate on your left — it requires a Crest Key later. Enter the main building through the large oak doors.
  2. Main Foyer (Save Room): The foyer is your first safe room. Locate the typewriter on the desk to the right and save your game. Search the side table drawer for Handgun Ammo (x8). Read the "Estate Welcome Letter" on the table — it contains background lore.
  3. East Corridor: Exit the foyer through the east door. The corridor is dimly lit. The first infected — a former estate staff member — shambles toward you from the far end. Use the tutorial prompts to aim and fire. Two headshots with the handgun dispatch standard infected.
  4. Kitchen: At the end of the east corridor, enter the kitchen. Search the pantry for a Green Herb. The knife block on the counter contains a Survival Knife (key item for cutting obstacles).
  5. Dining Room: Return to the foyer and enter the dining room through the west door. Four infected burst through the windows after you pick up the Dining Room Key from the sideboard. This is your first crowd encounter — use the table as a barrier and pick them off one by one.

Exploring the Ground Floor

  1. Library: Use the Dining Room Key on the locked door in the north wall of the dining room. The library contains the first major puzzle (see Puzzle Solutions below) and a hidden Handgun Stock attachment in the fireplace — essential for later accuracy upgrades.
  2. Study: After solving the library puzzle, proceed through the newly opened passage to the study. Search the desk for the Estate Map, which reveals all ground-floor rooms. Read the "Researcher's Journal" on the bookshelf — it hints at the underground laboratory.
  3. West Wing Hallway: Exit the study through the west door. A Feral Hound crashes through a window. These enemies are faster than standard infected but have lower HP. Two well-placed handgun shots or a single melee attack when they leap will dispatch them.
  4. Guest Bedroom: The first door on the left in the west wing. Search the wardrobe for Handgun Ammo (x10) and the nightstand for a Red Herb. Combine with Green Herb later for enhanced healing.

The First Encounter

  1. Grand Staircase: Return to the foyer and approach the grand staircase. A scripted event triggers — a chandelier crashes down, blocking the stairs. This forces you to find an alternate route through the cellar.
  2. Cellar Entrance: The cellar door is under the staircase, partially hidden behind a tapestry. Examine the tapestry to reveal the door. The cellar is dark — your flashlight activates automatically.
  3. Cellar Corridor: Three infected in the cellar. Use the narrow corridors to your advantage — funnel them into single-file for easy headshots. Search the wine racks for a Blue Herb (cures poison status).
  4. Boiler Room: At the end of the cellar corridor. Pull the boiler lever to restore power to the east wing. This unlocks the door at the top of the staircase that was previously blocked by electronic lock.
  5. Return to Foyer: The chandelier debris has been cleared (scripted timing). Ascend the grand staircase to the second floor. Chapter I ends with Leon entering the upper hallway and hearing a woman's scream from a nearby room.

PUZZLE SOLUTIONS

Library Book Puzzle

Four bookshelves in the library have books that can be pulled. The clue is in the "Researcher's Journal" found in the study: "The chronicles of earth, water, air, and flame — ordered as the seasons turn."

Solution: Pull books in this order: Green (Spring/Earth) → Blue (Summer/Water) → White (Autumn/Air) → Red (Winter/Flame). A secret passage opens behind the main desk.

PRO TIPS

PRO TIP 1

Conserve Ammo

Standard infected can be stunned with a single leg shot, then finished with a knife slash. This saves handgun ammo for emergencies. Practice this technique early — it will be essential on higher difficulties.

PRO TIP 2

Explore Everything

The Handgun Stock hidden in the library fireplace is missable. It reduces reticle bloom by 40%, making long-range shots significantly more accurate. Return to rooms after progressing — some contain new items after story triggers.

PRO TIP 3

Herb Management

Combine Green+Red for full heal (100% recovery). Green+Green restores 60%. Never use herbs raw unless absolutely necessary — the combined effect is significantly better.

Save point: After reaching the second floor, you will find a typewriter in the upper hallway study. Save before proceeding. For full chapter sequences, see our Complete Walkthrough.
