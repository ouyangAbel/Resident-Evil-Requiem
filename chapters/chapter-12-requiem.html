CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter XII is the conclusion of Resident Evil Requiem. The final boss is a multi-phase encounter that mirrors the entire game arc, escalating in intensity and requiring different weapon strategies for each phase. The ending you receive depends on your choices throughout the game and your collection of the six Lore Fragments.

This chapter has no puzzle elements — it is a pure combat finale. The atmosphere is apocalyptic, with the dimensional rift tearing reality apart around you. The soundtrack swells to a crescendo, combining motifs from every major boss theme into a single, epic track.

PRE-FIGHT CHECKLIST

Before entering the boss arena, verify you have all of the following:

  • Full health (use a Green+Red+Blue combo for maximum effect)
  • Maximum ammunition for every weapon type (at least 30 handgun rounds, 20 shotgun shells, 40 rifle rounds, 10 magnum rounds)
  • At least 3 First Aid Sprays (or equivalent healing items)
  • All weapons fully upgraded (visit the final merchant in the Requiem Key chamber if you missed upgrades)
  • 6 Lore Fragments collected (if aiming for the secret ending)
  • Grenades (at least 2, ideally 4)

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

The Final Corridor

  1. Requiem Gate: Use the Requiem Key on the massive door at the end of the central chamber. The door is made of fused organic matter and crystalline structures — it groans as it opens.
  2. Corridor of Echoes: A long corridor lined with frozen images from Leon's journey — brief glimpses of every major encounter. This is a narrative moment with no combat. Walk forward slowly and take in the atmosphere.
  3. The Arena: The corridor opens into a massive void — a circular platform floating in an endless purple-black space. Dimensional tears pulse on all sides. The Requiem Entity emerges from below the platform.

FINAL BOSS: THE REQUIEM ENTITY

See our dedicated Final Boss guide for the complete strategy. Summary of all phases below:

Phase 1: The Awakening (100%-75% HP)

  • The Entity manifests as a humanoid figure wreathed in purple-black energy. It attacks with energy blasts and teleporting slashes.
  • Strategy: Keep moving — never stop strafing. The energy blasts have a slight homing effect. Fire the Semi-Auto Rifle during the Entity's recovery frames after teleport slashes. Aim for the chest core.
  • Key tells: When the Entity raises both arms, it is about to fire a spread of energy blasts — dodge sideways. When it disappears, it is teleporting behind you — turn and dodge immediately.

Phase 2: The Laboratory (75%-50% HP)

  • The Entity grows larger and spawns tentacles from the platform edges. The tentacles sweep the arena floor in rotating patterns.
  • Strategy: Watch the tentacle patterns carefully — they rotate clockwise. Time your movements between sweeps. Use the Shotgun on the Entity's body when it is between attacks. Destroy tentacles with explosive canisters that appear at the platform corners.
  • Key tell: When the Entity roars and pulses, all tentacles retract for 5 seconds — this is your damage window. Unload all weapons during this period.

Phase 3: The Mirror (50%-25% HP)

  • The Entity splits into two copies — one visible, one invisible. Both attack simultaneously. The invisible copy leaves the same red glow trail as the Shadow Stalker.
  • Strategy: Focus on the visible copy first. Use shotgun blasts to stagger it. The invisible copy attacks every 8 seconds — dodge when you see the red glow. After defeating the visible copy (about 6 shotgun blasts), the invisible copy becomes visible and gains a damage boost for 10 seconds. Survive this enrage window, then finish it.

Phase 4: The Requiem (25%-0% HP)

  • The Entity merges both copies into its true form — a massive, winged abomination with three glowing weak points (head, chest, wings). It uses attacks from all previous phases simultaneously.
  • Strategy: This phase is a damage race. The Entity's attacks intensify over time. Use the Broken Butterfly Magnum — it deals the highest single-shot damage. Each Magnum shot to a weak point staggers the Entity for 1.5 seconds.
  • Rotate between the three weak points. If you focus on one, it becomes armored after 3 hits. Alternate: head → chest → wing → repeat.
  • When the Entity prepares its ultimate attack (20-second charge-up, visible by all three weak points glowing simultaneously), shoot all three weak points within 5 seconds to interrupt the attack. Failure means a one-hit-kill on Normal difficulty.

ENDING GUIDE

Three Possible Endings

The ending you receive depends on how many Lore Fragments you collected across Chapters VII-XII.

  • Standard Ending (0-2 Fragments): Leon defeats the Entity but is trapped in the collapsing dimension. The screen fades to white — ambiguous fate.
  • Extended Ending (3-5 Fragments): Leon escapes the dimension but the Entity's influence spreads to the real world. A post-credits scene hints at a sequel.
  • Secret Ending (6 Fragments): Using the lore gathered, Leon seals the dimensional rift permanently and exposes the conspiracy behind the outbreak. A final cutscene shows Leon walking away from the Rookwood Estate as dawn breaks. Full closure.
