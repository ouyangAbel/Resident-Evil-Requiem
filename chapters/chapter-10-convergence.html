CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter X marks the collapse of dimensional barriers. Both worlds — real and mirror — merge into a single nightmarish landscape. This chapter is a gauntlet of combat encounters with minimal exploration, designed to test your combat skills and resource management before the final act.

The centerpiece of the chapter is a 4-minute bridge defense sequence where you must protect an NPC ally. This encounter can be brutal on higher difficulties if you have not conserved enough shotgun ammo and grenades.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Merging Worlds

  1. Obelisk Passage: The central obelisk descends into the earth, revealing a passage lined with flickering torches. The walls phase between stone and ethereal blue energy — the dimensional barrier is collapsing.
  2. Arena Encounter #1 — The Collapsing Square: The first arena is a town square that shifts between the real city and its mirror version. Three waves of enemies: 3 Phantom Hounds, 4 City Sentinels, 2 Crimson Lycans. Use the pillars for cover and prioritize the Lycans.
  3. Arena Encounter #2 — The Inverted Cathedral: A cathedral interior that turns upside down halfway through the fight. When gravity flips, ceilings become floors. Enemies: 5 Shadow Wraiths, 3 Enraged Sentinels. The Semi-Auto Rifle is essential for the Shadow Wraiths.

NPC Ally Encounter

  1. NPC Ally Introduction: After the second arena, you encounter an NPC survivor — Agent Carter. She has important information about the Convergence Point. She fights alongside you but has limited HP and ammo.
  2. Arena Encounter #3 — The Bridge Approach: A narrow bridge path with enemies attacking from both ends. 2 Crimson Lycans from the front, 3 Phantom Hounds from behind. Keep Carter behind you and use shotgun blasts to stagger approaching enemies.

BRIDGE DEFENSE STRATEGY

CRITICAL ENCOUNTER

4-Minute Bridge Defense

The bridge defense is the hardest encounter in Chapter X. You and Carter must hold a position on a stone bridge while waves of enemies attack from both directions. Carter has a scripted position in the center — do not let her get surrounded.

Key Strategy:

  • Pre-fight preparation: Stockpile at least 20 shotgun shells, 40 rifle rounds, and 2 grenades before triggering this encounter. There is a merchant in the room before the bridge — buy everything you can.
  • Prioritize Crimson Lycans: They deal the most damage to Carter. As soon as a Crimson Lycan appears, focus all fire on it. Two shotgun blasts stagger it; three finish it.
  • Carter's HP: Carter has a visible health bar. She takes cover when her HP drops below 50%, reducing her combat contribution. If her HP reaches zero, the encounter fails and you restart from the checkpoint.
  • Wave breakdown: The 4-minute timer has 8 waves. Waves 1-3 are light (Phantom Hounds + 1 Lycan), Waves 4-6 are moderate (2 Lycans + Sentinels), Waves 7-8 are heavy (3 Lycans + 2 Enraged Sentinels).
  • Grenade usage: Use grenades on Wave 7 and Wave 8. One grenade clears an entire side of smaller enemies, letting you focus on the Lycans.

PREPARATION FOR CHAPTER XI

After the bridge: The Convergence Point is a massive swirling rift. Carter provides the final piece of exposition before the path to Chapter XI opens. Before proceeding, ensure you have full ammo and healing — Chapter XI: The Final Descent contains the Amalgam boss fight, the hardest encounter in the game.
