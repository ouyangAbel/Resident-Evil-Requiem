CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter V is the dramatic turning point of Resident Evil Requiem. An ally's betrayal shatters the narrative, and the gameplay shifts from methodical exploration to desperate escape. The chapter is relatively short but intense, featuring a timed escape sequence from a collapsing wine cellar that can catch unprepared players off guard.

Your earlier choices affect dialogue in the betrayal cutscene, though the outcome is fixed regardless of player decisions. The chapter permanently changes the game's tone and your relationship with every character encountered afterward.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Return to the Greenhouse

  1. Greenhouse Passage: Exit the greenhouse through the north door. The path leads to a hidden staircase behind a tapestry in the east wing. An NPC ally awaits at the top of the stairs — the betrayal cutscene triggers when you approach.
  2. Betrayal Cutscene: Your ally reveals their true allegiance. Earlier choices (whether you shared information or kept secrets in Chapters I-II) change the dialogue but not the outcome. The ally escapes through a hidden door, collapsing the passage behind them.
  3. Wine Cellar: The collapsing debris forces you downward into the estate's wine cellar. The room is filled with racked bottles and wooden barrels. An earthquake-like tremor shakes the entire structure — the cellar is collapsing.

Wine Cellar Escape

  1. Timed Escape: A 3-minute timer begins (Standard difficulty). Sprint through the wine cellar following the lit torches. Do not fight enemies — sprint past them.
  2. The Fork: At the fork in the path, take the LEFT path. The right path is a dead end containing a Gold Ingot treasure (2,500 Lei). Only take the right path if you have at least 90 seconds remaining.
  3. Collapsed Archway: A dead soldier is pinned under debris near the collapsed archway. Search the body for the Emergency Keycard.
  4. Elevator: Use the Emergency Keycard on the elevator panel at the end of the passage. The elevator descends to the underground laboratory. Chapter V ends as the elevator doors close.

TIMED ESCAPE STRATEGY

PRO TIP 1

Sprint, Don't Fight

The wine cellar enemies are infinite spawns — they will keep coming. Your goal is the elevator, not clearing the room. Sprint through and only dodge if an enemy is directly in your path.

PRO TIP 2

Follow the Torches

Lit torches mark the correct path. Unlit torch sconces indicate dead ends or rooms with minor loot. The Gold Ingot on the right fork is the only loot worth detouring for.

PRO TIP 3

Emergency Keycard Location

The Emergency Keycard is on a dead soldier near the collapsed archway — not on the elevator itself. Many players run straight to the elevator and panic when it requires a keycard they missed. Remember: the soldier is at the archway before the elevator.

KEY ITEMS

  • Emergency Keycard — Operates the laboratory elevator
  • Gold Ingot — Optional treasure (2,500 Lei, right fork dead end)
  • Storage Box Access — Use Luggage Tag from Chapter III if you found it
