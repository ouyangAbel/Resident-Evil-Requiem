CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter III takes the game underground. Beneath the Rookwood Estate lies a network of smuggling tunnels that predates the mansion itself. Resources are deliberately scarce here — the game forces you to rely on precise shots and knife finishers. This chapter culminates in the first mandatory boss fight against the Bloated Lycan, testing everything you have learned about combat mechanics.

The chapter is notable for its claustrophobic atmosphere and its shift from the relatively spacious estate interiors to tight, oppressive tunnel corridors. This is where Resident Evil Requiem reveals its true survival horror teeth.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Entering the Tunnels

  1. Front Gate: The chapter begins at the estate's front gate. Use the Crest Key (Silver+Green combined in Chapter II) to unlock it. Follow the path around the estate to the rear grounds.
  2. Garden Shed: A small structure near the back wall. Inside is a Green Herb and "Groundskeeper's Note" explaining the tunnel entrance is hidden behind the well.
  3. Well Descent: Interact with the well at the back of the garden. Leon lowers himself into the darkness. The well shaft leads to the tunnel system proper.

The Underground Tunnels

  1. Main Tunnel: The tunnels are marked with red painted arrows on the walls — follow these to navigate toward the exit. Ignore unmarked side passages initially, as most lead to dead ends with minor loot.
  2. Flooded Chamber: A large chamber filled with ankle-deep water. Three Infected Tunnelers emerge from the water. The water slows movement — use the raised stone platforms for better footing. Search the submerged crate for Handgun Ammo (x8).
  3. Water Valve Puzzle Room: The flooded chamber's exit is blocked by a water gate. The valve mechanism requires precise alignment (see Puzzle Solutions below).
  4. Drainage Tunnel: After draining the chamber, proceed through the newly exposed passage. Two Feral Hounds and an Infected Tunneler guard a cache containing Shotgun Shells (x4) and a Green Herb.

The Tunnel Exit

  1. Safe Room: A small alcove with a typewriter and item box. This is your last chance to prepare before the boss. The merchant (if you have sufficient Lei) sells Handgun Ammo (800 Lei) and a First Aid Spray (1,500 Lei).
  2. Boss Arena: The tunnel opens into a larger cavern. The Bloated Lycan emerges from a pile of corpses at the far end. Save your game before entering this area.

WATER VALVE PUZZLE SOLUTION

The Water Valve Puzzle has three valve wheels arranged in a triangle. The clue is carved into the stone above the mechanism: "The right hand turns left, the left hand turns right, the center stands true."

Solution: Turn the valves in this order:

  1. Left valve → turn RIGHT (clockwise) until it stops
  2. Right valve → turn LEFT (counter-clockwise) until it stops
  3. Center valve → turn it fully clockwise, then counter-clockwise halfway
The water level drops, revealing the exit passage.

BOSS: THE BLOATED LYCAN

See the full detailed boss strategy on our dedicated Bloated Lycan boss page. Below is the essential strategy summary:

Overview: This swollen, tumorous Lycan is your first boss. It has two phases and roughly 8,000 HP on Standard difficulty.

Phase 1 (100%-50% HP): The Lycan uses claw swipes and acid vomit. The acid vomit has a 2-second telegraph — dodge sideways and fire 3 precise headshots during its recovery. Two explosive canisters near the west wall deal ~30% HP each.

Phase 2 (50%-0% HP): Gains a charging lunge attack. Dodge sideways when it crouches and growls. Punish the missed lunge with 2 point-blank shotgun blasts. Below 10% HP, it enters a rage state — run loops around pillars until it slows.

Recommended Loadout: SG-09 R Handgun (15+ rounds), W-870 Shotgun (6+ shells), 1 Green+Red Herb combo, optional Flash Grenade.

POST-FIGHT REWARDS

  • Luggage Tag — Hidden in the corpse pile where the Lycan emerged. Opens a storage box in Chapter V.
  • Tunnel Exit Key — Automatically obtained. Opens the gate to the next area.
  • Access to Chapter IV — The tunnel leads to a forest path that eventually returns to the estate from a new direction.
