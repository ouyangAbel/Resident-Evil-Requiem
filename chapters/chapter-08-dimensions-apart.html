CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Chapter VIII transports Leon into a nightmarish mirror version of the estate from Chapters I-III. The layout is familiar yet inverted — rooms are mirrored, gravity behaves strangely in certain areas, and new enemy types phase in and out of visibility. This chapter deliberately disorients veteran players who have memorized the estate layout, creating genuine unease as familiar spaces become hostile and alien.

The chapter features a memorable mini-boss encounter with the Shadow Stalker and introduces the Inversion Puzzle, one of the game's most creative environmental challenges.

STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Entering the Mirror World

  1. Rift Arrival: Stepping through the dimensional rift, the world twists and reforms around you. You emerge in a version of the estate foyer, but everything is reversed — staircases are on opposite sides, doors lead to different rooms.
  2. Mirror Foyer (Save Room): The typewriter here has a faint blue glow — it works differently in this dimension. Searching the desk reveals "Estate Log — Mirror Version," a document that explains the inversion rules: "East is west, light is shadow, up is down."

Exploring the Inverted Estate

  1. Inverted Corridors: The east corridor from Chapter I now leads west. Rooms that were safe rooms are now danger zones. The kitchen contains a trap — the stove explodes when you approach it. Take the alternative route through the pantry.
  2. Shadow Wraiths: New enemy type introduced here. Shadow Wraiths phase between visible and invisible states. When invisible, they leave a faint shimmer. The Semi-Auto Rifle is effective — aim at the shimmer and fire. Two shots bring them down.
  3. Phantom Hounds: Spectral versions of Feral Hounds. They can phase through walls. Listen for their ethereal howling to anticipate attacks. Shotgun works best for the stagger effect.

Inverted Landmarks

  1. Inverted Library: The bookshelf puzzle from Chapter I is here but with reversed logic. Pull the books in the opposite order: Red → White → Blue → Green. A secret compartment opens containing the Dimensional Anchor component.
  2. Inverted Study (Save Room): The Chapter I study, now on the opposite side of the building. Save your game. Examine the inverted portrait on the wall — it conceals a safe with the Inversion Puzzle clue.
  3. Inverted Chapel: The chapel from Chapter II is now a corrupted shrine. The altar holds the second Dimensional Anchor component. A Phantom Hound pack attacks when you take it.

INVERSION PUZZLE SOLUTION

The Inversion Puzzle controls a gravity-shifting mechanism. Four levers on the wall, each labeled with a number (4, 3, 2, 1). The clue: "Count backward, and wait for the world to settle between each turn."

Solution: Pull the levers in this sequence: 4 → 3 → 2 → 1, with a 2-second pause between each pull. After the sequence, gravity shifts, opening a passage in the ceiling that was previously inaccessible. The Dimensional Anchor combines into a complete item.

BOSS: SHADOW STALKER

See our dedicated Shadow Stalker page for full strategy. Summary below:

Encounter: The Shadow Stalker ambushes you in the inverted main hall as you attempt to leave. It is a tall, gaunt humanoid that flickers between visible and invisible states.

  • When visible, it moves slowly and attacks with claw swipes. Two shotgun blasts force it to retreat and become invisible.
  • When invisible, track its movement by the red glow that appears when it prepares to attack. The glow precedes its lunge by 1 second — dodge sideways.
  • After dodging, fire a shotgun blast at the lunge origin point. This staggers it, making it visible for 3 seconds.
  • Approximately 12 shotgun shells total on Standard difficulty.

Reward: Shadow Core (key item used in Chapter X to weaken a barrier).
