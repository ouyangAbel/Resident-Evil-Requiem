RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CRAFTING GUIDE: ALL RECIPES, HERB COMBINATIONS & AMMO CRAFTING

Crafting Overview: Resident Evil Requiem features a deep crafting system using workbenches found in safe rooms. You can craft healing items, ammunition, weapon upgrades, and defensive tools using materials scavenged from the environment. Efficient crafting is the key to surviving Nightmare difficulty.

The crafting system in Resident Evil Requiem is essential for survival. Unlike earlier RE games where you relied on finding supplies, Requiem lets you convert raw materials into exactly what you need. Mastering herb combinations, ammo recipes, and upgrade material management will determine whether you barely survive or dominate every encounter. This guide covers every recipe, the best crafting strategies, and how to never run out of supplies.

WORKBENCHES: YOUR CRAFTING HUB

Workbenches are special stations found in safe rooms throughout the game. They allow you to craft items, upgrade weapons, and manage your inventory in a secure environment.

  • Location: Workbenches appear in most safe rooms and are marked on your map with a wrench icon once discovered.
  • Function: Craft ammunition, combine herbs, create defensive items, and install weapon modifications.
  • Safety: Enemies cannot enter safe rooms, so you can craft without interruption.
  • Availability: There are 14 workbenches across the game’s 12 chapters. You will never be far from one.

Pro Tip: Always visit a workbench before boss fights. Craft maximum ammo and healing items using all your stored materials. Materials do not carry over between chapters, so use them before you lose them.

HERB COMBINATIONS

Herbs are the foundation of your healing arsenal. RE Requiem features three herb colors and a robust combination system that rewards experimentation. You can combine herbs directly from your inventory without a workbench.

Combination Effect Priority
Green Herb Restores 25% health Basic healing
Green + Green Restores 50% health Efficient stack
Green + Red Full health restore Best single heal
Green + Blue 25% health + temporary defense boost Tank build
Red + Blue Full health + defense boost Premium combo
Green + Red + Blue Full health + defense + damage resistance Best combo

Herb Strategy: Always carry Green+Red combinations for emergencies. Save Blue herbs for boss fights where defense matters most. The triple mix (Green+Red+Blue) is extremely powerful but requires all three herbs — use it only before the hardest encounters.

Herb Farming Locations

Herbs respawn after certain story triggers. The best farming spots are:

  • Chapter II forest path: 3 Green herbs and 1 Blue herb respawn after the cabin encounter.
  • Chapter V garden: 2 Green, 1 Red, and 1 Blue herb available near the greenhouse.
  • Chapter VIII shadow realm: Herbs grow in the light patches. 2 Green and 1 Red per visit.
  • Chapter X convergence zone: All three herb types spawn in the dimensional rift garden.

AMMO CRAFTING RECIPES

Ammunition is crafted at workbenches using Gunpowder and Chemical Agents. Different combinations produce different ammo types. Managing your gunpowder supply is critical on higher difficulties where ammo is scarce.

HANDGUN
HANDGUN AMMO
Gunpowder (S) x1 + Chemical Agent x1

Yields: 15 rounds. The most efficient recipe for general combat. Craft these in bulk for exploration.

SHOTGUN
SHOTGUN SHELLS
Gunpowder (L) x1 + Chemical Agent x1

Yields: 6 shells. Essential for crowd control and boss fights. Save large gunpowder for these.

RIFLE
RIFLE AMMO
Gunpowder (S) x2 + Chemical Agent x1

Yields: 10 rounds. Long-range precision ammo. Useful for picking off distant threats and weak points.

MAGNUM
MAGNUM AMMO
Gunpowder (L) x2 + Chemical Agent (R) x1

Yields: 4 rounds. The most expensive recipe but deals massive damage. Reserve for bosses and elite enemies.

SPECIAL
INCENDIARY ROUNDS
Gunpowder (S) x1 + Chemical Agent (R) x1 + Plant Resin x1

Yields: 6 rounds. Deals fire damage over time. Extremely effective against organic enemies and bosses weak to fire.

SPECIAL
ACID ROUNDS
Gunpowder (L) x1 + Chemical Agent (R) x1 + Plant Resin x1

Yields: 4 rounds. Corrodes enemy armor and deals sustained damage. Best against heavily armored foes.

SPECIAL
FLASH ROUNDS
Gunpowder (S) x1 + Phosphorus x1

Yields: 3 rounds. Blinds and stuns all enemies in an area. Invaluable for crowd control in tight spaces.

DEFENSE & UTILITY CRAFTING

Beyond herbs and ammo, you can craft defensive tools and utility items that provide tactical advantages.

DEFENSE
DEFENSE ITEM
Chemical Agent x1 + Scrap Metal x1

Effect: Automatically counters a grab attack and deals damage to the attacker. You can carry up to 3.

UTILITY
FIRST AID MED
Green Herb x1 + Chemical Agent (R) x1

Effect: Instantly restores 75% health. Faster animation than herb combinations. Best crafted before boss fights.

UTILITY
HERB RECIPE (UPGRADED)
Herb Recipe + Any 2 Herbs at workbench

Effect: The Herb Recipe from the Chapter V safe unlocks enhanced combinations at workbenches, doubling the potency of herb mixes.

TOP 10 CRAFTING TIPS

TIP 01

Never Hoard Materials

Materials do not carry between chapters. Craft everything before leaving a chapter. Excess items carry over, but raw materials do not.

TIP 02

Prioritize Handgun Ammo

Handgun ammo is your bread and butter. Always craft it first when gunpowder is limited. Shotgun shells are your second priority.

TIP 03

Save Blue Herbs for Bosses

Blue herbs provide defense boosts that are most valuable during boss fights. Do not waste them on regular exploration.

TIP 04

Use Defense Items Proactively

Craft at least 2 Defense Items before entering new areas. They auto-counter grabs, which are the most dangerous attacks in the game.

TIP 05

Get the Herb Recipe Early

The Herb Recipe from the Chapter V Lab Safe (code: 06-17-89) doubles herb potency at workbenches. Do not skip this safe.

TIP 06

Craft Special Ammo for Bosses

Incendiary and Acid rounds are boss-killers. Stockpile Plant Resin and Chemical Agent (R) for these recipes before boss chapters.

TIP 07

Convert Excess Green Herbs

If you have more than 4 Green herbs, combine pairs into Green+Green for better inventory efficiency.

TIP 08

Flash Rounds Are Underrated

Flash rounds stun entire groups. Craft them before rooms with multiple enemies. They work on every enemy type including bosses.

TIP 09

Explore Before Crafting

Fully explore each area before returning to the workbench. You may find Gunpowder (L) or rare Chemical Agents in hidden rooms.

TIP 10

Nightmare Difficulty Strategy

On Nightmare, material drops are reduced by 40%. Focus on handgun ammo and Green+Red herb combos. Skip magnum ammo crafting unless facing the final boss.

FAQ: CRAFTING IN RE REQUIEM

Q1

Can you craft anywhere or only at workbenches?

Herbs can be combined anywhere from your inventory. However, ammunition, defense items, and weapon upgrades can only be crafted at workbenches.

Q2

Do materials carry over between chapters?

Finished items carry over, but raw materials do not. Always craft all materials into usable items before completing a chapter.

Q3

What is the most efficient ammo recipe?

Handgun Ammo (Gunpowder S + Chemical Agent = 15 rounds) is the most resource-efficient recipe. It gives the highest round-per-material ratio.

Q4

Where do I find the Herb Recipe?

The Herb Recipe is in the Chapter V Lab Storage Locker (code: 06-17-89). It doubles herb combination potency when used at workbenches.

Q5

Is crafting worth it on Standard difficulty?

Yes. Even on Standard, crafting gives you a significant advantage, especially special ammo types and defense items that are rarely found in the environment.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Mastering the crafting system in Resident Evil Requiem is the difference between struggling through every encounter and confidently taking on Nightmare difficulty. Prioritize handgun ammo and healing combinations, save special materials for boss fights, and always convert raw materials before chapter transitions.

The herb combination system rewards planning: carry Green+Red for emergencies, save Blue herbs for defense-heavy boss fights, and craft the triple mix only for the hardest encounters. With the Herb Recipe from Chapter V, your healing capacity doubles at workbenches.
