RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM INFINITE AMMO GUIDE: HOW TO UNLOCK ALL INFINITE WEAPONS

UPDATED JULY 15, 2026 • 16 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Running out of ammo mid-fight is one of the most terrifying moments in any Resident Evil game. In Resident Evil Requiem, Capcom brought back the beloved tradition of rewarding dedicated players with infinite ammo weapons that turn the survival horror experience into a power fantasy. Whether you want to breeze through Professional difficulty, farm resources effortlessly, or simply enjoy watching Lycans explode without worrying about your bullet count, this guide covers every method to unlock infinite ammo in RE Requiem.

There are three distinct paths to unlimited firepower: the Cat Ears accessory for all-weapon infinite ammo, the Developer Room terminal for a single weapon, and the Infinite Rocket Launcher from the Merchant. Each has different requirements, and choosing the right one depends on your playstyle, difficulty goals, and how far you have progressed through New Game Plus. We break down every option below so you can pick the fastest route to never reloading again.

KEY RULE: Infinite ammo weapons are only available after completing the game at least once. They are strictly a New Game Plus reward. You cannot access any infinite ammo method on your first playthrough, regardless of difficulty.

METHOD 1: CAT EARS ACCESSORY (ALL WEAPONS)

The Cat Ears are the gold standard of infinite ammo in Resident Evil Requiem. Equipping this accessory grants infinite ammunition for every weapon in your inventory simultaneously — handguns, shotguns, rifles, magnums, grenades, and even the Rocket Launcher. It is the single most powerful unlockable in the game and the ultimate reward for mastering RE Requiem's hardest difficulty.

UNLOCK REQUIREMENT: Achieve an S+ rank on Professional difficulty. This means completing the game in under 5 hours and 30 minutes with no more than 15 saves. Using infinite ammo weapons during the run will flag your save and disqualify you from S+.

How to Earn S+ on Professional

Professional difficulty in RE Requiem is brutal. Enemies deal roughly double damage compared to Standard, healing items are scarcer, and aggressive enemy AI means you cannot simply run past every encounter. Earning S+ requires a combination of route optimization, resource management, and boss fight mastery.

  • Time target: Under 5 hours 30 minutes total. The world record is around 2 hours 15 minutes, but most players should aim for 4 to 5 hours on a first S+ attempt.
  • Save limit: Maximum 15 saves across the entire run. Plan your saves before boss fights and difficult stealth sections, not after easy encounters.
  • No infinite ammo: The game tracks whether infinite ammo was active during your run. If it detects the Cat Ears, Infinite Rocket Launcher, or Developer Room infinite ammo on any weapon, your rank is capped at S.
  • Skip optional content: Side missions, collectibles, and secret areas waste time. Focus on the critical path and only detour for mandatory key items.

Pro Tip: Practice each chapter individually on Standard difficulty first. Learn the exact room layouts, enemy spawns, and key item locations. By the time you attempt Professional S+, you should know the critical path from memory. Our Speedrun Guide has optimized routes for every chapter.

METHOD 2: DEVELOPER ROOM TERMINAL (SINGLE WEAPON)

Hidden in Chapter 11, the Developer Room contains a secret terminal that grants infinite ammo for one weapon of your choice. Unlike the Cat Ears, this method does not require a speedrun or difficulty challenge — you just need to find the room and make a strategic choice about which weapon to bless with unlimited ammunition.

CHAPTER 11 - FINAL DESCENT
DEVELOPER ROOM AMMO TERMINAL
LOCATION: Rooftop behind the helipad (Developer Keycard required)

The terminal is a green-screened computer on the right wall of the Developer Room. Interact with it and you will be prompted to select one weapon from your current inventory. That weapon receives infinite ammo for the remainder of the playthrough. The choice is permanent per save file — you cannot re-select. The Developer Keycard is found in Chapter 9, Forgotten City archives, in a locked drawer (code: 1996).

METHOD 3: INFINITE ROCKET LAUNCHER (MERCHANT)

The classic Resident Evil tradition returns. After clearing the game once on any difficulty, the Merchant's shop adds the Infinite Rocket Launcher to his inventory. This weapon fires unlimited rockets that deal massive area-of-effect damage, one-shotting virtually every non-boss enemy in the game.

MERCHANT SHOP - NEW GAME PLUS ONLY
INFINITE ROCKET LAUNCHER
PRICE: 2,000,000 Lei

Available immediately at the start of NG+ after completing the game on any difficulty. The Infinite Rocket Launcher fires unlimited rockets with no reload. Each rocket deals approximately 4,500 damage in a wide blast radius. It cannot be upgraded or customized. The 2 million Lei price tag sounds steep, but our Farming Guide shows methods to accumulate this within 2 hours of dedicated farming.

Farming 2 Million Lei Fast

The Infinite Rocket Launcher costs a fortune, but RE Requiem has several lucrative farming spots:

  • Chapter 5 Merchant Loop: Clear the estate grounds, sell all treasures, reload checkpoint. Average yield: 180,000 Lei per run (about 8 minutes).
  • Chapter 9 City Arena: The Forgotten City arena respawns enemies that drop high-value gems. Kill, loot, sell, repeat. Average yield: 250,000 Lei per run.
  • NG+ Speed Farming: If you are already in NG+, rush through Chapters 1-3 selling every item. You can accumulate 2 million Lei in roughly 90 minutes with efficient routing.
  • Treasure Combos: Combine gems with treasures before selling. A Ruby + Elegant Bangle combo sells for 48,000 Lei versus 30,000 Lei sold separately. Always check our Merchant Guide for the optimal sell combinations.

DOES INFINITE AMMO DISABLE TROPHIES?

This is the most common question about infinite ammo in RE Requiem, and the answer is no. Unlike Resident Evil 4 Remake where certain challenges were locked behind no-infinite-ammo runs, RE Requiem allows you to earn every single trophy and achievement while using infinite ammo weapons.

However, there is one critical exception: S+ rank runs. If you equip the Cat Ears, fire the Infinite Rocket Launcher, or use the Developer Room infinite ammo terminal at any point during a Professional difficulty run, the game permanently flags that save file. Your maximum achievable rank drops from S+ to S, even if you unequip the infinite ammo weapon later.

Bottom Line: Use infinite ammo freely for trophy hunting, collectible gathering, and casual NG+ playthroughs. But keep a separate clean save file for your Professional S+ attempt — one that has never touched infinite ammo.

BEST STRATEGIES FOR INFINITE AMMO RUNS

STRATEGY 1

The Rocket Launcher Speedrun

Equip the Infinite Rocket Launcher and blast through every encounter in seconds. This trivializes the entire game but is the fastest way to earn trophies, complete NG+ challenges, and unlock remaining content. Just watch your positioning — the splash damage can stagger or kill you in tight corridors. Best used in open areas and against groups.

STRATEGY 2

Cat Ears + Magnum for Boss Rush

With Cat Ears equipped, the Lightning Hawk Magnum becomes a boss-deleting machine. Infinite magnum rounds mean you can spam the highest-damage-per-shot weapon in the game without hesitation. Boss fights that took 10+ minutes on Standard become 90-second encounters. This is the fastest method for farming boss-related trophies.

STRATEGY 3

Developer Room Shotgun for Exploration

If you prefer a more balanced experience, use the Developer Room terminal on the Assault Shotgun. You still need to manage ammo for your secondary weapons, which preserves some survival horror tension, but you always have a powerful close-range option available. This is the best approach for players who want infinite ammo convenience without completely removing the challenge.

STRATEGY 4

Primal Knife + Infinite Ammo Sidearm

Combine the unbreakable Primal Knife (from collecting all Mr. Raccoon figures) with the Developer Room infinite ammo on a pistol. This gives you unlimited close-range and mid-range capability while still requiring you to manage shotgun and rifle ammo. A great middle-ground for players who want to feel powerful but not invincible.

RECOMMENDED UNLOCK ORDER

Not sure which infinite ammo method to pursue first? Here is the optimal progression path for most players:

  1. First Playthrough (any difficulty): Complete the game normally. Enjoy the survival horror experience as intended. This unlocks NG+ and the Infinite Rocket Launcher in the Merchant's shop.
  2. Second Playthrough (NG+ on Standard/Hardcore): Buy the Infinite Rocket Launcher (farm Lei first using our Farming Guide). Use it to mop up remaining collectibles, side missions, and the True Ending path. Also find the Developer Room and use its terminal on your preferred weapon.
  3. Third Playthrough (NG+ Professional): With all key items, weapons, and upgrades from your previous runs, tackle Professional difficulty for the S+ rank. Use the optimized routes from our Speedrun Guide. This unlocks the Cat Ears.
  4. Fourth Playthrough (NG+ with Cat Ears): Equip Cat Ears and enjoy god mode. Clean up any remaining trophies, explore every corner of every chapter, and experiment with every weapon at infinite ammo. This is your victory lap.

FAQ: INFINITE AMMO IN RE REQUIEM

Q1

How do you get infinite ammo in Resident Evil Requiem?

There are three main methods: (1) Earn an S+ rank on Professional difficulty to unlock the Cat Ears accessory, which grants infinite ammo for all weapons. (2) Use the Developer Room terminal in Chapter 11 to grant infinite ammo for a single weapon per save file. (3) Purchase the Infinite Rocket Launcher from the Merchant after clearing the game once on any difficulty.

Q2

Does infinite ammo disable trophies in RE Requiem?

No. Unlike some older RE titles, using infinite ammo weapons does NOT disable trophy or achievement progress in Resident Evil Requiem. You can earn every trophy, including the platinum, while using infinite ammo. However, S+ rank runs require you to NOT use infinite ammo — the game flags your save if infinite ammo was active.

Q3

What is the best infinite weapon in RE Requiem?

The Infinite Rocket Launcher is the most powerful option for casual NG+ runs, one-shotting every non-boss enemy. For S+ rank Professional runs where infinite ammo is banned, the fully upgraded Stingray rifle with its exclusive upgrade offers the best damage-to-ammo efficiency. The Infinite Assault Shotgun (unlocked via Cat Ears) is the best all-around choice for crowd control and boss fights.

Q4

Can you get infinite ammo on your first playthrough?

No. All infinite ammo methods require completing the game at least once. The Developer Room terminal and Cat Ears both require NG+. The Infinite Rocket Launcher becomes available in the Merchant's shop only after your first clear. Infinite ammo is strictly a reward for repeat playthroughs.

Q5

Can I change my Developer Room terminal weapon choice?

No. The Developer Room terminal grants infinite ammo to one weapon permanently per save file. There is no way to reset or change your choice. If you want infinite ammo on a different weapon, you would need to start a new save or use the Cat Ears accessory instead, which covers all weapons.

Q6

Is the Infinite Rocket Launcher worth 2 million Lei?

Absolutely. At 2 million Lei, it sounds expensive, but efficient farming methods can generate this amount in 1 to 2 hours. The Infinite Rocket Launcher trivializes every encounter in the game and is the single best tool for trophy cleanup, collectible hunting, and casual NG+ enjoyment. It pays for itself in saved frustration immediately.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem handles infinite ammo better than any previous entry in the franchise. The three-method system — Cat Ears for completionists, Developer Room for strategic players, and Infinite Rocket Launcher for instant gratification — means there is an infinite ammo option for every playstyle and skill level.

The Cat Ears remain the ultimate flex, requiring genuine mastery of Professional difficulty and speedrun-level knowledge of every chapter. But even if S+ rank feels out of reach, the Developer Room terminal and Infinite Rocket Launcher provide powerful alternatives that still feel rewarding to unlock. Whichever path you choose, the moment you fire that first infinite magazine is one of the most satisfying experiences in RE Requiem.
