PUZZLE OVERVIEW & TYPES

Resident Evil Requiem features five distinct puzzle types, each requiring a different approach. Understanding these categories will help you recognize what kind of solution you're looking for when you encounter a new puzzle in the game.

The Five Puzzle Categories

Puzzle Type Description Example
Environmental Interact with objects in the correct sequence or manipulate the environment Restoring power to the estate wing
Item-Based Combine or use inventory items to solve a puzzle Creating a chemical key to melt ice barriers
Logic/Riddle Decode clues to find a solution Family portrait number sequence
Sequence/Timing Perform actions in a specific order within a time limit Firing range activation sequence

PRO TIP

Always Examine Everything

Many puzzles in RE Requiem are solved by closely examining environmental details. Rotate your camera, check behind furniture, and read every scrap of paper. The game rewards thorough exploration — if something looks slightly out of place, it probably is. The UV flashlight (found in Chapter IV) reveals hidden messages on walls and objects throughout the entire game, so backtrack with it to uncover secrets you missed earlier.

ACT I: CHAPTERS I–III PUZZLES

The opening chapters of Resident Evil Requiem introduce puzzle mechanics through relatively straightforward challenges. These early puzzles teach core skills you'll need for more complex challenges later in the game.

Chapter I: The Awakening — Fuse Box Puzzle

After the tutorial sequence, you'll encounter a locked door requiring power restoration. In the maintenance room, find three fuses labeled with Roman numerals (I, II, III) and a damaged fuse box. The solution requires placing them in the correct order based on a nearby maintenance log.

Solution: Insert fuses in order: III → I → II. The maintenance log reads "Phase three initialized first, followed by primary and secondary" — but the fuse slots are mislabeled. The actual sequence corresponds to the phases, not the numbers.

Reward: Access to the east wing, containing 500 Lei and a Handgun Ammo crafting material.

Chapter I: Portrait Number Puzzle

In the main hall, four portraits hang on the wall, each showing a family member with a small number painted near their hand. A locked cabinet requires a 4-digit code. The trick is that the numbers are only visible when you examine the portraits from the correct angle (kneel and look up).

Solution: The numbers from left to right are: 3, 7, 1, 9. Enter 3719 into the cabinet lock.

Reward: Shotgun Upgrade Part (increased pellet spread).

Chapter II: Echoes of the Estate — Musical Lock Puzzle

The music room contains a locked chest that requires playing the correct melody on a harpsichord. Sheet music is found in the library (Page 1) and the dining room (Page 2). The melody must be played in reverse order from how the pages suggest.

Solution: Play the notes: E♭, C, A, G♭, D, B♭. The sheet music shows B♭-D-G♭-A-C-E♭, but playing it backward opens the lock.

Reward: Treasure: Vintage Pocket Watch (sells for 3,000 Lei).

Chapter III: Below the Surface — Water Valve Puzzle

The flooded basement requires redirecting water flow through a series of valves. Five valves control water to different sections. A drain diagram in the pump room shows the correct configuration.

Solution: Turn valves A (open), B (closed), C (open), D (closed), E (open). The diagram shows open valves forming the shape of a crown — match that pattern.

Reward: Drains water, revealing a hidden room with a Magnum Ammo crafting material and the "Gardener's Diary" side mission clue.

ACT II: CHAPTERS IV–VI PUZZLES

The middle chapters introduce more complex puzzles that require combining items and backtracking with new tools. Act II puzzles are significantly more challenging and often require multiple steps to complete.

Chapter IV: The Crimson Wing — UV Message Puzzle

After acquiring the UV flashlight in the morgue, return to the patient ward. Shine the UV light on the wall above the beds to reveal a message: "THE CURE LIES WITHIN THE HEALER'S HAND." This refers to the nurse's station in the next room.

Solution: Examine the nurse's station with UV light active. A hidden drawer opens containing a Chemical Key item, needed to melt the ice barrier blocking the pharmacy.

Reward: Access to pharmacy with Green Herb x3, First Aid Spray, and a weapon upgrade material.

Chapter V: Betrayal in the Dark — Electrical Grid Puzzle

Grace Hospital's emergency power system requires rerouting electricity through circuit breakers. Six breakers must be set correctly, and the solution is encoded in a patient transfer log found on the third floor.

Solution: Breakers 1, 3, 5 = ON; Breakers 2, 4, 6 = OFF. The transfer log lists room numbers: 101, 103, 105 (active patients) vs 102, 104, 106 (discharged). Match active rooms to ON.

Reward: Power restored to elevator, accessing the basement laboratory with a rare crafting material and HUNK encounter trigger.

Chapter VI: The Living Laboratory — Specimen Organizer Puzzle

The laboratory contains a specimen room with 12 empty jars and a research journal describing the correct arrangement. Each jar has a colored label, and the journal provides clues about which specimens go where.

Solution: Match specimens to jars based on the journal's chronological order: 1-Liver, 2-Kidney, 3-Heart, 4-Lung, 5-Brain, 6-Eye, 7-Spleen, 8-Stomach, 9-Intestine, 10-Blood, 11-Bone, 12-Tissue. The journal entries are numbered but shuffled.

Reward: Unlocks a hidden passage containing the "Lab Technician's Last Log" side mission document and a Stock Upgrade for the Magnum.

IMPORTANT

Chapter VI Timing Puzzle

The "Containment Protocol" puzzle in Chapter VI requires activating four emergency lockdown switches in the correct sequence within 60 seconds. The sequence is: Red (Alpha Wing), Blue (Beta Wing), Green (Gamma Wing), Yellow (Delta Wing). This corresponds to the color-coded emergency map at the security desk. Failure triggers a Lycan ambush — be ready to fight.

ACT III: CHAPTERS VII–IX PUZZLES

Late-game puzzles in Resident Evil Requiem combine mechanics from earlier chapters and require mastery of multiple tools. These puzzles are the most challenging in the game and often guard the most valuable rewards.

Chapter VII: The Requiem Begins — Clock Tower Puzzle

The clock tower mechanism requires setting three clock faces to specific times based on clues found throughout the chapter. Each time corresponds to a significant event mentioned in found documents.

Solution: Set clocks to: 11:45 PM (estate purchase time), 3:33 AM (first experiment), and 7:19 PM (outbreak start). Found in: Estate deed (library), Lab log (basement), and News clipping (office).

Reward: Opens the tower staircase, accessing the rooftop with a sniper rifle upgrade part and lore document.

Chapter VIII: The Convergence Lab — Laser Alignment Puzzle

The security room contains three laser emitters that must be aligned to hit corresponding receivers. Rotating mirrors direct the beams, but some mirrors are locked and require finding their keys.

Solution: Mirror A (rotate 90° clockwise), Mirror B (rotate 180°), Mirror C (rotate 45° counter-clockwise). The key to Mirror B is in the supply closet (code: 2001, found on a calendar). The laser frequency must also be set to 650nm (red) for the receivers to activate.

Reward: Access to the developer area, containing the Developer Room keycard and infinite ammo weapon unlock token.

Chapter IX: Shadow Stalker Ambush — Pressure Plate Puzzle

The underground tunnels contain a room with pressure plates that must be activated in a specific sequence. The sequence is hinted at by the pattern of scratch marks on the walls.

Solution: Step on plates in the pattern shown by the scratch marks: Center → Top-Left → Bottom-Right → Top-Right → Bottom-Left. This creates an X pattern. A weight must remain on the center plate while the others are activated — use a nearby cinder block.

Reward: Opens the path to the Abyssal Altar side mission and a rare weapon skin.

ACT IV: CHAPTERS X–XII PUZZLES

The final chapters feature the most complex puzzles in Resident Evil Requiem, requiring knowledge of nearly every item and mechanic in the game. Mastery of earlier puzzles is essential for solving these endgame challenges.

Chapter X: The Forgotten City — Astral Projection Puzzle

The observatory contains a massive orrery that must be aligned to reflect a specific star chart. The chart is found in three separate pieces throughout the chapter, and the orrery must be set to the correct planetary positions.

Solution: Position planets: Mercury (leftmost), Venus (second), Earth (third), Mars (fourth), Jupiter (fifth), Saturn (rightmost). The star chart shows the solar system from above, with the sun at center. The clue reads "as above, so below" — position them in actual solar system order.

Reward: Reveals a hidden chamber containing the "Forgotten King" boss fight and the legendary weapon blueprint.

Chapter XI: The Final Descent — DNA Sequence Puzzle

The laboratory's genetic scanner requires inputting a DNA sequence. Four nucleotide chains (A, T, C, G) must be arranged in the correct order based on a viral research document found in Chapter VI.

Solution: Sequence: G-A-T-C-C-A-A-T-G-C. This is the "Requiem Sequence" mentioned in the viral research document found in the specimen room (Chapter VI). The document reads: "God And The Children Are The Messengers Of God's Creation" — first letters spell the sequence.

Reward: Access to the deepest laboratory area, containing the final weapon upgrade and the Developer Room entrance.

Chapter XII: Requiem — The Final Confrontation Puzzle

During the final boss encounter, the arena contains four power nodes that must be activated in sequence to weaken the boss. The activation order corresponds to the four elements shown in murals around the arena.

Solution: Activate nodes in order: Water (blue), Earth (green), Fire (red), Air (white). This order follows the alchemical dissolution process described in the Chapter XII lore document: "Water dissolves, Earth corrodes, Fire consumes, Air disperses." Activating them in the wrong order resets the sequence and heals the boss.

Reward: Boss weakened for the final phase, enabling the true ending path.

HIDDEN & SECRET PUZZLES

Beyond the main chapter puzzles, Resident Evil Requiem contains several hidden puzzles that require extraordinary attention to detail. These puzzles are optional but offer the best rewards in the game.

The Firing Range Secret

After completing the Chapter V firing range (target practice), hit all bullseyes in under 30 seconds. This triggers a hidden fourth challenge: moving targets that spell out a word when hit in the correct order.

Solution: Hit targets spelling: R-E-Q-U-I-E-M. The targets light up briefly in sequence — follow the flashing lights rather than choosing randomly. Completing this unlocks a secret weapon skin and 10,000 Lei.

The Gardener's Cipher

Found in the Gardener's Diary side mission, a page contains a cipher that decodes to a location. The cipher uses a substitution code where each letter is shifted by 3 positions (Caesar cipher).

Solution: Decoded message: "BENEATH THE FOUNTAIN LIES WHAT THE FAMILY COVERS." Dig beneath the garden fountain (Chapter II) to find a hidden cache containing a rare crafting material and the "Family Secret" lore document.

The Developer Room Access

The Developer Room, accessible in Chapter XI, requires a keycard hidden in Chapter 9. However, entering the room triggers a final puzzle: a console with a text-based mini-game.

Solution: Type commands in order: LOOK → TAKE KEYCARD → INVENTORY → USE KEYCARD → NORTH → LOOK → TAKE WEAPON → QUIT. This is an homage to classic adventure games and references the original Resident Evil mansion. Successfully completing it unlocks the "Classic Camera Angle" mode and infinite ammo for all weapons.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

How many puzzles are in Resident Evil Requiem?

There are 16 distinct puzzles across all 12 chapters, excluding safe combination locks. This includes 13 main chapter puzzles and 3 hidden secret puzzles. Safe codes are covered separately in our Safe Codes Guide.

Q2

Can I miss puzzles permanently?

Most puzzles can be revisited later, but the Developer Room puzzle (Chapter XI) becomes inaccessible after completing Chapter XII. The Firing Range secret (Chapter V) can be attempted at any time after unlocking the firing range. Other puzzles remain available until you finish their respective chapters.

Q3

Do puzzles affect the ending?

Yes — solving the final power node puzzle (Chapter XII) correctly is required for the true ending. Additionally, finding all hidden puzzles and collecting their associated lore documents contributes to unlocking the secret ending path. See our All Endings Guide for details.

Q4

What's the best order to solve puzzles?

Solve puzzles as you encounter them during your first playthrough. The only backtracking recommended is using the UV flashlight (Chapter IV) to revisit earlier chapters for hidden UV messages. On NG+, you can focus on missed puzzles with all items already collected.

Q5

Are there difficulty-specific puzzle differences?

No — puzzles are identical across all difficulty levels. The solution to each puzzle remains the same whether you're playing on Standard, Hardcore, or Professional. Only enemy placement and resource availability change between difficulties.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem's puzzles represent some of the best environmental problem-solving in the series. From the elegant simplicity of the fuse box puzzle in Chapter I to the multi-layered DNA sequence puzzle in Chapter XI, each challenge is designed to reward careful observation and lateral thinking. The hidden puzzles, in particular, add tremendous replay value and offer the game's most coveted rewards.

For 100% completion and the true ending, mastering every puzzle is essential. Keep this guide handy during your playthrough, and don't hesitate to experiment — sometimes the most complex solutions are hiding in plain sight. Good luck surviving Raccoon City's final chapter.
