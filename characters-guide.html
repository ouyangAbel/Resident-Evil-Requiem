RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CHARACTERS GUIDE: ALL CHARACTERS, BACKSTORIES & VOICE ACTORS

Resident Evil Requiem features one of the richest casts in the franchise's history, blending returning legends with compelling new characters who drive a story that spans multiple dimensions. From Leon Scott Kennedy's haunting journey through the Rookwood Estate to Grace Ashcroft's desperate fight for survival, every character brings something essential to Capcom's 2026 survival horror masterpiece.

This complete character guide covers all 10 major characters, their backstories, relationships, story roles, and voice actor details. Whether you are trying to understand Victor Gideon's motivations, untangle HUNK's allegiances, or simply want to know who voiced your favorite character, you will find everything here.

MAIN CHARACTERS

PROTAGONIST • SERIES VETERAN
LEON SCOTT KENNEDY
DSO Special Agent — Playable Character (Chapters 1–12)
VOICE ACTOR: Nick Apostolides

Leon Scott Kennedy returns as the primary protagonist of Resident Evil Requiem, and this may be his darkest chapter yet. Now a seasoned DSO (Division of Security Operations) agent with over two decades of bioterrorism experience, Leon is dispatched to investigate the mysterious Rookwood Estate after a distress signal from a missing research team.

What begins as a routine investigation spirals into a multidimensional nightmare. Leon's journey through RE Requiem tests him in ways no previous mission has — forcing him to confront not just grotesque bioweapons, but his own past traumas. The controversial "nurse scene" early in the game has become one of the most debated moments in Resident Evil history, raising questions about whether years of combat have fundamentally changed Leon's character.

Leon's relationship with Grace Ashcroft — a researcher he rescues from the Rookwood Estate — forms the emotional core of the story. Their dynamic evolves from reluctant allies to something deeper, with Leon serving as both protector and mentor as Grace uncovers her own hidden connections to the Umbrella legacy.

PROTAGONIST • NEW CHARACTER
GRACE ASHCROFT
Ark Lab Researcher — Playable Character (Chapters 3–12)
VOICE ACTOR: Nicole Tompkins

Grace Ashcroft is Resident Evil Requiem's breakout character — a brilliant biochemist working at the Ark Laboratory whose life is shattered when the facility falls to a biohazard outbreak. Initially introduced as a civilian in need of rescue, Grace quickly proves herself to be far more than a damsel in distress.

A researcher specializing in viral mutation patterns, Grace holds critical knowledge about the dimensional rift phenomenon that drives the game's central mystery. Her scientific expertise becomes essential to understanding — and ultimately stopping — the catastrophe unfolding across multiple realities. The game's second half features extensive Grace chapters, including the fan-favorite Grace's Hospital Escape sequence (Chapter 5) and her solo exploration of the Forgotten City (Chapter 9).

Perhaps the most impactful revelation about Grace is her hidden lineage: late-game documents reveal she is the granddaughter of a senior Umbrella Corporation scientist, a secret her family buried for decades. This discovery recontextualizes her entire character arc and directly ties into the True Ending's franchise-shaking revelations.

ANTAGONIST
VICTOR GIDEON
Cult Leader & Dimensional Catalyst — Main Antagonist
VOICE ACTOR: Neil Newbon

Victor Gideon is the chilling primary antagonist of Resident Evil Requiem and one of the most complex villains the franchise has ever produced. Once a respected quantum physicist, Gideon's obsession with accessing parallel dimensions led him down a dark path that fused cutting-edge science with ancient occult rituals.

Gideon commands a cult-like following known as The Resonance, composed of disillusioned scientists, mercenaries, and desperate civilians who believe he can access a dimension free of suffering. His experiments at the Rookwood Estate successfully tore open a dimensional rift — but what came through was not salvation. It was the Amalgam, a nightmarish fusion organism that began consuming and reshaping reality itself.

What makes Gideon truly memorable is his conviction. He genuinely believes he is saving humanity, and his philosophical debates with Leon throughout the game offer some of the best writing in the series. His boss fight — the "Dirt Bike Pursuit" sequence in Chapter 6 — stands as an instant classic, with Leon chasing Gideon through a collapsing dimensional corridor on a stolen motorcycle.

WILDCARD • SERIES VETERAN
HUNK
Umbrella Security Service Operative — Secret Boss / NPC
VOICE ACTOR: Keith Silverstein

The legendary Grim Reaper returns. HUNK (Human Unit Never Killed) appears in Resident Evil Requiem as both a hostile NPC encounter and an optional secret boss fight in Chapter 8. His presence suggests Umbrella's remnants have a vested interest in Gideon's dimensional research — and they sent their best operative to secure it.

HUNK operates with surgical precision throughout the game, appearing at critical moments to retrieve research data, eliminate witnesses, and — memorably — engage Leon in a brutal close-quarters fight in the RPD armory. Defeating HUNK as an optional boss rewards players with the HUNK Mask accessory (10% damage reduction) and the "The Fourth Survivor" achievement.

Despite his antagonistic role, HUNK's motivations are strictly professional. He has no personal vendetta against Leon; he is simply executing his mission parameters with the cold efficiency that has kept him alive through every major Umbrella incident. An encrypted file in Chapter 11 reveals his extraction was successful, confirming he survives — as always.

SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

VICTIM • STORY CATALYST
EMILY HARPER
Nurse at Rookwood Estate Infirmary
VOICE ACTOR: Kari Wahlgren

Emily Harper is a nurse employed at the Rookwood Estate's private medical facility, and her tragic fate serves as the emotional catalyst for the entire story. Hired by Victor Gideon to care for "test subjects" she was told were volunteers in a medical trial, Emily discovered the horrifying truth too late — Gideon was using the infirmary to monitor the biological effects of dimensional exposure on human subjects.

Her death scene, witnessed by Leon in the game's most controversial moment, has been analyzed endlessly by the community. Emily's final act — hiding a critical data drive in the hospital chapel before her death — provides Leon and Grace with the information they need to understand Gideon's plan. Posthumously, she may be the most important character in the game: without her sacrifice, the True Ending would be impossible to achieve.

MERCHANT • ALLY
MARCUS RHODES
Black Market Arms Dealer — The Merchant
VOICE ACTOR: Michael Schwalbe

Every Resident Evil game needs a merchant, and Marcus Rhodes fills that role with style. A former military armorer who went underground after refusing to supply an Umbrella-affiliated operation, Rhodes operates a network of safe rooms and supply caches throughout the game's locations. His basement workshop, accessible from Chapter 2 onward, serves as the game's primary hub for weapon upgrades, item trading, and valuable intel.

Rhodes is more than a vendor — he is a fully-realized character with his own arc. His brother was one of Gideon's early test subjects, and his personal investment in stopping The Resonance adds weight to every transaction. The game rewards players who invest in their relationship with Rhodes: purchasing enough upgrades unlocks exclusive dialogue, a hidden side mission (the Rhodes Gunsmith quest), and discounts on all future purchases.

Easter egg hunters will appreciate that Rhodes occasionally quotes the original RE4 Merchant — most notably dropping the iconic "Got some rare things on sale, stranger" when Leon visits with exactly zero Lei.

REFERENCED & EASTER EGG CHARACTERS

MYSTERY • FUTURE SETUP
ADA WONG
Shadow Operative — Referenced in Files Only
VOICE ACTOR: Lily Gao (voice cameo in True Ending)

Ada Wong does not physically appear in Resident Evil Requiem, but her presence is felt throughout. A classified document in Chapter 7's laboratory explicitly references "Agent Wong" as having been assigned to a parallel operation deemed higher priority than the Rookwood incident. This confirms Ada is active in the Requiem timeline and operating independently of both Leon and any known agency.

Players who unlock the True Ending are treated to a brief post-credits audio cameo: Ada's voice, unmistakable, speaking a single line into a communicator — "The package is secured. Tell them I said hello." The identity of who she is addressing and what "the package" contains are left deliberately ambiguous, but the community consensus is that this is a direct sequel hook.

Ada's sunglasses and grapple gun can be found as collectible Easter eggs in Chapter 9, and examining both in sequence triggers a unique piece of dialogue from Leon.

ANTAGONIST • EASTER EGG
ALBERT WESKER
Deceased — Referenced via Easter Eggs
VOICE ACTOR: D.C. Douglas (archival audio in Developer Room)

Though long dead in the mainline timeline (killed in RE5's volcano finale), Albert Wesker's shadow looms large over Resident Evil Requiem. His research into viral transcendence directly inspired Victor Gideon's dimensional experiments, and Gideon's personal journals reveal a near-religious reverence for Wesker's work.

Wesker's presence can be felt through multiple Easter eggs: his iconic sunglasses appear in the Chapter 9 museum (unlocking the Wesker costume for HUNK mode), a computer terminal in Chapter 10 flashes his legendary line "COMPLETE. GLOBAL. SATURATION.", and the Developer Room contains archival audio files of Wesker's speeches from across the series. The game strongly implies that Gideon's cult, The Resonance, was built on partially recovered Wesker research.

SIDE CHARACTER
AGENT CARTER
DSO Operative — Leon's Former Partner
VOICE ACTOR: Jeff Schine

Agent Carter appears only through audio logs and a single radio conversation in Chapter 4, but his role is crucial to understanding Leon's state of mind. Carter was Leon's partner on a mission one year before the events of Requiem — a mission that went catastrophically wrong, resulting in Carter being critically injured and forcibly retired from field duty. Leon carries guilt over Carter's fate, and their strained relationship adds depth to Leon's characterization throughout the game.

Carter's audio logs, scattered across Chapters 1–5, serve as a secondary narrative thread. Through them, players learn about the DSO's internal politics, the bureaucratic obstacles that delayed the Rookwood response, and Carter's growing suspicion that someone within the organization is covering up the true nature of Gideon's research. His final log, found in Chapter 11, reveals he has been running an off-the-books investigation and has uncovered evidence of a mole inside the DSO — a thread left tantalizingly unresolved.

EASTER EGG • SERIES VETERAN
RICHARD AIKEN
S.T.A.R.S. Bravo Team — Referenced via Easter Egg
VOICE ACTOR: None (text only)

Richard Aiken, the doomed S.T.A.R.S. Bravo Team member from the original Resident Evil, receives a touching tribute in RE Requiem. His hidden office in Chapter 7 — accessible by destroying a cracked wall near the east stairwell — contains a detailed backstory expansion. According to files found in the office, Richard was assigned to investigate the Rookwood Estate decades before the mansion incident, making him one of the first S.T.A.R.S. members to encounter bio-organic weapons.

The office contains Richard's signature anti-venom serum (a nod to the snake bite scene from RE1), an Assault Shotgun upgrade, and a personal journal that bridges the gap between the Rookwood investigation and his eventual assignment to the Spencer Mansion. It is a deeply rewarding Easter egg for longtime fans and one of the most emotionally resonant secrets in the game.

CHARACTER RELATIONSHIP MAP

Understanding how the characters connect is essential to following RE Requiem's layered narrative. Here is a breakdown of the key relationships:

  • Leon → Grace: Reluctant protector evolves into trusted partner. Leon sees echoes of his younger self in Grace's determination. Their bond is the story's emotional anchor.
  • Grace → Leon: Initially dependent, Grace grows to challenge and complement Leon. Her scientific mind fills the gaps in his tactical approach. By the final chapter, they function as equals.
  • Gideon → Leon: Philosophical nemesis. Gideon sees Leon as a kindred spirit — someone who has also been broken by bioterrorism — and spends much of the game trying to convert rather than kill him.
  • HUNK → Leon: Professional respect, zero personal investment. HUNK views Leon as an obstacle, not a target. Their encounters are transactional, never emotional.
  • Rhodes → Leon & Grace: Genuine ally. Rhodes lost his brother to Gideon's experiments, giving his support of Leon and Grace a deeply personal motivation beyond commerce.
  • Emily → Leon: The ghost that haunts him. Emily's death is the moment Leon questions everything he believes about himself as a protector. Her hidden data drive is her redemption.

FAQ: CHARACTERS IN RE REQUIEM

FAQ 1
Is Leon's personality different in Requiem?

Yes, and this is intentional. Capcom has stated that RE Requiem's Leon is a man who has been fighting bioterrorism for nearly 30 years. He is more withdrawn, more haunted, and more morally gray than in previous appearances. The controversial nurse scene is designed to make players question whether Leon has fundamentally changed — or whether the circumstances left him with no good options. It is a deliberate creative choice and has sparked exactly the kind of debate the writers intended.

FAQ 2
Will Grace Ashcroft appear in future RE games?

Capcom has not officially confirmed anything, but the True Ending strongly positions Grace as a major player in the franchise's future. Her hidden Umbrella lineage, her scientific expertise, and her newfound combat experience make her an ideal protagonist for a sequel. The community is overwhelmingly in favor of seeing her return — she has quickly become one of the most popular new characters in recent RE history.

FAQ 3
Does Ada Wong actually appear in the game?

Not physically, no. Ada does not have a character model or any on-screen appearances in RE Requiem. She is referenced in Chapter 7 files, and her voice is heard in the True Ending post-credits scene. This is widely interpreted as Capcom setting up Ada for a larger role in the next entry, likely tied to whatever "package" she mentions securing.

FAQ 4
How do I unlock the HUNK secret boss fight?

In Chapter 8 (Dimensions Apart), after the dimensional rift stabilizes but before exiting the chapter, return to the RPD armory. HUNK will appear as a hostile NPC. Defeating him rewards the HUNK Mask accessory and the "The Fourth Survivor" achievement. This fight is extremely difficult on first playthrough — bring magnum ammo and mastered Perfect Dodge timing.

FAQ 5
Is Victor Gideon connected to previous RE villains?

Yes, indirectly. Gideon's research was built on partially recovered Albert Wesker data, and his cult (The Resonance) incorporated remnants of the Los Illuminados' ideology alongside Wesker's viral transcendence theories. Gideon is a thematic fusion of the franchise's two great villain traditions: the scientific ambition of Wesker and the cult fanaticism of Osmund Saddler.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem delivers the richest character work in the franchise since Resident Evil 4. Leon's evolution from rookie cop to battle-hardened survivor reaches a new apex here, while Grace Ashcroft instantly establishes herself as a worthy addition to the series' pantheon of protagonists. Victor Gideon joins the ranks of Wesker and Saddler as a villain whose motivations are genuinely compelling, and the supporting cast — from the tragic Emily Harper to the scene-stealing Marcus Rhodes — fills out a world that feels lived-in and consequential.

The voice cast is arguably the series' best, with Nick Apostolides and Nicole Tompkins delivering career-defining performances and Neil Newbon bringing theatrical menace to every one of Gideon's scenes. The Easter egg appearances by Ada Wong and Albert Wesker, meanwhile, demonstrate how thoughtfully Capcom is weaving the franchise's legacy into its future.
