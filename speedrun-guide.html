GENERAL SPEEDRUN STRATEGIES

Before diving into chapter-by-chapter routing, master these universal speedrun principles. They apply to every chapter and collectively save over 30 minutes across a full run.

PRINCIPLE 1

Run Past Everything (Unless It Blocks a Door)

The single biggest time-save in RE Requiem is ignoring enemies. Most standard enemies can be sprinted past without taking damage if you zigzag. Only kill enemies that physically block a doorway or corridor you must traverse. Bloated Lycans, Shadow Wraiths in tight hallways, and enemies in mandatory combat arenas are the exceptions. For everything else: run, do not fight.

PRINCIPLE 2

Animation Cancel Everything

You can cancel almost every animation in the game. Door-opening animations cancel by quick-turning (down + sprint) the moment the door begins to open. Ladder climbs cancel by pressing crouch at the top. Item-pickup animations cancel by opening the inventory the frame the pickup begins. Vault animations cancel by aiming down sights mid-vault. Practice these until they become muscle memory — animation cancels save roughly 8 to 12 minutes across a full run.

PRINCIPLE 3

Master the Perfect Dodge

Speedrunning without taking damage is essential for S+ rank (zero deaths). The Perfect Dodge window on Professional difficulty is just 6 frames (100ms at 60fps). You must dodge at the exact moment an enemy attack connects. Practice against the Infected Staff enemies in Chapter 1 until you can chain five perfect dodges in a row. A successful Perfect Dodge grants a 3-second slow-motion window during which you can sprint past the attacker.

PRINCIPLE 4

Skip All Cutscenes

Hold the Options/Menu button during every cutscene to skip it. Some cutscenes have a 2-second minimum before the skip prompt appears, but mashing the button still triggers the skip at the earliest possible frame. Cutscene skipping saves approximately 45 minutes across a full playthrough. The in-game timer pauses during cutscenes — skipping them does not affect your rank time, but it does get you to the end faster in real time if you are streaming or competing.

PRINCIPLE 5

Route Your Inventory in Advance

On NG+ speedruns, arrange your attaché case before starting. Place your primary weapon (fully upgraded shotgun or magnum) in the top-left slot, healing items top-right, and key items in a designated corner. Never spend more than 2 seconds in the inventory during a speedrun. The in-game timer does NOT pause in the inventory screen — every second spent rummaging adds up fast.

CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER SPEEDRUN ROUTES

Below are the fastest routes through every chapter. Target times assume Professional difficulty with a fresh file (no NG+ weapons). NG+ runs with the Rocket Launcher can subtract roughly 20 percent from each target time.

CHAPTER 1 — TARGET: 8 MINUTES
THE AWAKENING
ROUTE: Main hall → Kitchen → East corridor → Armory → Courtyard exit

Key skips: Do not enter the West Wing at all — the drawing room puzzle is optional on repeat playthroughs. Grab the Shotgun from the Armory (mandatory pickup), sprint past all Infected Staff in the east corridor, and head directly to the Courtyard. The Infected Staff in the kitchen can be dodged by hugging the right counter. Save point: Do not waste a save here — Chapter 1 is too short to justify one of your 3 saves.

CHAPTER 2 — TARGET: 12 MINUTES
ECHOES OF THE ESTATE
ROUTE: Courtyard → Hedge maze (right path only) → East garden → Estate cellar → Underground passage

Key skips: The Hedge Maze has two paths — take the right path every time, it is 23 seconds faster than the left. Skip the Gardener's Shed entirely (no save point needed). The Bloated Lycan in the cellar must be killed — unload 4 shotgun rounds into its head at close range; do not waste magnum ammo here. The chapter ends the moment you reach the underground passage door.

CHAPTER 3 — TARGET: 15 MINUTES
BELOW THE SURFACE
ROUTE: Underground passage → Catacombs → Flooded chamber → Lift to Chapter 4

Key skips: After picking up the Lift Key, do NOT explore the side catacomb rooms — they contain only optional treasures. The flooded chamber has three Shadow Wraiths that materialize sequentially — shotgun the first, sprint past the second, flashbang the third. The lift activation cutscene cannot be skipped entirely but can be shortened by holding the confirm button.

CHAPTER 4 — TARGET: 18 MINUTES
THE CRIMSON WING
ROUTE: Main hall → Library → Upper gallery → Ballroom → Exit to Chapter 5

Key skips: The Candelabra puzzle in the main hall can be solved in one pass if you pull both levers simultaneously (stand at the midpoint and quick-turn between them). Skip the Hidden Chapel entirely. The Ballroom fight against the two Armored Lycans is the chapter's only mandatory combat — use 2 magnum rounds on each to end the fight in under 60 seconds.

CHAPTER 5 — TARGET: 22 MINUTES
BETRAYAL IN THE DARK
ROUTE: Estate ruins → Victor Gideon confrontation → Lake path → Chapel → Chapter 6 transition

Key skips: Victor Gideon's dialogue sequence can be shortened by shooting him before he finishes his monologue — this triggers the boss fight early. The Bloated Lycan boss: unload 6 shotgun rounds into the glowing weak point on its back after it charges. The Lake path has 4 Infected Staff — flashbang all of them and sprint through. SAVE HERE (Save 1 of 3).

CHAPTER 6 — TARGET: 20 MINUTES
THE LIVING LABORATORY
ROUTE: Grace Hospital entrance → ER → Morgue → Lab wing → Amalgam boss → Chapter 7

Key skips: Do not enter the Nurse Station — the Morgue Key is not needed. The Morgue drawer puzzle can be brute-forced: drawer 7 is always the correct answer on the second attempt. Amalgam boss: 4 Anti-Materiel Rifle rounds to the central mass, then shotgun the exposed heart. Entire fight under 90 seconds with good aim. Skip all patient rooms on the upper floor.

CHAPTER 7 — TARGET: 10 MINUTES
THE REQUIEM BEGINS
ROUTE: Laboratory entrance → Main lab → Rift chamber → Chapter 8

Key skips: The shortest chapter in the game. Only mandatory objective is activating the Rift stabilizer. The three lab terminals must be activated in order: left, right, center. Sprint past all lab-infected enemies. The Rift chamber cutscene is unskippable but only lasts 90 seconds — use this time to hydrate. No combat required in this chapter if you keep moving.

CHAPTER 8 — TARGET: 25 MINUTES
DIMENSIONS APART
ROUTE: RPD arrival → West office → Armory → Rooftop → Helipad → Return to Rift → Chapter 9

Key skips: Do NOT return to the armory after the Rift stabilizes — this triggers the optional HUNK boss fight that costs 5+ minutes. The RPD West Office has a skip: shoot the window from the hallway, vault through, and bypass the locked door entirely. The Rooftop Shadow Wraith ambush can be neutralized with a flashbang — toss it at the third wraith that spawns, then sprint to the helipad ladder. SAVE HERE (Save 2 of 3).

CHAPTER 9 — TARGET: 22 MINUTES
THE FORGOTTEN CITY
ROUTE: City gates → Central plaza → Museum → Forgotten King arena → Waterfall path → Chapter 10

Key skips: The Central Plaza has 8 enemies — do not engage any of them. Run a serpentine pattern from gate to museum entrance. Forgotten King boss: 6 Anti-Materiel Rifle rounds to the crown (head weak point) while strafing. His charge attack is telegraphed by a 2-second roar — perfect-dodge left. Skip the Museum display cases (optional treasures only).

CHAPTER 10 — TARGET: 18 MINUTES
CONVERGENCE
ROUTE: Convergence Lab → West wing → Central chamber → East wing → Chapter 11

Key skips: The West wing weapons locker (Anti-Materiel Rifle pickup) is worth the 90-second detour if you do not already have it. The Central chamber fight against the dual Abominations: flashbang both, shotgun the left one 4 times, then the right one. The East wing corridor is the most dangerous section — two Shadow Wraiths spawn behind you. Toss a flashbang the moment you hear their spawn audio cue and do not look back.

CHAPTER 11 — TARGET: 20 MINUTES
THE FINAL DESCENT
ROUTE: Descent elevator → Lower catacombs → Extraction point → Amalgam boss arena → Chapter 12

Key skips: The Descent elevator is 45 seconds of unskippable descent — use this time to craft ammo. The Lower Catacombs maze: always turn right at every junction. The extraction point ambush spawns 6 enemies in waves of 2 — shotgun each pair as they appear. SAVE HERE (Save 3 of 3). The Amalgam boss fight is your last checkpoint before the final chapter.

CHAPTER 12 — TARGET: 15 MINUTES
REQUIEM
ROUTE: Final arena → Final Boss (3 phases) → Ending sequence

Key skips: The Final Boss phases: Phase 1 — 8 shotgun rounds to the chest core. Phase 2 — flashbang to interrupt the AoE slam, then 4 Anti-Materiel rounds to the exposed back. Phase 3 — the desperation phase; use all remaining magnum rounds on the head, then finish with the shotgun. If you have the Rocket Launcher (NG+), one rocket per phase ends the fight in under 45 seconds. The credits sequence is unskippable but does not count toward your time.

ADVANCED SKIP TECHNIQUES

These techniques require practice but save enormous amounts of time when executed correctly. The RE Requiem speedrun community discovers new skips regularly — these are the most impactful ones as of July 2026.

SKIP TECHNIQUE
DOOR CANCEL (ALL CHAPTERS)
INPUT: Down + Sprint the frame the door begins to open

Cancels the door-opening animation, allowing you to move through doors approximately 1.2 seconds faster per door. With roughly 80 doors in the game, this skip saves approximately 96 seconds across a full run. Timing is tight — practice on the Chapter 1 main hall doors until consistent.

SKIP TECHNIQUE
LADDER SLIDE CANCEL (CHAPTERS 1, 4, 9, 11)
INPUT: Crouch at the top of any ladder

Cancels the ladder dismount animation. Saves approximately 0.8 seconds per ladder. There are 11 ladders in a full speedrun route, saving roughly 9 seconds total. Minimal but worth mastering — in a game where S+ Professional requires sub-3:30:00, every second counts.

SKIP TECHNIQUE
WINDOW VAULT SKIP (CHAPTER 8)
INPUT: Shoot the RPD West Office window from the hallway, vault through

Bypasses the locked West Office door entirely, saving the 3-minute detour to find the West Office Key. This skip saves approximately 3 minutes and is the single most impactful skip in the entire run. Shoot the window with any weapon — even the starting pistol works — and the vault prompt appears immediately.

SKIP TECHNIQUE
VICTOR GIDEON EARLY TRIGGER (CHAPTER 5)
INPUT: Fire any weapon at Victor Gideon during his monologue

Cuts his 2-minute monologue down to 10 seconds, triggering the boss fight immediately. Aim for his chest — a single pistol round is sufficient. This skip saves roughly 110 seconds. Be ready: the boss fight begins the instant the bullet connects, so have your shotgun equipped before firing.

SKIP TECHNIQUE
INVENTORY PICKUP CANCEL (ALL CHAPTERS)
INPUT: Open inventory the frame the pickup animation starts

Cancels the item-pickup animation. Saves approximately 0.5 seconds per mandatory pickup. With roughly 30 mandatory pickups in a speedrun route, this saves approximately 15 seconds. Easy to learn — just double-tap the inventory button when picking up any item.

PROFESSIONAL S+ STEP-BY-STEP STRATEGY

Earning S+ on Professional difficulty (sub-3:30:00, max 3 saves, 0 deaths) is the holy grail of RE Requiem speedrunning. This section outlines the exact strategy used by top runners.

THE MENTALITY: Professional S+ is a marathon of perfection. Every mistake costs time or a death — and a death means restarting from your last save, which might be an hour behind you. Play conservatively in the first third of the run, then push hard once Save 2 is banked.

Save Strategy

  • Save 1: End of Chapter 5 (roughly 1:15:00 into the run). This is your biggest reset point — if you die in Chapters 1-5, you restart the whole run.
  • Save 2: End of Chapter 8 (roughly 2:15:00 into the run). Chapters 6-8 contain the Amalgam boss and the RPD section, both high-death-risk zones.
  • Save 3: Start of Chapter 11, before the Amalgam boss (roughly 3:00:00 into the run). The final two chapters are the hardest and a death here without a recent save is devastating.

FAQ: RE REQUIEM SPEEDRUNNING

Q1

Does the in-game timer pause during cutscenes?

Yes. The in-game completion timer pauses during all cutscenes, including unskippable ones. This means you can safely watch cutscenes without affecting your rank. However, the real-time clock does not pause, which matters for streaming categories and community leaderboard submissions on sites like speedrun.com.

Q2

Can I get S+ rank on NG+?

Yes, absolutely. NG+ runs have the same rank requirements as fresh-file runs. Using NG+ weapons (Rocket Launcher, Primal Knife, fully upgraded shotgun) makes Professional S+ significantly easier. The only restriction: Professional difficulty must be unlocked first by completing the game on Hardcore. There is no penalty for using NG+ gear in ranked runs.

Q3

What is the current world record for RE Requiem?

As of July 2026, the Any% (Standard difficulty, fresh file) world record stands at approximately 1 hour 38 minutes. The Professional Any% NG+ record is around 1 hour 12 minutes. These times change frequently — check speedrun.com for the latest leaderboards. Our chapter target times above are tuned for sub-3:30:00 Professional S+ rather than world-record pace, making them achievable for intermediate runners.

Q4

Which difficulty should I speedrun first?

Start with Standard difficulty S+ (sub-2:30:00). This teaches routing, skip execution, and time management without the punishing damage of Hardcore or Professional. Once you can consistently clear Standard S+, move to Hardcore S+ (sub-3:00:00), then Professional S+ (sub-3:30:00). Jumping straight to Professional S+ is a recipe for frustration.

Q5

Are there any glitches or out-of-bounds skips?

The RE Requiem speedrun community currently runs the game glitchless — no out-of-bounds clips, no wrong-warps, no sequence breaks. Capcom's RE Engine is famously robust against clipping glitches. The skips documented in this guide all operate within the game's intended mechanics (animation cancels, early triggers, route optimization). If new glitch skips are discovered, speedrun.com typically creates a separate category to preserve the glitchless leaderboard.

Q6

Do I need to collect anything for S+ rank?

No. Rank is determined solely by completion time, save count, and deaths. Collectibles, treasures, side missions, and Lore Fragments do not affect your letter rank. For a speedrun, ignore everything that is not on the critical path. If you want to combine a speedrun with the True Ending requirements, you need all 12 Lore Fragments — that is an entirely different (and much harder) challenge requiring a separate guide.
