Resident Evil Requiem marks the boldest chapter in Capcom's legendary survival horror franchise. With a sprawling narrative that spans decaying mansions, underground laboratories, and nightmarish alternate dimensions, the game demands careful resource management, sharp combat instincts, and a willingness to explore every shadow. This comprehensive walkthrough covers every chapter from the opening hours to the final confrontation, including all boss strategies, puzzle solutions, weapon locations, and hidden collectibles.

Whether you are playing on Standard difficulty for the first time or pushing through Nightmare mode, this guide will ensure you never miss a critical item, never get stuck on a puzzle, and always know the optimal strategy for every encounter. Bookmark this page — you will want to refer back to it often.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN: DIFFICULTY & PREPARATION

Resident Evil Requiem offers four difficulty modes, each significantly altering the experience. Choosing the right one from the start will determine how much you enjoy the game.

DifficultyDescriptionRecommended For
AssistedAuto-aim, generous ammo drops, reduced enemy damage. Aim assist highlights weak points.Story-focused players, first RE game
StandardBalanced experience. Moderate ammo scarcity, fair enemy AI. The intended way to play.Most players, first playthrough
HardcoreScarcer resources, tougher enemies, limited saves (ink ribbons required). Enemy placement changes.RE veterans, survival horror enthusiasts
NightmareUnlocked after first completion. Brutal resource economy, aggressive AI. New enemy variants appear.Challenge seekers, completionists

Our Recommendation: Start on Standard difficulty for your first playthrough. Requiem's adaptive difficulty system will fine-tune the challenge based on your performance, so Standard provides the most consistent experience. Save Hardcore for your second run when you know the layouts and enemy patterns.

Essential Starting Tips

Before diving into the chapters, internalize these core principles that will carry you through the entire game:

  • Conserve ammunition aggressively. Requiem is stingy with ammo. Not every enemy needs to die — sometimes running past a zombie is the smartest play.
  • Explore every room thoroughly. Hidden items, weapon parts, and lore documents are tucked into corners, behind movable shelves, and inside breakable containers.
  • Combine herbs optimally. Green + Red + Yellow is the ultimate combination, providing full healing and a maximum health boost.
  • Upgrade your primary weapon first. Focus damage upgrades on one weapon rather than spreading upgrades thin across multiple guns.
  • Save often. Use typewriters before entering new areas, attempting boss fights, or after finding rare items.
CHAPTERS I-III
The Estate Grounds • Exploration Phase

CHAPTER I — THE ASHEN ARRIVAL

01
Opening Chapter
THE ASHEN ARRIVAL

The game opens with your protagonist arriving at a remote estate on the outskirts of a desolate European town. Rain hammers the cracked cobblestones as you approach the wrought-iron gates. This chapter serves as the game's tutorial, introducing core mechanics without excessive hand-holding.

Key Objectives:

  • Enter the estate grounds through the broken gate (left side, past the collapsed wall)
  • Find the Gate Key in the guardhouse (desk drawer)
  • Collect the Combat Knife from the dead officer near the fountain
  • Enter the main mansion through the front door (requires Gate Key)
  • Find the first Typewriter in the entrance hall (left corridor)

Enemies: This chapter introduces basic Lycans. There are three in the courtyard. Use the environment — the explosive barrel near the well can kill two of them instantly. The third can be dodged entirely by sprinting to the front door after using the key.

Collectibles:

  • Herb (Green) — garden beds, left side of the path
  • Handgun Ammo (x15) — guardhouse, on the shelf
  • File: "Guard's Journal" — guardhouse, on the desk (reveals safe code for Chapter II)
  • Old Pocket Watch (Treasure) — behind the fountain, partially hidden by foliage
02
Mansion Exploration
ECHOES OF THE ESTATE

The mansion interior is a masterclass in atmospheric design. Creaking floorboards, distant moans, and the flicker of candlelight create constant unease. This chapter is exploration-heavy and contains several critical puzzles that gate your progression.

Key Objectives:

  • Explore the ground floor: dining room, kitchen, library, and main hall
  • Solve the Grandfather Clock Puzzle (set to 11:45 to open the secret passage)
  • Find the Handgun in the locked study (code: 1492, found in Guard's Journal)
  • Obtain the West Wing Key from the safe in the master bedroom
  • Access the West Wing and locate the underground passage

Puzzle Solution — Grandfather Clock: Examine the clock in the main hall. You need to set the hands to 11:45. The clue is found in a painting in the dining room — the portrait shows a clock tower with the hands at that position. Setting it correctly opens a hidden panel behind the bookshelf in the library.

Puzzle Solution — Study Safe: The combination 1492 is found in the Guard's Journal from Chapter I. The safe is behind the painting of the knight in the study. Inside you will find the Handgun and some additional ammunition.

Enemies: Five standard zombies roam the mansion. The dining room contains two that burst through the windows — a scripted encounter you cannot avoid. Use the shotgun blast pattern if you have found the weapon, or aim for headshots with the handgun to stagger and run past.

Collectibles:

  • Shotgun Parts (Stock) — kitchen pantry, on the top shelf
  • Herb (Green + Red) — conservatory, planter box
  • File: "The Master's Confession" — master bedroom nightstand
  • Crystal Decanter (Treasure) — dining room display cabinet
  • Wooden Angel Figurine (Treasure) — library, inside the globe
03
Underground
BELOW THE SURFACE

The underground passage beneath the mansion leads to a network of tunnels that once served as smuggling routes. This chapter is tense and linear, with limited resources and some of the game's most claustrophobic encounters.

Key Objectives:

  • Navigate the tunnel system (follow the red markings on the walls)
  • Find the Tunnel Key in the flooded chamber (requires draining the water first)
  • Solve the Water Valve Puzzle (turn valves in order: right, left, center)
  • Defeat the first mini-boss: The Bloated Lycan
  • Reach the underground laboratory entrance
MINI-BOSS: THE BLOATED LYCAN
DIFFICULTY: ★★☆☆☆

This swollen, pulsating creature guards the tunnel exit. It attacks with wide sweeping claws and can vomit acid at range. Maintain medium distance. When it raises both arms for the acid vomit, dodge sideways and unload 3-4 headshots during the recovery. The explosive canisters near the tunnel walls can be shot for massive damage when the boss walks near them. Approximately 20 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun blasts on Standard difficulty.

Collectibles:

  • Handgun Ammo (x20) — smuggler's cache, behind the loose brick (left tunnel)
  • First Aid Med — medical station, right corridor
  • Gunpowder (x2) — scattered in the flooded chamber after draining
  • File: "Smuggler's Ledger" — hidden alcove near the valve room
  • Ruby (Treasure) — embedded in the tunnel wall (shoot to dislodge)
CHAPTERS IV-VI
Crimson Wing • The Laboratory
04
Mansion Revisited
THE CRIMSON WING

After emerging from the tunnels, you return to the mansion — but everything has changed. The Crimson Wing introduces new, more aggressive enemy variants and forces you to re-explore previously visited areas with fresh threats and new item placements.

Key Objectives:

  • Return to the mansion through the east entrance
  • Collect the Shotgun from the armory (now accessible with Tunnel Key)
  • Defeat the Crimson Lycan ambush in the main hall
  • Obtain the Garden Key from the study (newly available area)
  • Access the garden maze and find the path to the greenhouse

The Crimson Lycan Ambush: Entering the main hall triggers a three-wave encounter. Wave one: two standard Lycans from the second-floor balcony. Wave two: a Crimson Lycan (faster, more health) from the west corridor. Wave three: two more from the east. Use the hall's pillars as cover, and prioritize the Crimson Lycan — it can leap across the room in a single bound. The shotgun is your best friend here.

Garden Maze Solution: From the entrance, go: right, left, straight through the hedge gap, left at the statue, right at the fountain. The greenhouse is at the maze's center.

Collectibles:

  • Shotgun Ammo (x8) — armory shelf
  • Herb (Yellow) — greenhouse planter
  • File: "Botanical Research Notes" — greenhouse desk
  • Emerald Brooch (Treasure) — garden maze, hidden behind the statue
05
Plot Twist
BETRAYAL IN THE DARK

This is the game's narrative turning point. An ally reveals their true intentions, and the stakes escalate dramatically. Gameplay shifts from exploration to a more urgent, linear pace as you fight to escape a collapsing section of the estate.

Key Objectives:

  • Survive the ally betrayal cutscene (choices earlier affect dialogue)
  • Escape the collapsing wine cellar before the timer expires (3 minutes on Standard)
  • Find the Emergency Keycard in the rubble
  • Reach the elevator to the underground laboratory

Escape Sequence Tips: The cellar escape is timed. Do not stop to fight enemies — sprint through the debris. The path is linear: follow the lit torches. At the fork, take the left path (the right is a dead end with a treasure — only worth it if you have time). The Emergency Keycard is on a dead soldier halfway through, near the collapsed archway.

Collectibles:

  • Magnum Ammo (x3) — wine cellar, behind the broken barrel (quick grab)
  • File: "Betrayal Confession" — dropped during cutscene, auto-collected
  • Gold Ingot (Treasure) — dead-end right path (high risk during timed sequence)
06
The Laboratory
THE LIVING LABORATORY

The underground laboratory is the game's most complex area, featuring interconnected rooms, security systems, and some of the most disturbing environmental storytelling in the series. This chapter requires backtracking and methodical exploration.

Key Objectives:

  • Activate the laboratory power system (fuse box puzzle)
  • Access the Research Wing using Security Clearance B (found in Director's Office)
  • Solve the Specimen Tank Puzzle (align the colored tubes: Red-Blue-Green-Yellow)
  • Obtain the Master Keycard from the high-security vault
  • Confront the chapter boss: The Abomination

Puzzle Solution — Fuse Box: The lab needs three fuses restored. Fuse A is in the break room (locker code: 2481). Fuse B is in the operating theater (on the surgical tray). Fuse C is in the storage room (inside a locked crate — use the knife to pry it open). Place them in positions A1, B3, and C2.

Puzzle Solution — Specimen Tank: Four tubes need the correct color order. The clue is on the whiteboard in the Research Wing: "The order of creation." Biological samples on the shelves are labeled 1-4: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.

THE ABOMINATION
DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆

Phase 1 (100%-60% HP): Focus on the glowing weak points on its torso. 3-4 shotgun blasts per weak point cause it to stagger, opening a 5-second damage window. Two weak points in this phase.

Phase 2 (60%-30% HP): It grows two additional arms and gains a grab attack. Stay at medium range. When it slams both fists into the ground, dodge the shockwave and fire during recovery. Weak points move to shoulders.

Phase 3 (30%-0% HP): Enraged, faster movement. Explosive canisters spawn around the arena. Lure the boss near them and shoot for massive damage. Two well-placed canister explosions plus sustained fire ends the fight.

Collectibles:

  • Rifle Ammo (x10) — security armory (Master Keycard required)
  • Enhanced Handgun Parts — Director's Office desk
  • File: "Project Requiem: Phase I Report" — high-security vault
  • File: "Director's Personal Log" — Director's Office, hidden compartment
  • Sapphire Pendant (Treasure) — specimen storage, inside a broken tank

Recommended Loadout by Chapter Phase

EARLY GAME

Chapters I – III

Primary: VP-70 Handgun | Secondary: Combat Knife | Items: 2x Green Herbs, all handgun ammo

Focus on headshots to conserve ammunition. Use the knife to finish downed enemies. You will not have the shotgun yet, so distance management is critical.

MID GAME

Chapters IV – VII

Primary: W-870 Shotgun | Secondary: VP-70 Handgun | Items: Mixed herbs, grenade rounds

The shotgun becomes your workhorse. Use it for anything at close range. Save handgun ammo for weaker enemies and environmental interactions.

LATE GAME

Chapters VIII – XII

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle | Secondary: W-870 Shotgun | Reserve: Broken Butterfly (bosses only) | Items: Full herb inventory, all ammo types

By late game, you should have the rifle fully upgraded. Use it for precision shots at range. The shotgun handles anything that gets close. Reserve the magnum exclusively for boss encounters.

Weapon Upgrade Priority

The Merchant offers upgrades at key points. Always prioritize in this order:

  1. Damage — More damage per shot means fewer shots needed, conserving ammo economy
  2. Capacity — Larger magazines reduce reload frequency in intense fights
  3. Reload Speed — Helpful but lower priority than the above
  4. Rate of Fire — Only relevant for the TMP and Rifle
CHAPTERS VII-XII
The Requiem • Final Chapters
07
Act II Begins
THE REQUIEM BEGINS

The narrative takes a dramatic leap forward. You emerge from the laboratory into an entirely new environment — a decaying cathedral town that exists in a dimension overlapping with reality. The atmosphere shifts from scientific horror to supernatural dread.

Key Objectives:

  • Explore the cathedral town and find the Merchant's new shop location
  • Obtain the Cathedral Key from the bell tower
  • Solve the Stained Glass Puzzle (arrange panels to depict the Requiem Mass)
  • Find the three Altar Stones scattered throughout the town
  • Open the path to the dimensional rift

Stained Glass Puzzle: Arrange four stained glass panels in the correct order on the cathedral window. Left to right: Angel, Chalice, Skull, Cross. The clue is in the Priest's quarters — a painting showing the stages of the Requiem Mass ceremony.

Altar Stone Locations:

  • Stone of Grief — Cemetery, inside the open mausoleum (defeat the two Lycan Guardians first)
  • Stone of Penance — Confessional booth in the chapel (requires Priest's Key from the rectory)
  • Stone of Passage — Bell tower summit (platforming section, watch for crumbling ledges)

Collectibles:

  • Rifle (Semi-Auto) — bell tower armory chest
  • File: "The Requiem Mass: An Account" — cathedral altar
  • File: "Town Records, Entry 47" — town hall desk
  • Gold Chalice (Treasure) — cathedral sacristy, locked cabinet
  • Herb (Green + Red + Yellow) — cemetery, behind the angel statue
08
Dimensional Rift
DIMENSIONS APART

Stepping through the rift transports you to a nightmarish mirror version of the estate. Gravity shifts, architecture distorts, and new enemy types emerge from the walls themselves. This chapter tests everything you have learned.

Key Objectives:

  • Navigate the twisted mirror-estate (layout is inverted from Chapter II)
  • Defeat the Shadow Stalker mini-boss in the main hall
  • Find the Dimensional Anchor to stabilize your presence
  • Solve the Inversion Puzzle (operate mechanisms in the correct reversed order)
SHADOW STALKER
DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆

This enemy phases in and out of visibility. When invisible, track it by the faint red glow and the sound of its breathing. Wait for it to materialize before attacking. The shotgun is most effective — two close-range blasts force it to retreat and reappear elsewhere. Approximately 12 shotgun rounds total to defeat.

Inversion Puzzle: The puzzle room has four levers. In the normal world, you pull them left-to-right (1-2-3-4). In the mirror dimension, pull them in reverse: 4-3-2-1, with a 2-second pause between each. Pulling too quickly resets the puzzle.

09
Open World Segment
THE FORGOTTEN CITY

The Forgotten City is the game's largest explorable area, featuring interconnected streets, crumbling buildings, and numerous optional encounters. This is where completionists will spend the most time.

Key Objectives:

  • Map the city using the Cartographer's Notes (found in the watchtower)
  • Locate and activate four City Beacons to reveal the path to the inner sanctum
  • Defeat optional boss: The Forgotten King (rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum)
  • Collect all six Lore Fragments to unlock the secret ending path

Beacon Locations: North Plaza (rooftop), East Market (underground cellar), South Gate (clock tower), West Quarter (collapsed church). Each beacon is guarded by a mini-boss encounter.

OPTIONAL: THE FORGOTTEN KING
DIFFICULTY: ★★★★☆

A towering armored figure wielding a massive halberd. His halberd sweep covers 180 degrees — stay behind him. When he plants the halberd for a shockwave slam, that is your window. Use the rifle to target the weak point on his back. Rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum and a large treasure.

10
Convergence
CONVERGENCE

The dimensional barriers begin to collapse. Both worlds merge into a single nightmarish landscape. This chapter is a gauntlet of combat encounters with minimal exploration, building toward the finale.

Key Objectives:

  • Fight through three consecutive arena encounters (rest between each in safe rooms)
  • Protect the NPC ally during the bridge defense sequence (4 minutes)
  • Reach the Convergence Point and prepare for the final act

Bridge Defense Tips: Stock up on shotgun ammo and grenades before this sequence. Enemies attack from both sides. Place proximity mines at both entrances if available. Focus fire on Crimson Lycans first — they deal the most damage to your NPC ally. If the ally's health drops to zero, you restart the sequence.

11
The Descent
THE FINAL DESCENT

This chapter descends into the heart of the dimensional rift. The environment is surreal and constantly shifting. New, terrifying enemy types appear for the first time, and the game's resource economy reaches its tightest point.

Key Objectives:

  • Navigate the shifting corridors (follow the red light to avoid dead ends)
  • Defeat the Amalgam boss in the central chamber
  • Recover the Requiem Key from the boss arena
  • Make final preparations before the endgame
THE AMALGAM
DIFFICULTY: ★★★★☆

A horrific fusion of every major enemy type you have faced. It cycles through forms, each mimicking a previous boss's attack patterns.

Lycan Form: Fast melee attacks. Keep distance, use rifle for precision shots on the glowing core.

Abomination Form: Slow but devastating. Tentacle attacks cover wide areas. Use pillars for cover and fire during recovery.

Shadow Form: Phases in and out. Track by red glow. Shotgun when visible, reposition when invisible.

Final Form (25% HP): Combines all three. Focus everything — magnum rounds, grenades, shotgun blasts. Two explosive canisters respawn every 30 seconds. Use them strategically.

12
Finale
REQUIEM

The final chapter. Everything leads to this confrontation.

Pre-Fight Checklist:

  • Full health (use all remaining herbs)
  • Maximum ammunition for every weapon type
  • At least 3 First Aid Meds
  • All weapons fully upgraded (visit the Merchant one final time)

The final boss has multiple phases that mirror the game's entire arc. Each phase escalates in intensity, requiring different weapon strategies. The arena provides ammunition and health pickups between phases — collect them all before the next phase begins.

ALL COLLECTIBLES & SECRETS

Resident Evil Requiem features several categories of collectibles. Finding them all unlocks bonus content and is required for the secret ending.

Files & Documents (47 total)

Files are scattered throughout every chapter. They provide lore context and often contain puzzle clues. Finding all of them unlocks the "Scholar" achievement and a bonus epilogue scene.

Treasures (32 total)

Treasure items can be sold to the Merchant for currency. Some can be combined for higher value:

  • Ruby + Silver Ring = Ruby Ring (3x value)
  • Emerald + Gold Ingot = Emerald Brooch (5x value)
  • Sapphire + Silver Pendant = Sapphire Pendant (4x value)

Lore Fragments (6 total)

The six Lore Fragments are hidden across Chapters VII-XI. Collecting all six unlocks the secret ending path in Chapter XII. They appear as glowing red crystals and require solving optional puzzles or defeating optional encounters to obtain.

Weapon Parts (14 total)

Weapon modification parts enhance specific weapon attributes beyond the Merchant's standard upgrades. They are hidden in hard-to-reach locations and often require backtracking with new key items.

Completionist Note: 100% completion requires at least two playthroughs — some collectibles are locked behind difficulty-gated content in Nightmare mode. The secret ending requires all 6 Lore Fragments and all 47 Files collected in a single save file.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem is a monumental achievement in survival horror. Its twelve chapters offer roughly 20-25 hours of content on a first playthrough, with significant replay value through difficulty modes, collectible hunting, and multiple endings. The game rewards patience, observation, and strategic resource management above all else.

The key to success is never feeling rushed. Take your time in every new environment. Check every corner, read every document, and listen to the audio cues. The game communicates everything you need to survive — you just have to pay attention.
