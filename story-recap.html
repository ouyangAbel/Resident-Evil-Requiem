THE PREMISE: WHAT IS RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM ABOUT?

Resident Evil Requiem takes place in 2026, roughly four years after the events of Resident Evil Village. The story follows Leon S. Kennedy, now a veteran DSO agent in his late 40s, and Grace Ashcroft, a young trauma surgeon at Grace Hospital in a remote English town. The narrative weaves between two parallel storylines — Leon's covert investigation of a bioweapons laboratory hidden beneath an abandoned Victorian estate, and Grace's desperate fight for survival when her hospital is overrun by grotesque infected creatures.

The game's title, "Requiem," refers to the central mystery: a project code-named "Project Requiem" — a bioweapons research initiative that attempted to fuse the Las Plagas parasite with a newly discovered dimensional rift technology. The result is a catastrophe that threatens not just one world, but the fabric of reality itself. The game explores themes of grief, redemption, and the cost of survival — earning its somber name.

QUICK CONTEXT: You do not need to have played every Resident Evil game to understand Requiem. The game provides enough exposition to stand on its own. However, familiarity with Leon's history (RE2, RE4), the Las Plagas parasite (RE4), and the Mold (RE7, Village) enriches the experience significantly. The story also features a major cameo from a fan-favorite character — more on that below.

THE COMPLETE STORY TIMELINE

Below is the full Resident Evil Requiem story, organized chapter by chapter. This section contains complete spoilers — proceed only if you have finished the game or do not mind full narrative disclosure.

PROLOGUE — BEFORE THE GAME
PROJECT REQUIEM: ORIGINS
TIMELINE: 2020–2025

After the fall of the Connections in 2021, scattered bioweapons research continued in secret. Victor Gideon, a former Umbrella Corporation senior virologist who had evaded the BSAA for decades, established a clandestine laboratory beneath Rookwood Estate in the English countryside. His project combined Las Plagas specimens recovered from Spain with a newly discovered Dimensional Rift — a naturally occurring tear in spacetime beneath the estate, dating back to medieval times. Gideon believed that merging biological parasites with transdimensional energy could create "perfect organisms" immune to all disease and death. By 2025, the project was fully operational, and the first rift-born creatures began to emerge.

CHAPTERS 1–3 — LEON'S INVESTIGATION
THE AWAKENING, ECHOES OF THE ESTATE & BELOW THE SURFACE
LOCATION: Rookwood Estate & Underground Catacombs

Leon S. Kennedy is dispatched to Rookwood Estate after the DSO intercepts a distress signal from Dr. Elena Voss, a Project Requiem researcher. Leon arrives to find the estate in chaos — staff transformed into Infected, half-human creatures with Plagas-like parasites controlling their nervous systems. In the catacombs beneath the estate, Leon discovers the first evidence of the Dimensional Rift: a pulsating gateway that emits a low-frequency hum and warps the architecture around it. He also encounters HUNK for the first time in years, who saves Leon from a Bloated Lycan ambush before disappearing without explanation. HUNK's presence raises the central question: is he here to destroy Project Requiem, or to secure its research for a new employer?

CHAPTERS 4–5 — GRACE'S OUTBREAK & THE BETRAYAL
THE CRIMSON WING & BETRAYAL IN THE DARK
LOCATION: Grace Hospital & Estate Ruins

The narrative shifts to Grace Ashcroft at Grace Hospital, where a sudden outbreak transforms patients and staff into Plagas-infected horrors. Grace fights through the hospital using her medical knowledge to treat victims and her surgical precision to dispatch enemies. Her chapters reveal that the outbreak originated from a patient — a Rookwood Estate worker who had been bitten and transferred to the hospital before symptoms manifested. Midway through, Grace discovers that Victor Gideon intentionally allowed the outbreak to spread as a "field test" for his latest strain. Meanwhile, Leon confronts Gideon at the estate ruins, where Gideon reveals his philosophy: humanity is weak, and dimensional merging is the only path to evolution. The confrontation ends with Gideon injecting himself with a refined Plagas-Rift hybrid, transforming into a monstrous form and retreating into the Rift.

CHAPTERS 6–8 — THE LABORATORY & THE RIFT
THE LIVING LABORATORY, THE REQUIEM BEGINS & DIMENSIONS APART
LOCATION: Underground Laboratory & RPD Dimension

Leon breaches the underground laboratory, where Elena Voss reveals the full scope of Project Requiem. The Rift is not just a portal — it is a consciousness bridge between dimensions. Gideon's experiments have already pulled entities from a parallel reality into ours, including the Forgotten King, a tyrannical ruler from a medieval dimension where the Plagas parasite evolved unchecked for centuries. Leon and Grace's storylines converge when the Rift destabilizes, pulling Grace from the hospital into the laboratory. The two must work together, alternating between Leon's combat expertise and Grace's medical and puzzle-solving skills. Chapter 8 sees Leon pulled through the Rift into a twisted mirror of the Raccoon City Police Department, where he relives fragments of his trauma from RE2. This chapter provides crucial character development: Leon confronts the ghosts of his past and resolves to stop Gideon not out of duty, but out of a genuine desire to protect Grace and prevent another Raccoon City.

CHAPTERS 9–10 — THE FORGOTTEN CITY
THE FORGOTTEN CITY & CONVERGENCE
LOCATION: The Forgotten City (Rift Dimension)

Leon and Grace enter the Rift dimension and find themselves in the Forgotten City, a decaying medieval metropolis ruled by the Forgotten King. Here they learn the Rift's true origin: it was created centuries ago by an alchemist who attempted to reach "the world beyond death." The Forgotten King was pulled through and has been waiting for another rift event to expand his kingdom into new dimensions. Gideon, now fully transformed by his self-injection, has allied with the King — promising to open stable portals to Earth in exchange for the King's power. Chapter 10 reveals HUNK's true agenda: he was sent not by any corporation, but by a clandestine international organization that has been monitoring dimensional anomalies since the 1970s. His mission is to collapse the Rift permanently, even if it means sacrificing everyone trapped inside. Leon and Grace must choose: trust HUNK's plan to close the Rift from the inside, or find another way home.

CHAPTERS 11–12 — THE FINAL CONFRONTATION
THE FINAL DESCENT & REQUIEM
LOCATION: The Abyss (Rift Core) & Final Arena

The final two chapters are a gauntlet. Leon, Grace, and HUNK descend into the Rift's core to confront Gideon and the Forgotten King. Chapter 11 features the Amalgam boss fight — Gideon's final monstrous form, a fusion of human, Plagas, and rift energy. The fight requires alternating between Leon's heavy weapons and Grace's ability to disable the rift conduits powering Gideon's regeneration. In Chapter 12, the Forgotten King emerges for the final battle — a three-phase encounter that is arguably the most cinematic boss fight in Resident Evil history. The outcome depends on choices made throughout the game and which collectibles were gathered (see our Endings Explained guide). After the King's defeat, the Rift begins to collapse. HUNK stays behind to manually trigger the collapse sequence, giving Leon and Grace time to escape. His final words: "Some debts can only be paid in blood."

MAJOR PLOT TWISTS AND REVELATIONS

Resident Evil Requiem is packed with narrative surprises. Here are the most significant twists, ranked by impact.

TWIST 1

HUNK's True Allegiance

The game's biggest reveal: HUNK works for the Global Anomaly Response Division (GARD), a secret multinational organization founded in 1973 after the first documented dimensional event. GARD has been quietly containing rift incidents for 50 years. HUNK's decades of work for Umbrella, the Connections, and various bioweapons groups were all deep-cover missions aimed at monitoring organizations that might stumble upon dimensional technology. His entire career has been a long con — and Leon realizing this reshapes everything we thought we knew about the character.

TWIST 2

The Rift Is Sentient

Elena Voss discovers that the Dimensional Rift is not merely a tear in spacetime — it is a living consciousness. The Rift has been "calling" to people across dimensions for centuries, manipulating events to bring more beings into its domain. Gideon did not choose the Rookwood Estate — the Rift chose Gideon. Every infection, every outbreak, every death feeds the Rift's growth. The Forgotten King is not the Rift's master; he is its most successful pawn.

TWIST 3

Grace's Connection to Project Requiem

Late in the game, Grace discovers that her late father, Dr. Marcus Ashcroft, was one of the original Project Requiem researchers before his death in a laboratory accident 15 years prior. Her medical training, her assignment to the hospital near the estate, and even her survival instincts during the outbreak were all, in some sense, preordained by her father's unfinished work. Grace must confront the possibility that her entire life has been shaped by the same project she is now trying to destroy.

TWIST 4

The Nurse Scene: Leon's Breaking Point

The infamous scene where Leon watches a nurse die — analyzed in depth in our Leon Nurse Scene Explained guide — is not about the nurse. It is about Ada Wong. The nurse's death triggers Leon's suppressed grief over Ada's fate (ambiguous since RE6), and his momentary paralysis represents his deepest fear: that he can never save anyone. The scene frames his entire arc: Leon must learn that survival is not failure, and that allowing himself to care about Grace is not a weakness. UNANSWERED QUESTIONS AND LORE MYSTERIES

Even after all three endings, Resident Evil Requiem leaves several threads dangling. Here are the biggest unanswered questions, along with community theories.

  • Who leads GARD? The Global Anomaly Response Division is only glimpsed through HUNK's flashbacks. Its leadership remains unidentified, though a brief shot of a silhouetted figure in the Good Ending's post-credits scene has fans speculating it could be an aged Jill Valentine or a returning Chris Redfield.
  • Is Ada Wong alive? Leon's memories of Ada are referenced multiple times but never resolved. The game hints that Ada may have her own connection to GARD — a possible setup for a DLC expansion or sequel.
  • What is on the Moon? The True Ending's lunar reveal is the biggest sequel hook. Dataminers have found references to a "Lunar Requiem" project in the game files, suggesting Capcom has already planned the next chapter.
  • Does the Mold (RE7/Village) connect to the Rift? Elena Voss's research notes mention that the Mold's regenerative properties share "structural similarities" with rift energy. This implies a previously unknown link between the Mold, the Plagas, and dimensional anomalies — potentially uniting all RE biohazards under a single cosmic origin.
  • What happened to the Connections? The global crime syndicate from RE7 and Village is mentioned only in passing. Their absence suggests either they were dismantled, or they have gone so deep underground that even GARD cannot find them.

FAQ: RE REQUIEM STORY AND LORE

Q1

Do I need to play previous Resident Evil games to understand Requiem's story?

No, but it helps. Requiem provides enough in-game exposition to be enjoyed standalone. However, Leon's character arc is significantly richer if you know his history from RE2 and RE4. The game also contains numerous Easter eggs and references that reward longtime fans. For newcomers, our Beginner Guide covers everything you need before starting.

Q2

Is the Dimensional Rift canon to the Resident Evil universe?

Yes, fully canon. Capcom has confirmed in developer interviews that the dimensional rift storyline is the "next evolution" of the Resident Evil mythology, expanding beyond biological horror into cosmic and interdimensional territory. The Rift does not contradict previous lore — it ties together previously disparate threads (Las Plagas, the Mold, the Progenitor virus) under a unified origin.

Q3

Who is the mysterious figure in the True Ending post-credits scene?

Capcom has not confirmed the identity. The most popular community theory is Ada Wong, given her history with both Leon and secret organizations. Other theories include Jill Valentine (who has experience with mind-control and parasites from RE5) and a brand-new character for the sequel. The figure's silhouette is deliberately androgynous, and the voice line — "It's time to go home" — could apply to multiple returning characters.

Q4

Does HUNK die in any ending?

In the Standard and Bad endings, HUNK is presumed dead but his body is never shown — a classic Resident Evil trope that leaves the door open. In the Good and True Endings, he explicitly survives. Given that HUNK has "died" in multiple previous games only to return, fans should assume he will appear again regardless of which ending is declared canon.

Q5

Is there going to be a Resident Evil Requiem sequel?

Capcom has not officially announced a sequel, but the True Ending is an unambiguous sequel hook. Industry insiders report that a follow-up — tentatively titled "Resident Evil: Lunar Requiem" — has entered pre-production. Given RE Requiem's critical and commercial success (9.4 Metacritic, 3.3 million monthly searches), a direct sequel is almost certain.

Q6

What is the significance of the Golden Egg quest?

The Golden Egg is a reference to Resident Evil 4, where a golden chicken egg could be found in the village. In Requiem, the Golden Egg quest is a multi-chapter secret quest chain that reveals additional lore about the medieval alchemist who first opened the Rift. Completing the quest is required for the True Ending. See our Secrets Guide for the full quest walkthrough.

THE BIG PICTURE: WHAT REQUIEM MEANS FOR RESIDENT EVIL

Resident Evil Requiem is more than just another entry in the series — it is a pivot point. By introducing dimensional rifts as a canonical phenomenon tied to the biohazard outbreaks, Capcom has created a narrative framework that allows for virtually limitless storytelling possibilities. The franchise can now explore alternate timelines, parallel dimensions, and cosmic-scale threats without breaking established lore. The grounded survival horror of the Spencer Mansion has evolved — over 30 years — into something far stranger and more ambitious.

The game's thematic core remains unmistakably Resident Evil: ordinary people facing extraordinary horror, the corrupting influence of power, and the question of what it means to be human when humanity itself is under siege. Leon and Grace's story is a requiem in the truest sense — a lament for the dead, and a hymn for those who survive. Whether you are here for the scares, the lore, or the characters, RE Requiem delivers on every front.
