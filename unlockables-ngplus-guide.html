So you have finished Resident Evil Requiem and the credits are rolling. Before you close the game, take a moment to appreciate what you have accomplished — because now the real game begins. WHAT IS NEW GAME PLUS IN RE REQUIEM?

New Game Plus (NG+) unlocks automatically after you complete the main story on any difficulty. When you start a fresh playthrough from the main menu, you can select "New Game Plus" and carry over most of your progress from the previous save file. NG+ is completely optional — you can also start a brand new game without bonuses if you prefer the vanilla experience.

How to Start New Game Plus

  1. Complete the story on any difficulty. Save your cleared file.
  2. From the main menu, select New Game.
  3. Choose "New Game Plus" from the mode selection screen.
  4. Select your cleared save file as the data source.
  5. Choose your difficulty for the NG+ run.

Note: You can replay NG+ multiple times. Each successive playthrough stacks your carry-over progress, so completing NG+ on Hardcore and then starting another NG+ on Professional will carry everything from both runs.

BONUS WEAPONS UNLOCK GUIDE

One of the biggest rewards of NG+ is access to exclusive bonus weapons. Some unlock automatically based on difficulty completion, while others require specific achievements or collectible milestones.

TIER 1 BONUS
SAMURAI EDGE — LEON'S CUSTOM PISTOL
Unlock: Complete Standard difficulty

Cost: 5,000 RP in Bonus Shop. A custom high-damage handgun with faster reload and improved critical hit rate. Available immediately in any NG+ run. Pairs excellently with handgun ammo crafting for efficient enemy clearing.

TIER 2 BONUS
CHICAGO SWEEPER — TYPEWRITER SMG
Unlock: Complete Hardcore difficulty

Cost: 8,000 RP in Bonus Shop. A high-capacity submachine gun with excellent rate of fire. Devours handgun ammo but clears rooms faster than any other non-magnum weapon. The definitive crowd-control tool for higher difficulties.

TIER 3 BONUS
LE-5 SUBMACHINE GUN
Unlock: NG+ exclusive — Chapter IV locked container

Cost: Free (in-game find). Found only in New Game Plus. Located in the Crimson Wing armory safe (code: 04-21-92). Uses rifle ammo, making it a unique weapon that bridges SMG fire rate with rifle damage output.

TIER 4 BONUS
INFINITE ROCKET LAUNCHER
Unlock: Complete Professional difficulty

Cost: 15,000 RP in Bonus Shop. The ultimate power fantasy. One-shot kills almost everything in the game. Does not work on scripted boss phases but trivializes every other encounter. Must be equipped in your inventory and can be unequipped when you want a fair fight.

SECRET WEAPON
EXPERIMENTAL RAILGUN
Unlock: Find all 14 weapon parts across any playthrough

Cost: Included with weapon part collection payoff. Once you have collected all 14 weapon parts across any combination of playthroughs, the Railgun becomes available at every workbench. Requires the Railgun Battery from Chapter IX. Deals massive single-target damage with piercing properties.

INFINITE AMMO UNLOCK

Infinite ammo is the holy grail for RE speedrunners and completionists. Unlike some previous RE games where infinite ammo was tied exclusively to difficulty completion, RE Requiem uses a Bonus Shop system where you purchase weapon-specific infinite ammo unlocks using Requiem Points (RP).

HUNK & SECRET CHARACTERS

Beyond Leon and Grace costumes, there is one major unlockable character in RE Requiem.

SECRET CHARACTER
HUNK — THE GRIM REAPER
Unlock: Complete Professional difficulty with S+ rank

How it works: HUNK becomes playable in all chapters once unlocked. He has his own unique weapon set (MP5 and combat knife), cannot use herbs or healing items (passive health regeneration instead), and has a unique dodge mechanic with faster recovery. HUNK mode is widely considered the ultimate challenge in RE Requiem — and the ultimate reward for mastering the game.

NEW GAME PLUS EXCLUSIVE CONTENT

NG+ is not just about carrying over your gear. Several areas and items are completely inaccessible in a first playthrough and only appear in NG+.

  • New enemy variants: Crimson Molded (faster, deadlier versions of standard Molded) appear in random locations throughout NG+. They drop rare crafting materials.
  • Bonus Shop items: Concept art gallery, 3D model viewer, developer commentary tracks — all purchasable with RP.
  • LE-5 SMG: The only weapon exclusive to NG+, found in Chapter IV armory safe.
  • Plague Doctor shrine hunt: 12 hidden shrines appear only in NG+ runs. Destroying all unlocks Leon's Plague Doctor costume.
  • NG+ only locked chests: Certain treasure chests in chapters I, V, and X require NG+ status to open. They contain large RP payouts and rare weapon attachments.
  • Grace Laboratory coat: Only obtainable by accessing the Chapter VI secret terminal during NG+.

REQUIEM POINTS FARMING GUIDE

Requiem Points (RP) are the currency for the Bonus Shop. Everything from infinite ammo to concept art costs RP. Here is how to earn them fast:

FARM METHOD 1

Challenge Milestones

Challenge progress carries across all playthroughs. Prioritize: Speedrun under 4 hours (3,000 RP), Kill 500 enemies (4,000 RP), No recovery item run (5,000 RP), Find all Lore Fragments (6,000 RP). Stack multiple challenges in one run for maximum efficiency.

FARM METHOD 2

NG+ Exclusive Chests

The three NG+ locked chests contain 2,000 RP each. Found in Chapter I (Estate basement), Chapter V (Research lab safe), and Chapter X (Dimensional rift treasure room).

FARM METHOD 3

Collectible Milestones

Every 10 Lore Fragments collected grants a bonus RP payout. Complete collections also grant large one-time bonuses. Use our Mission List Guide to track all Lore Fragment locations.

FARM METHOD 4

Difficulty Completion Bonuses

Completing Standard grants 5,000 RP. Hardcore grants 10,000 RP. Professional grants 20,000 RP. These stack — completing Professional also retroactively awards the Standard and Hardcore bonuses (total 35,000 RP).

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

Can you unlock everything in a single NG+ run?

No. Some unlocks require specific difficulty completions (Professional for Infinite Rocket Launcher, S+ rank for HUNK). Plan for at least 3-4 playthroughs to unlock everything.

Q2

Do collectibles carry over between NG+ runs?

Yes. Lore Fragments, file documents, and photographs carry over. You only need to find each collectible once across all playthroughs to complete the collection.

Q3

Can I play NG+ on a lower difficulty?

Yes. You can select any difficulty you have unlocked for your NG+ run. Playing NG+ on Standard with your fully upgraded gear makes for a cathartic power fantasy.

Q4

Does using infinite ammo prevent achievements?

No. Infinite ammo does not disable trophies or achievements in RE Requiem. The only unlock that blocks Professional S+ rank is using the Rocket Launcher (it disables rank tracking). Use infinite SMG or handgun freely.

Q5

How long is a typical NG+ run?

A focused NG+ speedrun with max gear takes 2-3 hours. A thorough NG+ completionist run (all collectibles, all exclusive chests) takes 5-7 hours.

Q6

Can I replay individual chapters in NG+?

Yes. Chapter Select mode is available with your NG+ gear after completing the game at least once. Your inventory saves when you complete a chapter in Chapter Select mode.
