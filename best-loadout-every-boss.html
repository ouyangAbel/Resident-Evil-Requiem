RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM BEST LOADOUT FOR EVERY BOSS: OPTIMAL GEAR GUIDE

Boss fights in Resident Evil Requiem are the ultimate test of your preparation. Unlike standard encounters where you can scrape by with whatever you have, bosses demand the right weapons, the right items, and the right strategy. A poorly equipped run can turn a manageable fight into a nightmare of wasted ammo and repeated deaths. This guide covers the best loadout for every boss in the game, from the early Bloated Lycan to the apocalyptic Final Boss.

GENERAL BOSS FIGHT PREPARATION

Before diving into specific boss loadouts, there are universal rules that apply to every boss encounter in RE Requiem. Follow these fundamentals and you will enter every arena with a significant advantage.

Universal Rule: Every boss arena has a storage crate and Merchant nearby before the entrance. Always restock, reorganize your inventory, and craft extra ammunition before triggering the boss cutscene. You cannot access the Merchant mid-fight.

The Golden Boss Loadout Formula

Every boss loadout follows the same structural template. Adapt the specific weapons and items to the boss at hand:

  • Primary Weapon: Your highest-damage ranged weapon (Rifle or Magnum depending on availability)
  • Secondary Weapon: A close-range option for when bosses rush you (Shotgun or SMG)
  • Melee/Explosives: Combat Knife + 2-4 Grenades (type varies by boss)
  • Healing: 3-4 First Aid Meds + full herb inventory
  • Ammo: Maximum carry capacity for your primary weapon type
TIP 01

Upgrade Capacity Before Boss Fights

The Capacity upgrade automatically refills your magazine when purchased. If you are low on ammo before a boss, buy the Capacity upgrade at the Merchant — it gives you a free reload worth of ammunition even from empty. This can add 10-15 free rounds to your reserves.

BLOATED LYCAN (CHAPTER III)

The Bloated Lycan is your first major boss fight and serves as a skill check for the rest of the game. It is a massive werewolf-like creature that charges, swipes, and leaps across the arena. Its weak point is the glowing cyst on its back, which is only exposed after a charge attack.

CHAPTER III BOSS
BLOATED LYCAN LOADOUT

Primary: W-870 Shotgun | Secondary: SG-09 R Handgun | Explosives: 2 Hand Grenades | Healing: 2 First Aid Meds, 3 Mixed Herbs

Strategy: Wait for the Lycan to charge. Dodge sideways, then blast the exposed cyst on its back with the shotgun. Two to three shots per opening is optimal. Use the handgun for consistent damage when the cyst is not exposed. Save grenades for the final phase when it becomes more aggressive.

Difficulty Note: On Hardcore difficulty, the Bloated Lycan's cyst takes 50% more damage to stagger. Prioritize the W-870's Power upgrade before this fight if possible.

THE ABOMINATION (CHAPTER VI)

The Abomination is a hulking Umbrella creation that serves as the Chapter VI finale. It has three phases: a ranged acid-spitting phase, a melee slam phase, and a berserk finale. Its weak points are its eyes and the exposed spine when it rears back.

CHAPTER VI BOSS
ABOMINATION LOADOUT

Primary: SR M1903 Rifle | Secondary: W-870 Shotgun | Explosives: 2 Incendiary Grenades, 1 Flash Grenade | Healing: 3 First Aid Meds, 4 Mixed Herbs

Strategy: The rifle is king here. Aim for the eyes during the ranged phase — headshots deal 2x damage. When it rears back for a slam, shoot the exposed spine for massive stagger. Incendiary grenades are devastating during the berserk phase, dealing continuous burn damage while you reload.

TIP 02

Incendiary Grenade Timing

Throw Incendiary grenades at the Abomination's feet during its berserk phase. The fire pool persists for 8 seconds and deals damage every time the boss walks through it. Combined with rifle headshots, this melts its health bar.

SHADOW STALKER (CHAPTER VIII)

The Shadow Stalker is the most unique boss in the game. It is a semi-invisible entity that phases in and out of reality, attacking from darkness. It can only be damaged when fully materialized, which happens after it attacks or when hit by a Flash grenade.

CHAPTER VIII BOSS
SHADOW STALKER LOADOUT

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle | Secondary: Striker Shotgun | Explosives: 4 Flash Grenades, 2 Hand Grenades | Healing: 3 First Aid Meds, 3 Mixed Herbs

Strategy: Flash grenades are mandatory. They force the Shadow Stalker to fully materialize for 4 seconds. When it appears, unload with the Semi-Auto Rifle. The Striker is your backup if it materializes close to you. Never waste ammo firing at its shadow form — it takes zero damage.

Critical Tip: Craft extra Flash Grenades before this fight. You need at least 4 for the full encounter. If you run out, the boss becomes nearly impossible to damage consistently. Bring all the Gunpowder you can carry.

THE FORGOTTEN KING (CHAPTER IX, OPTIONAL)

The Forgotten King is an optional boss that rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum upon defeat. It is a regal, armored entity wielding a massive sword. Its weak point is the glowing core on its back, which is only exposed after a spinning slash attack.

CHAPTER IX OPTIONAL BOSS
FORGOTTEN KING LOADOUT

Primary: SR M1903 Rifle | Secondary: W-870 Shotgun | Explosives: 3 Hand Grenades | Healing: 3 First Aid Meds, 4 Mixed Herbs

Strategy: This is a patience fight. The Forgotten King has a limited set of attacks. After its spinning slash, it is stunned for 3 seconds — run behind it and blast the core with the shotgun. The rifle is for consistent damage during its recovery animations. Hand grenades are best saved for its second phase when it summons adds.

Reward: Defeating The Forgotten King grants the Broken Butterfly Magnum for free. If you skip this fight, you must wait until Chapter X to purchase it from the Merchant for 78,000 Pesetas.

THE AMALGAM (CHAPTER XI)

The Amalgam is a nightmarish fusion of multiple creatures, serving as the penultimate boss. It has four distinct attack patterns and shifts between them unpredictably. Its weak points are the multiple faces embedded in its body, each with its own health pool.

CHAPTER XI BOSS
AMALGAM LOADOUT

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle (with piercing upgrade) | Secondary: Broken Butterfly Magnum | Explosives: 2 Incendiary Grenades, 2 Flash Grenades | Healing: 4 First Aid Meds, 5 Mixed Herbs

Strategy: The Semi-Auto Rifle's piercing upgrade is game-changing here — bullets pass through multiple faces, dealing damage to several weak points simultaneously. Save the Broken Butterfly for when multiple faces are exposed at once. Incendiary grenades deal passive damage while you focus on dodging its complex attack patterns.

TIP 03

Face Priority Order

Destroy the faces in this order: Top (ranged attacks) → Left (grab attack) → Right (charge attack) → Center (final phase). Eliminating the top face first removes the most dangerous ranged attack, making the rest of the fight significantly easier.

THE FINAL BOSS (CHAPTER XII)

The Final Boss is a multi-phase epic that tests everything you have learned. It has five distinct phases, each with unique attack patterns and weak points. This is the fight where all your preparation pays off. There are no second chances — if you die, you restart the entire 20-minute encounter.

CHAPTER XII FINAL BOSS
FINAL BOSS LOADOUT

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle (fully upgraded, piercing) | Secondary: Broken Butterfly Magnum | Emergency: Railgun | Explosives: 3 Incendiary, 3 Flash, 2 Hand Grenades | Healing: 5 First Aid Meds, 6 Mixed Herbs

Phase 1-2: Use the Semi-Auto Rifle. Aim for the glowing chest core. Dodge the horizontal slashes by rolling sideways.

Phase 3: The boss becomes airborne. Use the Broken Butterfly Magnum — two shots bring it back to the ground. Save at least 4 Magnum rounds for this phase.

Phase 4: The arena shrinks. Use Incendiary grenades to create safe zones. The Striker shotgun is excellent here for close-range burst damage.

Phase 5: Unload everything. The Railgun can be saved for the final vulnerability window — one shot deals 10,000 damage. Combine with a Flash grenade for a guaranteed hit.

Final Boss Pro Tip: Craft 10+ Magnum ammo and 20+ Rifle ammo before this fight. The Merchant before the Final Boss sells all crafting materials. Spend every Peseta you have — leftover money is useless after the credits roll.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the best overall weapon for boss fights in RE Requiem?

The Broken Butterfly Magnum deals the highest single-target damage and is the best boss-killing weapon. However, its ammo is extremely scarce. For sustained DPS, the Semi-Auto Rifle with its piercing upgrade is the best all-rounder, especially against multi-weakpoint bosses like the Amalgam.

Should I fight the Forgotten King optional boss?

Absolutely yes. The Forgotten King rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum for free upon defeat. If you skip this fight, you must wait until Chapter X and pay 78,000 Pesetas to buy it. The fight is challenging but fair, and having the Magnum early makes the Final Boss significantly easier.

Can I change my loadout during a boss fight?

No. Once a boss fight begins, you cannot access the Merchant or storage crates. You can only use what you bring into the arena. However, most boss arenas have a few breakable crates that may contain herbs or ammo, so explore the arena during calm moments.

How much ammo should I bring to the Final Boss?

Bring maximum carry capacity for Rifle and Magnum ammo. Craft at least 10 Magnum rounds and 20 Rifle rounds before the fight. The Final Boss has five phases and a massive health pool — running out of ammo means you cannot damage the boss during its vulnerability windows.

What difficulty should I play for boss fights?

For your first playthrough, Standard difficulty provides the best balance. Bosses have manageable health pools and you can carry more ammo. Hardcore difficulty increases boss health by 50% and reduces ammo drops significantly. Professional is only for masochists and speedrunners.
