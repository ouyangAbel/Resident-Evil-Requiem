GENERAL BOSS FIGHT TIPS

RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

Always Enter Boss Fights Prepared

Save your game at the typewriter before every boss arena. Carry two Green+Red herb combos and one First Aid Med minimum. Spread your ammo across at least two weapon types. If you have been hoarding magnum ammo, boss fights are exactly when to use it.

ENVIRONMENT

Study the Arena First

Every boss arena has environmental advantages: explosive canisters, destructible pillars, elevated platforms, or cover spots. Spend the first 15 seconds learning the layout. Knowing where canisters spawn is often the difference between a clean kill and a frustrating retry.

WEAPON SWITCHING

Match the Weapon to the Phase

Late-game bosses cycle through phases that each favor a different weapon. The Rifle excels at precision weak-point shots. The Shotgun staggers at close range. The Magnum is your emergency button for enrage states. Use quick-swap to change weapons without opening the inventory.

DIFFICULTY SCALING

Boss Health Changes Per Difficulty

On Standard, boss HP is baseline. Hardcore increases HP by roughly 40% and adds one extra attack pattern per phase. Nightmare doubles HP, tightens dodge windows, and reduces arena ammo drops. Adjust your reserves accordingly.

THE BLOATED LYCAN (CHAPTER III)

THE BLOATED LYCAN
CHAPTER III: BELOW THE SURFACE • FIRST BOSS ENCOUNTER
DIFFICULTY: ★★☆☆☆
HP (Standard)
~4,500
Phases
2
Arena
Tunnel Exit
Mandatory
Yes

The Bloated Lycan is your introduction to RE Requiem's boss design philosophy. This swollen creature guards the tunnel exit at the end of Chapter III. It teaches you the core loop every subsequent boss will demand: read the attack animation, exploit the recovery window, use the environment.

Phase 1 (100%-50% HP)

The Lycan attacks with wide sweeping claws at close range and vomits acid at medium distance. Maintain medium distance (roughly 5-8 meters). When it raises both arms to charge the acid vomit, dodge sideways and fire 3-4 headshots during the 2-second recovery. The explosive canisters near the tunnel walls deal roughly 30% of its HP when shot near the boss.

Phase 2 (50%-0% HP)

The Lycan becomes faster and adds a charging lunge attack. It crouches low and growls for about 1 second before lunging. Dodge to either side and punish with 2-3 shotgun blasts. Continue using canisters for burst damage. Approximately 20 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun blasts finish it on Standard.

Recommended Loadout: SG-09 R Handgun (15+ rounds), W-870 Shotgun (6+ shells), 1 Green+Red Herb combo. The handgun handles Phase 1 safely; switch to shotgun for Phase 2 stagger windows.

Pro Tip: Two canister explosions alone deal over 60% of its total HP. If low on ammo, kite the Lycan near canisters and detonate them for free damage.

THE ABOMINATION (CHAPTER VI)

THE ABOMINATION
CHAPTER VI: THE LABYRINTH BELOW • FIRST MAJOR BOSS
DIFFICULTY: ★★★☆☆
HP (Standard)
~12,000
Phases
3
Arena
Underground Lab
Mandatory
Yes

A grotesque fusion of multiple test subjects and the game's first real skill check. Three distinct phases with escalating aggression demand you manage resources carefully and switch weapons between phases.

Phase 1 (100%-65% HP): Exposed Weak Points

Two glowing weak points on the torso. Use the W-870 Shotgun at close-medium range. Each weak point takes 3-4 shotgun blasts to destroy. When both are destroyed simultaneously, the boss staggers for 5 seconds - unload everything during this window. Dodge backward to avoid its wide arm sweeps.

Phase 2 (65%-30% HP): Extra Arms and Shockwave

It grows additional arms and gains a ground slam shockwave. Dodge sideways to avoid the shockwave. After the slam, a 3-second recovery window opens - switch to the Semi-Auto Rifle and land 2-3 precision shots on the exposed core.

Phase 3 (30%-0% HP): Enrage and Explosive Canisters

Faster movement, more aggressive attacks. Explosive canisters spawn around the arena. Lure the Abomination near a canister and shoot it for massive damage (~15% HP per canister). Two canister explosions plus sustained rifle fire ends the fight.

Recommended Loadout: W-870 Shotgun (12+ shells), Semi-Auto Rifle (15+ rounds), Handgun (20+ rounds backup), 2 herb combos, 1 First Aid Med.

Pro Tip: In Phase 2, the shockwave hitbox is deceptive. Dodge before the slam connects, not after. Early timing is more forgiving.

SHADOW STALKER (CHAPTER VIII)

HP (Standard)

Phases

Inverted Main Hall
Mandatory
Yes (Progression)

A stealth-type enemy that phases between visible and invisible states. This fight is about patience and positioning. Rushing in will get you killed.

Phase 1 (100%-40% HP): Visible and Invisible Cycling

Alternates between visible (attacking) and invisible (repositioning) every 8-10 seconds. Track it by the faint red glow when invisible. When it materializes, fire two close-range shotgun blasts to force it to retreat. Each window lasts about 4 seconds. ~12 shotgun rounds total on Standard.

Phase 2 (40%-0% HP): Aggressive Repositioning

Faster movement, shorter 3-second visible windows. It teleports behind you for backstab attacks. Keep your back to a wall to prevent flanking. Continue the same cycle: wait, two shotgun blasts, reposition.

Recommended Loadout: W-870 Shotgun (10+ shells), Handgun (backup), 1 herb combo.

Pro Tip: The red glow is most visible against dark floor tiles. Look down at ground level rather than scanning at eye height for easier tracking.

THE FORGOTTEN KING (CHAPTER IX - OPTIONAL)

HP (Standard)

Phases
3
Arena

Mandatory

The Forgotten King is the game's optional superboss. A towering armored figure wielding a massive halberd, he guards the Broken Butterfly Magnum - one of the strongest weapons in the game. Defeating him is highly recommended if you have the ammo reserves.

Phase 1 (100%-60% HP): Halberd Sweeps

Wide horizontal sweeps and overhead slams. Stay behind him - his back is unprotected. Dodge behind during recovery and fire 2-3 rifle shots at the glowing red weak point between his shoulder blades. His turning speed is slow.

Phase 2 (60%-25% HP): Shockwave Slam

He plants the halberd into the ground for a radial shockwave. Back away to mid-range when you see the plant animation, then use the rifle during the long recovery. A safe zone exists directly behind him at close range.

Phase 3 (25%-0% HP): Enrage

Faster attacks, chained swings. Wait for the overhead slam (long telegraph, feet planted wide), dodge behind, and fire. Use saved magnum ammo here for burst damage to end the fight quickly.

Recommended Loadout: Semi-Auto Rifle (20+ rounds), W-870 Shotgun (8+ shells for emergency stagger), 2 herb combos, 1 First Aid Med. Bring magnum ammo if available.

Pro Tip: Defeat the Forgotten King before Chapter X so the Merchant can upgrade the Broken Butterfly Magnum, making the Amalgam and Final Boss significantly easier. This is the most impactful optional fight in the game.

THE AMALGAM (CHAPTER XI)

THE AMALGAM
CHAPTER XI: THE FINAL DESCENT • HARDEST BOSS IN THE GAME
DIFFICULTY: ★★★★★
HP (Standard)
~28,000
Phases
4 (3 Forms + Final)
Arena
Central Chamber
Mandatory
Yes

Widely considered the hardest boss in RE Requiem. A horrific fusion of every major enemy type, it cycles through three distinct forms then combines them all in a brutal Final Form. Chapter XI has the tightest resource economy in the game - enter with full supplies.

Lycan Form (100%-70% HP)

Fast melee with lunging claws. Use the Semi-Auto Rifle at range while backpedaling. Dodge lunge attacks to the side (not backward - the range is deceptively long). Three to four rifle headshots stagger it for additional shots. Easiest form if you maintain distance.

Abomination Form (70%-45% HP)

Slow but devastating tentacle attacks. Use stone pillars for cover. When a tentacle wraps a pillar, it gets stuck for 3 seconds - switch to W-870 Shotgun and fire 2-3 close-range shots. Never fight in the open; tentacle sweep covers nearly 180 degrees.

Shadow Form (45%-25% HP)

Phases in and out like the Shadow Stalker but more aggressive. Fire shotgun when it materializes - two shots stagger, four shots force phase-out. Track the red glow during invisibility and position near explosive canisters. Do not waste ammo on nothing.

Final Form (25%-0% HP): All Three Combined

Cycles all three patterns every 15-20 seconds. Explosive canisters respawn every 30 seconds. Use canisters as primary damage - lure the Amalgam near one and detonate for ~8% HP per explosion. Supplement with rifle during Lycan windows, shotgun during other forms. This is a marathon, not a sprint.

Recommended Loadout: Semi-Auto Rifle (fully upgraded, 30+ rounds), W-870 Shotgun (15+ shells), Broken Butterfly Magnum (4+ rounds for emergency), 3 herb combos, 2 First Aid Meds. Conserve magnum for Final Form only.

Pro Tip: Before entering the Central Chamber, backtrack and search every room in Chapter XI. Hidden ammo caches behind destructible crates in the eastern corridor are easy to miss.

THE FINAL BOSS (CHAPTER XII)

THE FINAL BOSS
CHAPTER XII: REQUIEM • FINAL ENCOUNTER • ENDING-DEPENDENT
DIFFICULTY: ★★★★★
HP (Standard)
~35,000
Phases
5
Arena
Dimensional Nexus
Mandatory
Yes

A multi-phase gauntlet mirroring the entire game arc. Each phase references a previous boss, escalating in intensity. The ending depends on your choices and whether you collected all six Lore Fragments, but the boss fight itself is the same regardless.

Phase 1 (100%-80% HP): Lycan Echo

Mirrors the Bloated Lycan with faster attack speed. Maintain medium distance, dodge acid vomit, use rifle headshots. Warm-up phase - conserve ammo with precision shots.

Phase 2 (80%-60% HP): Abomination Echo

Tentacle attacks with tighter dodge windows. Arena pillars are destructible and do not regenerate. Manage pillar usage carefully, switch to shotgun during stagger windows.

Phase 3 (60%-40% HP): Shadow Echo

Invisibility cycling with shorter materialization windows. Track red glow, fire shotgun when visible. Keep back to arena walls to prevent teleport flanking.

Phase 4 (40%-15% HP): Dimensional Collapse

Arena collapses - glowing red cracks mark hazardous floor. Boss combines Amalgam-style form switching with ranged energy attacks. Stay on stable ground, use rifle for ranged damage. Canisters spawn periodically near arena edges.

Phase 5 (15%-0% HP): Requiem

Rapid attacks, area denial energy fields, and a one-hit kill grab. The grab has a 2-second telegraph (boss rears back with both arms) - dodge sideways immediately. Use everything: magnum, grenades, shotgun. Below 5% HP, a QTE triggers to finish the fight.

Recommended Loadout: Everything. Semi-Auto Rifle (30+ rounds), W-870 Shotgun (20+ shells), Broken Butterfly Magnum (6+ rounds), Handgun (30+ backup), all grenades, 3 herb combos, 2 First Aid Meds.

Pro Tip: On Standard, the grab can be survived with full health and auto-triggering First Aid Med. On Hardcore and Nightmare, it is instant death. Learn the telegraph.

AFTER THE FINAL BOSS

New Game Plus and Chapter Select

After defeating the final boss, you unlock Chapter Select and New Game Plus. In NG+, weapon upgrades and inventory carry over. Bosses have slightly increased HP but upgraded weapons compensate. Use NG+ for missed collectibles, higher difficulties, and the secret ending if you missed Lore Fragments.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many bosses are in Resident Evil Requiem?

6 boss encounters: The Bloated Lycan (Ch. III), The Abomination (Ch. VI), Shadow Stalker (Ch. VIII), The Forgotten King (Ch. IX, optional), The Amalgam (Ch. XI), and the Final Boss (Ch. XII).

What is the hardest boss in Resident Evil Requiem?

The Amalgam in Chapter XI. Its four-form cycling mechanic, tight resource economy, and the brutal Final Form combining all patterns make it the most challenging encounter.

What weapon is best for boss fights?

The Semi-Auto Rifle for precision weak-point shots at range. The W-870 Shotgun for close-range stagger windows. The Broken Butterfly Magnum for highest single-shot damage (save for hardest phases).

Should I fight the Forgotten King?

Yes, if you have ammo. The Broken Butterfly Magnum reward is extremely useful against the Amalgam and Final Boss. This is the most impactful optional fight in the game.

Can you replay boss fights?

Yes. After completing the game, Chapter Select lets you replay any chapter including bosses. Inventory and upgrades carry over.
