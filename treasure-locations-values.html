RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM TREASURE LOCATIONS & VALUES: ALL TREASURES, GEMS & MERCHANT PRICES

UPDATED AUGUST 5, 2026 • 22 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Maximizing your Lei income in Resident Evil Requiem is one of the most overlooked paths to power. Every treasure, gemstone, and ornate trinket you find can be sold to the Merchant for serious coin — and with 47 treasures and 23 gemstones scattered across all 12 chapters, a full treasure run nets you over 312,000 Lei. That is enough to fully upgrade two weapons and still have plenty left over for craft materials and first-aid meds. This complete guide covers every treasure location and value in the game, including gem set bonuses, missable items, and the most profitable farming routes to fuel your upgrade empire.

Quick Summary: 47 treasures (worth 248,450 Lei total) + 23 gemstones (worth 64,000 Lei total) = 312,450 Lei maximum per playthrough. Six treasures are permanently missable. Gem sets grant a 25% bonus when sold together.

TREASURE TYPES & CATEGORIES

Treasures in RE Requiem fall into three distinct categories, each with different rarity tiers and sell values. Understanding these categories helps you prioritize which treasures to hunt first and which ones justify backtracking for.

Antique Treasures

The most common treasure type, antiques are ornate historical artifacts found in drawers, cabinets, and display cases throughout the game world. They range from 2,000 Lei (Silver Locket) to 25,000 Lei (Gilded Monstrance) depending on rarity. Most antiques are found in optional rooms off the critical path.

Gemstones

Ruby, Emerald, and Diamond gemstones are the second treasure category. These are always found in treasure chests (the locked ones requiring lockpicks) and from solving environmental puzzles. Gemstones can be sold individually or as a matched set for a significant bonus. Individual values range from 3,000 Lei (Ruby) to 10,000 Lei (Diamond).

Quest Treasures

Quest treasures are unique items tied to side missions and story events. They auto-complete quest objectives when collected and cannot be sold until the associated quest finishes. The most valuable quest treasure is the Crimson Crown from Chapter 11's Forgotten Throne puzzle, worth 50,000 Lei after the Antiquarian quest completes.

GEM SET BONUSES

The most important treasure mechanic in RE Requiem is the Gem Set Bonus. When you sell a matched set of one Ruby, one Emerald, and one Diamond to the Merchant, you receive a 25% bonus on top of the individual values. Combined with the Collector Badge (10% price boost, unlocked by finding 15 treasures), a matched gem set becomes extraordinarily profitable.

MISSABLE TREASURES — CRITICAL WARNINGS

Six treasures in RE Requiem are permanently missable on a first playthrough because their locations become inaccessible after story events. If you want 100% treasure completion, you MUST grab these before progressing the critical path.

The Six Missable Treasures:

  1. Antique Pocket Watch (Chapter 3) — Grab before the cellar collapse cutscene.
  2. Jade Cicada (Chapter 6) — Grab before defeating the Abomination.
  3. Chapter 12 Ruby (Side Corridor) — Take the left path at the final junction.
  4. Chapter 12 Diamond (Collapsed Chapel) — Left path.
  5. Chapter 12 Ruby (Underground Armory) — Left path.
  6. Chapter 12 Emerald (Watchtower) — Left path.

Total missable value: 45,000 Lei. The other 41 treasures and all gemstones can be collected via Chapter Select.

For the Chapter 12 treasures, the key is to always take the left path at the junction before the final boss. The right path leads directly to the end. The left path is a winding exploration route that hits all four missable treasures, then loops back to the final boss door. There are no enemies on the left path, so there is no risk in exploring it.

BEST TREASURE FARMING ROUTES

While a full 100% treasure run takes about 2.5 hours, specific route loops are far more efficient for Lei farming. Here are the top three treasure farming routes in RE Requiem.

ROUTE 1

Rookwood Estate Western Wing Loop

Time: 4 minutes | Lei per run: 40,000 | With Collector Badge: 44,000

Treasures: Ruby (Library cabinet) → Emerald (hidden passage) → Antique Compass (grandfather clock) → Diamond Ring (storage room). Start from the Chapter 2 checkpoint at the Main Hall, grab all four treasures, then restart. This is the single most efficient Lei-per-minute route in the entire game.

ROUTE 2

Hospital Pharmacy Run

Time: 6 minutes | Lei per run: 26,000 | With Collector Badge: 28,600

Treasures: Lockpick #3 (morgue) → Ruby (pharmacy cabinet) → Diamond (Surgery Room B) → Jade Pendant (nurse's station) → Gold Cigarette Case (ambulance). This route has slightly lower Lei/minute but has the advantage of dropping a Lockpick every run, useful for other treasure chests.

ROUTE 3

Forgotten City Jewelry Heist

Time: 5 minutes | Lei per run: 30,000 | With Collector Badge: 33,000

Treasures: Ruby (jewelry store vault) → Diamond (clock tower) → Ancient Coin (fountain) → Monstrance (cathedral, if Antiquarian quest active). This route is excellent because the Antiquarian quest treasure has a 100% drop rate and the other three treasures are clustered tightly together.

Important: Treasure farming resets on Chapter Select restart. The Merchant retains all Lei earned, and treasures do not need to be re-sold after restarting. The most efficient loop is to collect, open the menu, restart the chapter, and repeat. The Merchant's shop interface is accessible from any typewriter save point.

COLLECTOR BADGE & PRICE OPTIMIZATION

The Collector Badge is a passive accessory that increases all treasure sell prices by 10%. It unlocks automatically after finding your 15th unique treasure in a single save file. This badge is the single most important item for treasure hunters because it applies to EVERY treasure you sell retroactively — even treasures you found before unlocking the badge.

Unlocking the Collector Badge

Find any 15 treasures across any combination of chapters. The badge unlocks immediately upon collecting the 15th treasure. Since there are 47 treasures total, hitting the 15-mid mark requires roughly one full playthrough of Chapters 1-6.

Stacking Bonuses

The Collector Badge's 10% bonus stacks multiplicatively with the Gem Set Bonus (25%). This means a matched gem set with the badge earns 37.5% more than individual sales without the badge. The math:

This 6,750 Lei difference per gem set adds up fast. Over a full playthrough with 7 complete gem sets, the badge earns you an extra 47,250 Lei compared to selling individually without it.

MERCHANT SELLING STRATEGY

Knowing WHEN to sell treasures matters almost as much as finding them. The Merchant buys treasures at fixed prices, but your spending priorities shift dramatically across the game.

Early Game (Chapters 1-4)

Sell every treasure immediately. You need Lei for weapon upgrades and craft materials. The Silver Locket (2,000 Lei) and Brass Compass (3,500 Lei) from Chapter 1 alone fund your first shotgun upgrade. Do NOT hoard treasures early — the Lei is far more valuable as upgrade currency than as stored wealth.

Mid Game (Chapters 5-8)

Continue selling individual treasures but start banking matched gem sets. You should have the Collector Badge by Chapter 6 at the latest. Hold matched sets for the bonus while selling unneeded antiques immediately. The mid-game is where weapon upgrade costs spike (Shotgun Level 3 costs 28,000 Lei), so steady Lei income is critical.

Late Game (Chapters 9-12)

By Chapter 9, you should be selling matched gem sets exclusively. The Antiquarian quest unlocks the Monstrance for 50,000 Lei — this single treasure funds the Magnum Level 2 upgrade and a full set of body armor. Prioritize completing the Antiquarian quest in Chapter 9 for this windfall.

Pro Tip: The Merchant has unlimited Lei and will always buy your treasures. There is no downside to selling early. Treasures do not degrade, do not expire, and the Merchant's prices never change. Sell as soon as you find them and let the upgrade bonuses compound across your playthrough.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

What is the most valuable treasure in Resident Evil Requiem?

The Crimson Crown is the single most valuable treasure in RE Requiem, selling for 50,000 Lei at the Merchant. Found in Chapter 11's Forgotten Throne room behind a hidden wall puzzle, it requires the Monstrance key item from Chapter 9. On a first playthrough, this treasure is only available in New Game Plus because the Monstrance quest requires a completed playthrough to unlock the Antiquarian NPC.

Q2

How much total Lei can be earned from treasures in Resident Evil Requiem?

Across all 12 chapters, there are 47 treasures and 23 gemstones (rubies, emeralds, diamonds) worth a combined 312,450 Lei at the Merchant. This excludes the Crimson Crown bonus treasure which appears only in New Game Plus. With optimal matched gem set selling and the Collector Badge, the effective total is approximately 340,000 Lei per playthrough.

Q3

Can you miss treasures permanently in Resident Evil Requiem?

Yes. Six treasures are permanently missable: the Antique Pocket Watch (Chapter 3, one-time area), the Jade Cicada (Chapter 6, behind a door that locks after the Abomination fight), and four treasures in Chapter 12 that vanish if you skip the optional left-path exploration branch before the final boss. All other treasures can be collected via Chapter Select.

Q4

Where is the best treasure farming route in RE Requiem?

The most efficient route is the Rookwood Estate Western Wing loop (Chapter 2): collect the Ruby (5,000), Emerald (8,000), Antique Compass (12,000), and Diamond Ring (15,000) in a single 4-minute run, totaling 40,000 Lei per loop. Combined with the Merchant's 10% price boost from the Collector Badge, this yields 44,000 Lei per run — over 660,000 Lei per hour.

Q5

Do gemstones stack for bonus Lei in Resident Evil Requiem?

Yes. Selling a matched set of Ruby, Emerald, and Diamond together grants a 25% set bonus. The Collector Badge adds another 10% on top. A matched set sells for 18,000 Lei base, which becomes 24,750 Lei with both bonuses — a significant boost over selling individually. Always sell matched sets for maximum profit.

Q6

When should I sell treasures to the Merchant in RE Requiem?

Always sell treasures immediately after collecting them. Unlike weapons, treasures do not take up inventory weight or slots — they auto-store. There is no benefit to hoarding, and selling early gives you Lei for weapon upgrades when you need them most. The only exception is gemstones, which you may want to hold until you have a matched set.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Treasure hunting in Resident Evil Requiem is not just about padding your Lei count — it is the foundation of your entire upgrade economy. A thorough treasure hunter entering New Game Plus will start with over 300,000 Lei in upgrades already invested, making the first several chapters dramatically easier. The key takeaways are simple: sell treasures immediately, always prioritize matched gem sets, grab the Collector Badge by Chapter 6 at the latest, and never skip the left path in Chapter 12.

With six permanently missable treasures on a first playthrough, a single careless run can cost you 45,000 Lei — enough for a full weapon upgrade tier.
