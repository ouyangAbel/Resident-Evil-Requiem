Full gameplay walkthrough covering all 12 chapters of Resident Evil Requiem

ACT I: THE ESTATE GROUNDS (CHAPTERS I–III)

The first three chapters of Resident Evil Requiem establish the setting, introduce core mechanics, and immerse players in the oppressive atmosphere of the estate. These chapters are slower-paced and exploration-heavy, emphasizing resource management and puzzle-solving over combat.

01
Opening Chapter • 30–45 min
THE ASHEN ARRIVAL

Resident Evil Requiem opens with your protagonist arriving at a remote European estate under a relentless rainstorm. The wrought-iron gates are broken, the guardhouse is abandoned, and the first signs of violence are everywhere. Chapter I serves as the game's tutorial, teaching movement, combat basics, and item interaction without excessive hand-holding.

Key objectives: Enter the estate grounds, find the Gate Key in the guardhouse, collect the Combat Knife from the dead officer near the fountain, and reach the main mansion entrance. The first Typewriter save point is in the entrance hall's left corridor.

Enemies: Three basic Lycans appear in the courtyard. An explosive barrel near the well can kill two of them instantly. The third can be dodged by sprinting to the front door.

Why it matters: The Guard's Journal found in the guardhouse contains the safe code (1492) needed in Chapter II. Read every document you find.

02
Exploration-Heavy • 60–90 min
ECHOES OF THE ESTATE

The mansion interior is a masterclass in atmospheric design. Creaking floorboards, distant moans, and the flicker of candlelight create constant unease. Chapter II is the longest chapter in Act I and contains several critical puzzles that gate your progression through the estate.

Key objectives: Explore the ground floor (dining room, kitchen, library, main hall), solve the Grandfather Clock Puzzle (set to 11:45 using the clue from the dining room painting), find the Handgun in the locked study (code: 1492 from Chapter I), obtain the West Wing Key from the master bedroom safe, and locate the underground passage.

Enemies: Five standard zombies roam the mansion. Two burst through the dining room windows in a scripted encounter.

Why it matters: This chapter establishes the exploration loop that defines the game. Thorough exploration rewards you with Shotgun Parts, herbs, treasures, and crucial lore documents.

03
First Boss • 45–60 min
BELOW THE SURFACE

The underground passage beneath the mansion leads to a network of tunnels that once served as smuggling routes. Chapter III is tense and linear, with limited resources and some of the game's most claustrophobic encounters. It culminates in the first real boss fight.

Key objectives: Navigate the tunnel system (follow red wall markings), drain the flooded chamber by solving the Water Valve Puzzle (right, left, center), find the Tunnel Key, and defeat the chapter boss.

Boss: The Bloated Lycan. This swollen creature guards the tunnel exit. It attacks with sweeping claws and ranged acid vomit. Maintain medium distance, dodge the acid sideways, and fire during recovery windows. Explosive canisters near the walls deal massive damage. Approximately 20 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun blasts on Standard difficulty.

Why it matters: This chapter transitions the game from exploration into combat survival. The Bloated Lycan teaches you boss-phase thinking that will be essential for every major encounter ahead.

ACT II: BETRAYAL & THE LABORATORY (CHAPTERS IV–VI)

Act II raises the stakes dramatically. An ally's betrayal shifts the narrative into high gear, the estate transforms into a combat arena, and the underground laboratory reveals the scientific horror beneath the surface.

04
Mansion Revisited • 50–70 min
THE CRIMSON WING

Returning to the mansion reveals that everything has changed. New, more aggressive enemy variants patrol the halls, and previously visited areas now contain fresh threats and new item placements. The Crimson Wing introduces the Crimson Lycan — a faster, deadlier variant that can leap across rooms in a single bound.

Key objectives: Collect the Shotgun from the newly accessible armory, survive the three-wave Crimson Lycan ambush in the main hall, obtain the Garden Key, navigate the garden maze (right, left, straight through hedge gap, left at statue, right at fountain), and reach the greenhouse.

Why it matters: The Shotgun becomes your primary weapon from this point forward. The garden maze contains the Emerald Brooch, one of the most valuable treasures in the game.

05
Narrative Turning Point • 40–55 min
BETRAYAL IN THE DARK

This is the game's most dramatic chapter. An ally reveals their true intentions, and the story pivots from survival horror to desperate escape. Gameplay shifts to a more urgent, linear pace as the estate begins to collapse around you.

Key objectives: Survive the betrayal cutscene (earlier choices affect dialogue), escape the collapsing wine cellar within 3 minutes (Standard difficulty), find the Emergency Keycard on a dead soldier near the collapsed archway, and reach the elevator to the underground laboratory.

Pro tip: During the timed escape, do not fight enemies — sprint through debris and follow the lit torches. At the fork, take the left path (the right is a dead end with a Gold Ingot treasure).

Why it matters: This chapter permanently changes the game's tone. The trust you placed in earlier characters is shattered, and every NPC encounter afterward carries new tension.

The Abomination boss fight — Chapter VI boss strategy

06
Major Boss Fight • 60–80 min
THE LIVING LABORATORY

The underground laboratory is the game's most complex area. Interconnected rooms, security systems, and disturbing environmental storytelling make this chapter a methodical exploration challenge. Two critical puzzles gate your progress, and the chapter ends with the first major boss encounter.

Key objectives: Restore the power system (find three fuses in the break room, operating theater, and storage room), access the Research Wing with Security Clearance B, solve the Specimen Tank Puzzle (align tubes: Red-Blue-Green-Yellow), obtain the Master Keycard from the high-security vault, and defeat The Abomination.

Boss: The Abomination. A grotesque fusion of multiple test subjects with three distinct phases. Phase 1: target two glowing weak points on the torso with shotgun blasts. Phase 2: it grows extra arms; dodge the ground slam shockwave and fire during recovery. Phase 3: lure it near explosive canisters that spawn around the arena.

Why it matters: This is the game's difficulty spike. The Abomination demands everything you have learned: resource management, pattern recognition, environmental awareness, and precise aiming.

ACT III: THE REQUIEM & BEYOND (CHAPTERS VII–XII)

The final six chapters take the game into entirely new territory. Dimensional rifts, nightmarish alternate realities, and the collapse of the barrier between worlds escalate the horror to cosmic levels.

07
New World • 50–65 min
THE REQUIEM BEGINS

Emerging from the laboratory, you find yourself in a decaying cathedral town that exists in a dimension overlapping with reality. The atmosphere shifts from scientific horror to supernatural dread.

Key objectives: Explore the cathedral town, obtain the Cathedral Key from the bell tower, solve the Stained Glass Puzzle (Angel, Chalice, Skull, Cross from left to right), collect three Altar Stones, and open the path to the dimensional rift.

Why it matters: Chapter VII is the first time the Semi-Auto Rifle becomes available. It also introduces the Lore Fragments collectibles required for the secret ending.

08
Mirror Dimension • 55–70 min
DIMENSIONS APART

Stepping through the rift transports you to a nightmarish mirror version of the estate from Chapters I–III. The layout is inverted, gravity behaves strangely, and new enemy types phase in and out of visibility.

Key objectives: Navigate the inverted mirror-estate, defeat the Shadow Stalker mini-boss (track by red glow when invisible, shotgun burst when materialized), find the Dimensional Anchor, and solve the Inversion Puzzle (pull levers 4-3-2-1 with 2-second pauses).

Boss: Shadow Stalker. This enemy phases between visible and invisible states. Two close-range shotgun blasts force it to retreat. Approximately 12 shotgun rounds total to defeat.

Why it matters: The mirror dimension concept forces veteran players to unlearn the estate layout, creating genuine disorientation.

Exploring The Forgotten City — the game's largest open area

09
Open Exploration • 70–100 min
THE FORGOTTEN CITY

The Forgotten City is the game's largest explorable area, featuring interconnected streets, crumbling buildings, and numerous optional encounters. This is where completionists will spend the most time.

Key objectives: Map the city using the Cartographer's Notes, activate four City Beacons, defeat the optional Forgotten King boss, and collect all six Lore Fragments for the secret ending path.

Optional Boss: The Forgotten King. A towering armored figure wielding a massive halberd. Stay behind him. When he plants the halberd for a shockwave slam, use the rifle to target the weak point on his back. Rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum.

Why it matters: This is the only chapter with true open-world exploration. The six Lore Fragments are required for the secret ending.

10
Combat Gauntlet • 45–60 min
CONVERGENCE

The dimensional barriers collapse. Both worlds merge into a single nightmarish landscape. Chapter X is a gauntlet of combat encounters with minimal exploration, building intensity toward the finale.

Key objectives: Fight through three consecutive arena encounters, protect the NPC ally during the 4-minute bridge defense sequence, and reach the Convergence Point.

Pro tip: Stock up on shotgun ammo and grenades before the bridge defense. Focus fire on Crimson Lycans first — they deal the most damage to your NPC ally.

11
Penultimate Chapter • 50–65 min
THE FINAL DESCENT

This chapter descends into the heart of the dimensional rift. The environment is surreal and constantly shifting. Follow the red light to navigate the shifting corridors and avoid dead ends.

Key objectives: Navigate the shifting corridors, defeat the Amalgam boss in the central chamber, recover the Requiem Key, and make final preparations before the endgame.

Boss: The Amalgam. A horrific fusion of every major enemy type. It cycles through forms: Lycan Form (fast melee, use rifle), Abomination Form (slow tentacle attacks, use pillars), Shadow Form (phases in/out, shotgun when visible), and Final Form at 25% HP (combines all three — use explosive canisters that respawn every 30 seconds).

Final boss encounter — all phases of the Chapter XII showdown

12
Finale • 40–60 min
REQUIEM

The final chapter. The final boss has multiple phases that mirror the entire game arc, escalating in intensity and requiring different weapon strategies for each phase.

Pre-fight checklist: Full health, maximum ammunition for every weapon type, at least 3 First Aid Meds, and all weapons fully upgraded.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CHAPTERS

Q1

How many chapters are in Resident Evil Requiem?

Resident Evil Requiem has 12 chapters divided into three acts. Act I (Chapters I–III) covers the estate exploration. Act II (Chapters IV–VI) introduces betrayal and the laboratory. Act III (Chapters VII–XII) takes the game into dimensional horror and the final confrontation.

Q2

How long does it take to beat all 12 chapters?

A first playthrough on Standard difficulty takes approximately 10–14 hours. Completionist runs with all collectibles and secrets can take 25–30 hours. Speedrunners have completed the game in under 3 hours.

Q3

Which chapter is the hardest?

Most players find Chapter XI (The Final Descent) to be the most challenging due to the Amalgam boss fight and the extremely tight resource economy. Chapter VI is a close second.

Q4

Can I replay specific chapters?

Yes, after completing the game you unlock Chapter Select from the main menu. Your inventory carries over between chapter replays.

Q5

Which chapters have boss fights?

Four chapters have mandatory boss fights: Chapter III (The Bloated Lycan), Chapter VI (The Abomination), Chapter XI (The Amalgam), and Chapter XII (Final Boss). Chapter VIII has the Shadow Stalker mini-boss, and Chapter IX has the optional Forgotten King boss.

Q6

What is the longest chapter?

Chapter IX (The Forgotten City) is the longest chapter at 70–100 minutes. It features the game's largest open explorable area. Chapter II (Echoes of the Estate) is the second longest at 60–90 minutes.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem's 12 chapters deliver one of the most ambitious narratives in the franchise's history. The three-act structure ensures that the game never feels repetitive — each act introduces new mechanics, new environments, and new threats that keep the experience fresh from start to finish.

Whether you are a longtime Resident Evil fan or a newcomer drawn in by the hype, these 12 chapters offer approximately 15 hours of tightly designed survival horror that rewards exploration, patience, and strategic thinking. Every chapter builds upon the last, and the payoff in Chapter XII makes the entire journey worthwhile.
