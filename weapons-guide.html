MELEE & EXPLOSIVES

Your knife and thrown explosives are supplementary tools that save your life when ammunition runs dry.

13
MELEE
COMBAT KNIFE

The combat knife never runs out of ammo. It deals low damage but is essential for conserving resources. Use it to finish staggered enemies, break crates, and perform stealth kills from behind.

Location: Chapter I, always in your inventory from the start.

Best Use: Knife kills on staggered enemies deal 3x more damage than standard attacks.

14
EXPLOSIVE
HAND GRENADES & PROXIMITY MINES

Hand grenades deal massive area damage and stagger bosses. Proximity mines detonate when enemies approach, excellent for defensive sequences.

Locations: Found throughout the game in breakable crates and Merchant stock. Mines available from Chapter VI.

Best Use: Pre-placed mines for arena defense. Grenades for boss phase transitions. Never waste on single enemies.

WEAPON UPGRADE PATHS

The Merchant offers four upgrade categories for each weapon. Upgrades cost increasing amounts of Pesetas, so planning your path is critical.

Upgrade TypeEffectPriority
PowerIncreases base damage per shotHIGHEST
CapacityIncreases magazine size (also refills magazine free)HIGH
Reload SpeedDecreases reload animation timeMEDIUM
Rate of FireDecreases time between shotsLOW

Pro Tip: Upgrade Capacity just before entering a boss fight. The capacity upgrade automatically refills your magazine, giving you a free reload worth of ammunition — even if you are fully empty.

Exclusive Upgrades

Each weapon has a final "Exclusive" upgrade that unlocks only after all four base stats are fully maxed. These are expensive but game-changing:

  • SG-09 R: 5x critical hit chance — makes the starter pistol viable even in late game
  • Red9: 2x base damage — turns it into a pocket magnum
  • Blacktail: 1.5x all stats — the ultimate all-rounder
  • W-870: 2x pellet count — devastating at close range
  • Striker: 1.8x fire rate — becomes almost automatic
  • Semi-Auto Rifle: Piercing shots — bullets penetrate through multiple enemies

BEST LOADOUTS BY CHAPTER RANGE

Your inventory is limited, so carrying the right combination of weapons is essential. Here are our recommended loadouts for each phase of the game.

Chapters I – III
EARLY GAME LOADOUT

Primary: SG-09 R (Handgun) | Secondary: W-870 (Shotgun) | Reserve: Combat Knife | Items: 2 Green Herbs, 1 First Aid Med

Keep it simple early on. The SG-09 R handles most enemies; switch to the W-870 when they get close. Use the knife on staggered foes to save ammo. The SR M1903 rifle joins your loadout in Chapter III.

Chapters IV – VI
MID-GAME LOADOUT

Primary: Red9 or Blacktail | Secondary: W-870 (Shotgun) | Reserve: SR M1903 (Rifle) | Optional: TMP | Items: 3 Mixed Herbs, 2 First Aid Meds

Upgrade to a stronger handgun and add the TMP for burst damage. The rifle becomes essential for precision encounters. Buy the Blacktail from the Merchant in Chapter V if you can afford it.

Chapters VII – IX
LATE-GAME LOADOUT

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle | Secondary: Striker (Shotgun) | Reserve: Blacktail (Handgun) | Optional: TMP or Grenade Launcher | Items: Full herb inventory, all ammo types

The Semi-Auto Rifle is your primary from Chapter VII onward. The Striker handles close encounters. Drop the bolt-action rifle and Red9 — they are obsolete by this point.

Chapters X – XII
ENDGAME LOADOUT

Primary: Semi-Auto Rifle (fully upgraded) | Secondary: Striker (Shotgun) | Boss Killer: Broken Butterfly (Magnum) | Reserve: Railgun or Grenade Launcher | Items: Full herbs, all ammo, 3+ First Aid Meds

By endgame, the Semi-Auto Rifle should be fully upgraded with its exclusive piercing upgrade. The Broken Butterfly is reserved exclusively for boss vulnerability windows.

AMMO ECONOMY & MANAGEMENT

Ammo scarcity is the defining challenge of RE Requiem. The game uses adaptive difficulty that reduces ammo drops when you are well-stocked and increases them when you are low.

Ammo Drop Mechanics

  • The game tracks your total ammo across all weapons. When reserves are high, crates drop crafting materials instead of ammo.
  • Carrying fewer total rounds triggers more ammo drops. This is counterintuitive but effective.
  • Killing enemies with headshots and melee finishers yields higher ammo drop rates than body-shot kills.
  • Breaking all crates and barrels in every room is essential. Many players miss hidden crates behind furniture.

Ammo Crafting

Starting in Chapter V, you can craft ammunition at workbenches using Gunpowder and Resources:

  • Handgun Ammo: 1 Gunpowder = 15 rounds
  • Shotgun Shells: 1 Gunpowder + 1 Resource (L) = 6 shells
  • Rifle Ammo: 1 Gunpowder + 1 Resource (L) = 5 rounds
  • Magnum Ammo: 2 Gunpowder + 2 Resources (L) = 3 rounds (Chapter IX onward)
  • Special Ammo: 1 Gunpowder + 1 Resource (L) + 1 Rare Resource = varies

Ammo Conservation Rule: Never use more than 2 handgun shots on a standard enemy without going for a headshot. If you miss two headshots in a row, switch to the knife or reposition. Wasted ammo compounds quickly and can leave you defenseless before a boss fight.

MERCHANT UPGRADE PRIORITY

Currency (Pesetas) is limited. Here is the optimal spending order to maximize combat effectiveness across a single playthrough.

  1. Semi-Auto Rifle — Power upgrades (all levels) Your primary weapon for 60% of the game. Every damage upgrade reduces shots needed per kill, which compounds into massive ammo savings.
  2. Semi-Auto Rifle — Capacity upgrades More rounds per magazine means fewer reloads in combat. The free refill on upgrade purchase is a bonus.
  3. W-870 or Striker — Power upgrades Your backup weapon for close encounters needs to stagger enemies reliably.
  4. Handgun — Power + Capacity Whichever handgun you prefer (Blacktail recommended), upgrade after rifle and shotgun are invested.
  5. Broken Butterfly — Power only If you obtained the magnum, one power upgrade can mean the difference between a 2-shot and 3-shot boss kill.
  6. Everything else Distribute remaining Pesetas across reload speed, rate of fire, and exclusive upgrades as available.

Budget Tip: Sell all small treasures immediately. Combine large treasures (Ruby + Ring, Emerald + Gold Ingot) before selling for 3-5x value. This can fund an entire weapon upgrade path from scratch.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM WEAPONS

Q1

What is the best weapon in Resident Evil Requiem?

The Semi-Auto Rifle fully upgraded is the best overall weapon. Its combination of damage, fire rate, range, and the exclusive piercing upgrade makes it effective in every situation. For pure boss damage, the Broken Butterfly Magnum is unmatched.

Q2

How many weapons are in RE Requiem?

There are 14 unique weapons: 4 handguns (SG-09 R, Red9, Blacktail, Punishment MK2), 2 shotguns (W-870, Striker), 2 rifles (SR M1903, Semi-Auto Rifle), 1 SMG (TMP), 1 magnum (Broken Butterfly), 1 grenade launcher (Grandeur GL), 1 railgun, 1 combat knife, plus thrown explosives.

Q3

Can you fully upgrade all weapons in one playthrough?

Not all of them. You will earn enough Pesetas to fully upgrade 3-4 weapons in a single playthrough if you collect and sell all treasures. Prioritize your rifle and shotgun first. New Game+ carries over all upgrades.

Q4

Should I keep the SG-09 R after getting better handguns?

Yes, at least until you unlock its Exclusive upgrade (5x crit chance). Once unlocked, the SG-09 R becomes one of the highest-DPS handguns in the game. Many speedrunners prefer it over the Blacktail for this reason.

Q5

Is the TMP worth buying?

Yes, but only if you can also afford the TMP Stock from the Merchant in Chapter VI. Without the stock, the TMP's accuracy is too poor to be reliable. With the stock, it becomes excellent burst damage for boss weak points.

Q6

Where do you find the Broken Butterfly Magnum?

Defeat The Forgotten King, an optional boss in Chapter IX (The Forgotten City). If missed, it can be purchased from the Merchant in Chapter X for 78,000 Pesetas. See our Complete Walkthrough for the boss strategy.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Mastering the weapon system in Resident Evil Requiem is about more than just picking the strongest gun. It is about understanding ammo economy, upgrade timing, and loadout synergy. The right weapon at the right moment can turn a desperate fight into a controlled encounter.

The Semi-Auto Rifle is your best friend for most of the game, but do not neglect your handgun and shotgun.
