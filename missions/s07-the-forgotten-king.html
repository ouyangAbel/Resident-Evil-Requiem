MISSION s07
THE FORGOTTEN KING

SIDE MISSION • UPDATED JUNE 2026
SHARE THIS
Mission Info
  • Objective: Challenge the optional Forgotten King boss in the District of Crowns for Lore Fragment #4 and the Royal Arms upgrade.
  • Reward: Royal Arms upgrade, Lore Fragment #4

OVERVIEW

The Forgotten King is an optional boss encounter in Chapter IX's Forgotten City, accessible through the District of Crowns. This is one of the most resource-intensive optional fights in the game, but the rewards are significant: Lore Fragment #4 and the Royal Arms weapon upgrade that increases all weapon damage by 15%.

HOW TO START

In Mission 10, after collecting at least 3 of the 5 city seals, the District of Crowns central gate opens. Enter to find the Forgotten King's throne room.

BOSS STRATEGY

THE FORGOTTEN KING — OPTIONAL BOSS

Health: Large single-phase health pool (equivalent to a main story boss)

Weakness: Overhead slam recovery window (3 seconds), back attacks during charge

Recommended Weapons: Shotgun (primary), Magnum (emergency burst)

Attack Pattern:

  • Horizontal Sweep: Wide arc attack, dodge backward. Recovery: 1 second.
  • Overhead Slam: Slow, powerful attack. Dodge sideways. Recovery: 3 seconds — best counter window.
  • Shield Charge: Charges with his massive shield. Roll sideways at the last moment. He crashes into the wall and is stunned for 2 seconds.
  • Royal Decree: AoE attack around his position. Creates a shockwave. Back away to medium range to avoid.

Optimal Strategy: Stay at medium range and bait the Overhead Slam. Dodge sideways and fire 2-3 Shotgun blasts during recovery. Repeat 15-20 times. Use Magnum when he is stunned after Shield Charge for bonus damage.

TIPS

TIP

Patience Over Power

This fight rewards patience. The Forgotten King punishes aggressive play with his Royal Decree AoE. Wait for safe counter windows and chip away at his health. The fight takes 5-7 minutes on average.

REWARDS

  • Lore Fragment #4 — Fourth of six fragments needed for the secret ending.
  • Royal Arms Upgrade — Increases all weapon damage by 15%. Stacks with other weapon upgrades. One of the best upgrades in the game.
