RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM SECRETS GUIDE: ALL EASTER EGGS, HIDDEN WEAPONS & SECRET AREAS

UPDATED JUNE 29, 2026 • 20 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Resident Evil Requiem is absolutely packed with secrets, Easter eggs, and hidden content that most players will never find on a first playthrough. From developer callbacks to classic survival horror references, from hidden weapons to secret boss encounters, Capcom buried a staggering amount of hidden content in this 2026 masterpiece.

We spent over 150 hours uncovering every secret the game has to offer. This guide catalogs all 40+ secrets and Easter eggs across all 12 chapters, organized by category so you can hunt them down systematically or cherry-pick the ones that interest you most. Whether you are looking for the True Ending path, the Developer Room, or the infamous Golden Egg quest, we have you covered.

SPOILER WARNING: This guide reveals hidden content, secret areas, and optional encounters throughout the entire game. If you want to discover these naturally, bookmark this guide for your second playthrough.

HIDDEN WEAPONS AND UPGRADES

CHAPTER 3 - ESTATE ARMORY
M1897 TRENCH GUN
LOCATION: Behind the broken bookshelf (C4 key required)

A vintage pump-action shotgun with higher stagger power than the standard W870. Destroy the bookshelf with a grenade or magnum round to reveal the weapon case. The C4 key is in the Estate Office safe (code: 03-21). This shotgun excels at crowd control and can stagger Bloated Lycans in one shot.

CHAPTER 6 - HOSPITAL BASEMENT
INCENDIARY GRENADE LAUNCHER
LOCATION: Morgue drawer 7 (requires Morgue Key from Nurse Station)

A grenade launcher pre-loaded with 6 incendiary rounds. Found inside a body drawer in the hospital morgue. The Morgue Key is hanging on a hook in the Nurse Station office - easily missed if you do not look up. Incendiary rounds deal massive damage to the Amalgam-type enemies found in later chapters.

CHAPTER 9 - FORGOTTEN CITY VAULT
PRIMAL KNIFE (FULLY UPGRADED)
LOCATION: Vault behind the waterfall (requires all 16 Mr. Raccoon figures)

The Primal Knife starts at maximum durability with infinite repairs - it never breaks. Unlocked only after collecting every Mr. Raccoon figure across all chapters. The waterfall vault opens automatically when you approach with all 16 figures in your inventory. This is the single most missable secret in the game.

CHAPTER 10 - CONVERGENCE LAB
ANTI-MATERIEL RIFLE
LOCATION: West wing weapons locker (keypad code: 4-8-6-5)

A one-shot-kill rifle against most non-boss enemies. Input H-U-N-K on the numerical keypad (4-8-6-5) to open the level-3 security weapons locker on the west side of the Convergence Lab. The rifle uses rare .50 BMG rounds - only 12 exist in the entire game.

CHAPTER 11 - FINAL DESCENT
GOLDEN EGG LAUNCHER
LOCATION: Developer Room pedestals (requires both Golden Eggs)

A joke weapon that fires exploding eggs dealing 999 damage per hit. Complete the Golden Egg quest chain: find Egg 1 in Chapter 6 hospital coop, Egg 2 in Chapter 9 Forgotten King sarcophagus, then place both on the Developer Room pedestals. Only 3 eggs exist per playthrough. NG+ only.

SECRET AREAS AND HIDDEN ROOMS

CHAPTER 1 - ROOKWOOD ESTATE
THE GARDENER'S SHED
LOCATION: Behind the overgrown hedge maze (shoot the scarecrow)

A hidden shed containing a Typewriter save point, Green+Red herb combo, and the Gardener's Diary (Lore Fragment 1). Shooting the scarecrow next to the hedge maze reveals a concealed dirt path leading behind the greenery.

CHAPTER 4 - THE CRIMSON WING
THE HIDDEN CHAPEL
LOCATION: Pull both candelabras in the main hall simultaneously

A secret chapel beneath the Crimson Wing mansion. Contains a treasure chest with 10,000 Lei worth of gems, a Ruby (needed for Merchant special stock), and a file that foreshadows the True Ending plot twist. Use the dual-candelabra mechanism on opposite sides of the main hall.

CHAPTER 7 - THE REQUIEM BEGINS
RICHARD AIKEN'S OFFICE
LOCATION: Third floor, east wing (break the cracked wall with explosives)

A hidden office belonging to Richard Aiken, a direct callback to the original Resident Evil. Contains the Assault Shotgun upgrade, a file discussing the T-Virus origins, and Richard's signature anti-venom serum. The cracked wall is barely visible - look for hairline fractures near the east stairwell.

CHAPTER 11 - FINAL DESCENT
DEVELOPER ROOM
LOCATION: Rooftop behind the helipad (requires Developer Keycard from Ch.9)

Classic Capcom Developer Room with character model viewer, concept art gallery, sound test (all 47 tracks), and a heartfelt thank-you message from the dev team. The keycard is in Chapter 9 Forgotten City archives - locked drawer, code: 1996 (the year Resident Evil 1 released). The room also contains a terminal that grants infinite ammo for one weapon per save file.

CLASSIC RESIDENT EVIL EASTER EGGS

EASTER EGG 1

The Jill Sandwich Note

In Chapter 3 Library, examine the bulletin board to find a faded note reading: "Watch out for falling ceilings. You do not want to become a Jill sandwich." This is a direct nod to Barry Burton's infamous line from the original Resident Evil (1996).

EASTER EGG 2

Merchant's Classic Quote

When you visit the Merchant in Chapter 5 with exactly 0 Lei in your inventory, he has a roughly 10 percent chance to say: "Got some rare things on sale, stranger" - a callback to the iconic RE4 Merchant. He immediately follows it with his normal dialogue as if nothing happened.

EASTER EGG 3

Save Room Piano Motif

The first Typewriter room in Chapter 1 plays a subtle 8-note piano motif lifted directly from the RE1 save room theme. Subsequent save rooms mix it with RE Requiem's original score, but the first one is pure RE1 nostalgia for longtime fans.

EASTER EGG 4

Leon's Original RE2 Jacket

In Chapter 8 (Dimensions Apart), examine Leon's locker in the RPD armory to find his original RPD jacket. This unlocks the Classic Leon costume for New Game Plus - the exact RE2 remake outfit, complete with accurate damage and weathering details from that game.

EASTER EGG 5

Wesker's Sunglasses

In Chapter 9 Forgotten City museum, a display case contains a pair of black sunglasses. The plaque reads "Donated by A.W. - 1998." Examining them triggers a brief flash of red eyes in the reflection. Unlocks the Wesker costume for HUNK mode in Extras.

EASTER EGG 6

The Keeper's Diary

Chapter 6 hospital records room contains a patient journal. Read the first entry and it begins with "May 9. Fever gone but itchy. Itchy. Tasty." - the exact words from the Keeper's Diary in the original Resident Evil. The full entry traces the patient's transformation into a zombie.

EASTER EGG 7

Boulder Punch Reference

In Chapter 10, Grace sarcastically asks Leon: "What are you going to do, punch through this wall?" Leon replies: "Do not tempt me." This is a direct callback to Chris Redfield's infamous boulder-punching scene from Resident Evil 5, which has become one of the franchise's most notorious memes.

EASTER EGG 8

HUNK's Theme in the Extraction

When the extraction helicopter arrives in Chapter 11, listen closely: the musical score subtly weaves HUNK's theme (Looming Dread) into the orchestra for exactly 8 seconds before transitioning back. It is easy to miss amid the chaos of the final escape sequence.

EASTER EGG 9

Dog Kennel Meta-Commentary

Examine the empty dog kennel in the Estate east garden (Chapter 2) five times in a row. On the fifth examination, Leon mutters: "At least there is no dog jumping through the window this time." A knowing wink to the iconic RE1 hallway dog scare that traumatized a generation of players.

EASTER EGG 10

Del Lago Lake Monster

Fire 10 or more rounds into the lake in Chapter 5 (Betrayal in the Dark). After the tenth shot, a massive fin briefly surfaces and submerges. Leon says nothing, but the camera lingers on the ripples for an uncomfortable 3 seconds. A clear callback to Del Lago from Resident Evil 4.

SECRET BOSS ENCOUNTERS

CHAPTER 8 - DIMENSIONS APART
HUNK (OPTIONAL BOSS FIGHT)
UNLOCK: Return to the armory after the Rift stabilizes

If you return to the RPD armory after the dimensional rift stabilizes but before exiting the chapter, HUNK appears as a hostile NPC. He fights with military precision: flashbangs, neck-snap attempts, and his signature TMP. Defeating him unlocks the HUNK Mask accessory (10 percent damage reduction) and the "The Fourth Survivor" achievement.

CHAPTER 12 - REQUIEM
AMALGAM PRIME (SUPERBOSS)
UNLOCK: Collect all 12 Lore Fragments plus complete all side missions

The ultimate secret boss. Appears during the credits sequence if you have collected every Lore Fragment and completed all 9 side missions (s01 through s09). Amalgam Prime is a nightmarish fusion of every major boss you have defeated, featuring 3 distinct phases and an enormous HP pool. Defeating it unlocks the True Ending cutscene.

THE GOLDEN EGG MYSTERY

Resident Evil fans know the Golden Egg is a beloved series tradition. In RE Requiem, the Golden Egg quest is the game's most elaborate Easter egg chain, spanning multiple chapters and rewarding dedicated hunters with one of the series' most ridiculous joke weapons.

GOLDEN EGG QUEST
THE FULL EASTER EGG CHAIN
  1. Chapter 6: Find Golden Egg 1 in the hospital kitchen chicken coop. Shoot the clucking chicken to make it drop the egg, then grab it before the chicken pecks you to death.
  2. Chapter 6: Place Golden Egg 1 on the hospital chapel offering plate. A secret compartment opens, revealing a Ruby treasure worth 8,000 Lei.
  3. Chapter 9: Find Golden Egg 2 inside the Forgotten King's sarcophagus after defeating him. Check the back of the sarcophagus - it blends into the gold trim.
  4. Chapter 11: Place both Golden Eggs on the twin pedestals in the Developer Room. The Egg Launcher descends from the ceiling in a beam of golden light.

Fun Fact: The Egg Launcher has appeared in multiple RE titles (RE4, RE5, RE2 Remake, RE3 Remake). In RE Requiem, it is the single most powerful weapon per-shot - but you only get 3 Golden Eggs per playthrough, and the launcher is NG+ only. Use them wisely.

SECRET DIALOGUE AND HIDDEN INTERACTIONS

DIALOGUE 1

Grace's "Master of Unlocking" Line

When Grace opens her very first locked door in Chapter 3, there is a 25 percent chance she will say: "Guess I am the master of unlocking now." A direct reference to Jill Valentine's famously awkward line from the original Resident Evil.

DIALOGUE 2

Ada Wong's File

In Chapter 7, a classified document in the laboratory mentions "Agent Wong" as having been assigned to a separate, unspecified operation. This confirms Ada Wong exists in the Requiem timeline and strongly suggests she may appear in DLC or a sequel.

DIALOGUE 3

The "Complete. Global. Saturation." Terminal

A computer terminal in Chapter 10 displays a scrolling log that briefly flashes the phrase "COMPLETE. GLOBAL. SATURATION." - Wesker's iconic line from RE5 - before the text corrupts into unreadable glyphs and the screen goes black.

DIALOGUE 4

Shooting the Photograph

In Chapter 2, the Estate library contains a framed photograph of the Spencer Mansion. Shoot it, and the glass cracks to reveal "STARS Office - 1998" written on the back. Leon comments: "Some things never stay buried."

THE TRUE ENDING PATH

The secret ending - officially called the "Requiem Ending" - is the canon conclusion to Resident Evil Requiem and sets up the next chapter in the series. Unlocking it requires completing almost everything the game has to offer.

TRUE ENDING CHECKLIST:

  1. Collect all 12 Lore Fragments across all chapters
  2. Complete all 9 side missions (s01 through s09) before starting Chapter 12
  3. Find the Developer Room keycard in Chapter 9 (archive drawer code: 1996)
  4. Defeat the optional HUNK boss encounter in Chapter 8
  5. Defeat Amalgam Prime in Chapter 12
  6. Choose "Refuse" when the entity offers you its bargain in the final scene

If all six conditions are met, the post-credits scene extends by 4 additional minutes, revealing Leon's true fate, Grace's hidden connection to the Umbrella legacy, and a teaser trailer for what appears to be the direct sequel. The community is still analyzing every frame of this ending for clues about the franchise's future.

NOTE: The True Ending is significantly harder than the normal path. Amalgam Prime has roughly 3 times the HP of the regular final boss and requires mastery of Perfect Dodge mechanics. We strongly recommend attempting this on NG+ with fully upgraded weapons and a stockpile of healing items.

FAQ: SECRETS IN RE REQUIEM

Q1

Can I find all secrets on my first playthrough?

Technically yes, but it is extremely difficult. Several secrets require backtracking with items from later chapters, and the Amalgam Prime boss demands NG+ level firepower to beat comfortably. Our recommendation: enjoy a blind first playthrough, then return with this guide for a dedicated 100 percent completion run.

Q2

Do secrets and unlockables carry over to New Game Plus?

Yes. All Lore Fragments, Mr. Raccoon figures, costumes, accessories, and secret weapon unlocks carry over to NG+. The Egg Launcher, HUNK Mask, and Primal Knife are available from the very start of your next playthrough.

Q3

Is the Developer Room actually worth finding?

Absolutely. Beyond the pure novelty, the Developer Room contains exclusive concept art you cannot see anywhere else, a character model viewer with every enemy and NPC, the full 47-track sound test, and a terminal that grants infinite ammo for one weapon of your choice. That last bonus alone makes the detour worthwhile.

Q4

What is the hardest secret to unlock?

The Primal Knife requires collecting all 16 Mr. Raccoon figures, several of which are located in one-time-only areas with no chapter select on a first playthrough. Missing even a single figure means restarting the entire game. Combat-wise, the True Ending (Amalgam Prime) is the hardest fight, but the Primal Knife is the most missable secret.

Q5

Are there any secrets that permanently change gameplay?

The Primal Knife (infinite durability, never breaks), Egg Launcher (999 damage per shot, NG+ only), HUNK Mask (10 percent damage reduction), and the Anti-Materiel Rifle (one-shot kills on standard enemies) all provide permanent gameplay advantages. The Developer Room infinite ammo terminal can only be used once per save file, so choose your weapon carefully.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem continues Capcom's proud tradition of burying incredible secrets for dedicated fans to discover. With over 40 Easter eggs, hidden weapons, secret boss encounters, and the elaborate True Ending quest chain, the game rewards exploration and multiple playthroughs more generously than any RE title since the original Resident Evil 4.

The Developer Room alone justifies the effort - it stands as one of the most elaborate developer tribute areas in franchise history, packed with concept art, a model viewer, and a genuinely touching message from the development team. The Golden Egg quest chain culminating in the absurd Egg Launcher is pure, unfiltered Resident Evil charm at its best. And the HUNK secret boss fight is arguably the toughest and most rewarding optional encounter in the entire game.
