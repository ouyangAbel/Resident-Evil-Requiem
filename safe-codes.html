RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM SAFE CODES: ALL LOCK COMBINATION SOLUTIONS

This comprehensive guide covers every safe code, lock combination, and puzzle solution in Resident Evil Requiem. We have organized them by chapter so you can quickly find the code you need, along with the exact location of each safe and what you will unlock inside.

Pro Tip: Many safes in RE Requiem can be missed permanently once you progress past certain story points. We recommend solving each safe as soon as you encounter it to avoid backtracking or missing unique items entirely.

ACT I SAFE CODES (CHAPTERS I–III)

Act I introduces you to the basics of safe-cracking in RE Requiem. These early safes contain essential supplies that will help you survive the opening chapters. Pay close attention to environmental clues — each code is hidden somewhere near its safe.

01
CHAPTER I • MAIN HALL
MAIN HALL DRAWER LOCK

The first safe you will encounter is located in the main hall of the Ashcroft Estate, on the ground floor. Look for a ornate wooden drawer near the grand staircase. The lock has a three-digit combination mechanism.

07-04-08

Where to find the clue: Examine the family portrait hanging above the fireplace in the adjacent living room. The date “July 4th, 2008” is inscribed on the back of the frame — this is the Ashcroft family reunion date.

Contents: 30 rounds of Handgun Ammo — a generous supply for the early game that will help you through the first Lycan encounter.

02
CHAPTER I • STUDY ROOM
STUDY ROOM SAFE

Hidden inside a bookshelf in the second-floor study, this wall-mounted safe is easy to miss. Push the third shelf from the left to reveal the safe door behind a false panel.

12-25-97

Where to find the clue: A torn diary page on the study desk mentions “Christmas Eve, 1997” as the date of a mysterious incident. This date is the combination.

Contents: A Green Herb and a First Aid Spray — critical healing supplies for the upcoming boss fight in Chapter III.

03
CHAPTER II • LIBRARY
LIBRARY LOCKBOX

The library contains a locked metal lockbox sitting on a reading table near the window. This is a key item — the Spare Key inside unlocks a shortcut in Chapter III that saves significant backtracking time.

03-31-66

Where to find the clue: Check the book spine of the red leather journal on the top shelf. The date “March 31, 1966” is embossed in gold lettering — the founding date of the Arklay Research Facility.

Contents: Spare Key (unlocks the basement shortcut) and 20 rounds of Rifle Ammo.

04
CHAPTER II • BEDROOM
BEDROOM CLOSET LOCK

Inside the master bedroom on the third floor, the walk-in closet contains a small floor safe. This is one of the most well-hidden safes in the game — you must crouch and look under the hanging clothes to spot it.

08-15-02

Where to find the clue: A photo frame on the bedroom nightstand shows a couple at a beach. The date stamp reads “August 15, 2002” — their anniversary.

Contents: 12 rounds of Magnum Ammo — an extremely valuable early-game find since Magnum weapons do not become available until Chapter V.

05
CHAPTER III • CELLAR
CELLAR CHEST LOCK

The wine cellar beneath the estate kitchen contains a rusted metal chest chained to a support beam. Cut the chain with your knife and enter the three-digit code to unlock it.

09-22-41

Where to find the clue: Scratched into the cellar wall near the furnace: “Sept 22, 1941 — the day everything changed.” This refers to the date Oswell Spencer first visited the Arklay Mountains.

Contents: 25 Shotgun Shells — essential for the Chapter III boss fight against the Bloated Lycan.

Reminder: The Spare Key from the Library Lockbox (Chapter II) opens a shortcut in the cellar that leads directly to the Chapter III boss arena. If you missed it, you will need to backtrack through the entire estate.

ACT II SAFE CODES (CHAPTERS IV–VI)

Act II takes you deeper into the conspiracy, and the safes become more challenging to locate. These codes guard weapon upgrades, key items, and emergency supplies that are critical for surviving the Act II boss gauntlet.

06
CHAPTER IV • OFFICE
OFFICE DESK SAFE

In the corporate office building that serves as the Umbrella subsidiary headquarters, a high-security desk safe sits behind the CEO's desk. This safe contains one of the most sought-after weapon modifications in the game.

11-08-53

Where to find the clue: A framed employee award on the wall reads “Outstanding Achievement — November 8, 1953” — the date the first T-Virus sample was synthesized. Rotate the award to see the full date.

Contents: Weapon Part: Extended Barrel — increases handgun range by 25% and improves accuracy at long distances. This modification can be applied to any handgun at a workbench.

07
CHAPTER V • LAB STORAGE
LAB STORAGE LOCKER

The underground laboratory storage room contains a combination locker that requires a four-digit code. This is the only four-digit safe in the game — all others use three digits.

06-17-89

Where to find the clue: A computer terminal in the adjacent control room displays a log entry dated “06/17/89” — the day the lab was officially decommissioned. The screen flickers, making it easy to miss if you are not paying attention.

Contents: Herb Recipe (allows you to combine two herbs into a more potent healing item) and a Blue Keycard that unlocks the secure weapons vault in Chapter VI.

08
CHAPTER VI • BOSS ARENA
BOSS ARENA EMERGENCY CACHE

Hidden behind a destructible wall in the Chapter VI boss arena, this emergency cache is designed to help players who are struggling with the Abomination fight. Break the cracked wall section on the left side of the arena to access it.

04-02-10

Where to find the clue: Emergency protocol signs on the arena walls read “Protocol 04-02-10: Open in case of containment failure.” The Abomination is the ultimate containment failure.

Contents: 3 First Aid Meds and 2 Frag Grenades — enough to recover from a near-death situation and deal significant damage to the boss.

ACT III SAFE CODES (CHAPTERS VII–IX)

Act III is where Resident Evil Requiem becomes truly intense. The safes in this act contain some of the rarest items in the game, including Lore Fragments required for the secret ending. Do not skip these.

09
CHAPTER VII • CONTROL ROOM
CONTROL ROOM PANEL

The control room in the dimensional rift facility contains a sealed electronic panel. Enter the code on the numeric keypad to unlock a hidden compartment containing critical endgame supplies.

14-33-77

Where to find the clue: Three separate documents scattered throughout Chapter VII each contain two digits: a shipping manifest (“14”), a lab report (“33”), and a personal letter (“77”). You must find all three to reconstruct the full code.

Contents: Electronics Key (unlocks the final weapons vault) and Flash Rounds — the only source of flash ammunition in the game, extremely effective against Shadow-type enemies.

10
CHAPTER VIII • SHADOW VAULT
SHADOW VAULT

This is the most hidden safe in the entire game. Located in a secret room accessible only through a puzzle involving light and shadow mechanics, the Shadow Vault contains one of the six Lore Fragments needed for the secret ending.

21-06-58

Where to find the clue: The code is split across three shadow puzzles. Each puzzle projects numbers onto the wall when solved correctly: “21” from the mirror puzzle, “06” from the candle puzzle, and “58” from the sundial puzzle.

Contents: Lore Fragment #4 — required for the secret ending. This fragment reveals the true origin of the dimensional rift and is one of the most important collectibles in the game.

Important: The six Lore Fragments are required to unlock the secret ending in Chapter XII. If you miss any fragment, you will need to replay that specific chapter using Chapter Select after your first playthrough. See our All Endings Explained guide for full details.

11
CHAPTER IX • UNDERGROUND BUNKER
UNDERGROUND BUNKER SAFE

The military-style underground bunker in the Forgotten City contains a heavy-duty safe bolted to the floor. This is one of the most challenging safes to reach due to the surrounding enemy density.

05-19-83

Where to find the clue: A military logbook on the bunker desk contains the entry “May 19, 1983 — Project REQUIEM initiated.” This date is also the code to the bunker's main door if you are exploring from a different angle.

Contents: Railgun Battery (required to power the experimental Railgun weapon) and 8 rounds of Magnum Ammo.

LATE-GAME SAFE CODES (CHAPTERS X–XII)

The final chapters of RE Requiem contain the last few safes. These contain powerful supplies that will help you survive the climactic boss fights and reach the endings.

12
CHAPTER X • DIMENSIONAL RIFT
DIMENSIONAL RIFT CACHE

In the merged dimensional landscape of Chapter X, a glowing cache appears in a hidden alcove during the bridge defense sequence. You must complete the defense event first, then explore the left side of the bridge to find it.

18-44-62

Where to find the clue: The numbers appear projected on the dimensional walls during the bridge defense: “18” flickers first, then “44” appears on the second wave, and “62” appears during the final wave. You must survive all three waves to see the full code.

Contents: 2 Ultimate Herbs — the most powerful healing items in the game, fully restoring health and providing temporary damage resistance.

13
CHAPTER XI • FINAL PREPARATIONS
FINAL PREPARATIONS SAFE

The last safe in the game is located in the safe room just before the Chapter XII final boss arena. This room serves as your final opportunity to prepare, and the safe contains supplies that can make or break the final fight.

01-13-99

Where to find the clue: The code is written on the wall behind the safe room's main desk, partially hidden by a torn poster. The numbers “01-13-99” refer to January 13, 1999 — the date of the Mansion Incident cover-up.

Contents: 5 Special Ammo Kits — each kit converts 10 rounds of standard ammunition into specialized rounds (Incendiary, Explosive, or Acid) depending on your equipped weapon.

TIPS FOR FINDING SAFE CODES

Resident Evil Requiem follows the classic RE tradition of hiding codes in environmental storytelling. Here are our top strategies for never missing a safe:

  • Read everything. Diary entries, logbooks, photo frames, and wall inscriptions all contain potential codes. If a number appears in the environment, write it down.
  • Examine items closely. Many clues are on the back of objects or inside drawers. Use the examine function on every item you pick up.
  • Listen for audio cues. When you are near a safe, you will hear a faint metallic clicking sound. This helps locate safes you might have walked past.
  • Check your map. Safes are marked with a small lock icon on your map after you discover them. If you see an icon you have not visited, go back.
  • Save before solving. Some safe contents vary slightly based on your difficulty level. Save first if you want to optimize your rewards.

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

Even experienced RE players can make errors when dealing with safes in RE Requiem. Here are the most common mistakes we see:

  1. Skipping the Chapter VIII Shadow Vault. This is the easiest Lore Fragment to miss, and without it you cannot access the secret ending. The shadow puzzles are optional but critical.
  2. Not finding the Chapter VII documents. The three-digit code for the control room panel is split across three separate documents. Many players find only one or two and assume the code is incomplete.
  3. Opening the Chapter VI cache too late. The emergency cache is only accessible during the boss fight. If you defeat the Abomination too quickly, the wall may not be breakable afterward.
  4. Using Magnum Ammo early. The Magnum Ammo from Chapter II is valuable, but Magnum weapons do not become available until Chapter V. Save it for the Act II bosses.
  5. Forgetting the Herb Recipe. The Herb Recipe from Chapter V allows you to craft superior healing items. Combine it with the Green Herbs from earlier safes for maximum efficiency.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM SAFE CODES

Q1

Are all safe codes fixed or do they change each playthrough?

All safe codes in Resident Evil Requiem are fixed and do not change between playthroughs. The codes listed in this guide will work every time, regardless of difficulty level or New Game+ status.

Q2

Can I miss safes permanently?

Yes, several safes can be missed permanently. The Chapter VIII Shadow Vault and the Chapter X Dimensional Rift Cache are the most commonly missed. However, after completing the game you unlock Chapter Select, which allows you to return to any chapter and open any safe you missed.

Q3

What is the most important safe to open?

The Chapter VIII Shadow Vault is the most important because it contains Lore Fragment #4, which is required for the secret ending. The Chapter XI Final Preparations Safe is also critical as it provides the Special Ammo Kits needed for the final boss.

Q4

Do safe contents change on higher difficulties?

The codes remain the same, but the contents may have reduced quantities on Hardcore and Nightmare difficulties. For example, the Main Hall Drawer gives 20 rounds of Handgun Ammo on Hardcore instead of 30. The weapons and key items remain unchanged.

Q5

Is there a safe that gives a weapon?

No safe directly contains a weapon. However, the Chapter IV Office Desk Safe gives the Extended Barrel weapon part, and the Chapter V Lab Storage Locker gives the Herb Recipe and Blue Keycard, which are essential for weapon upgrades and accessing the secure weapons vault.

Q6

How many total safes are in RE Requiem?

There are 13 safes in total across all 12 chapters. Chapters I and II each have two safes, while most other chapters have one. Chapter VIII's Shadow Vault is the most hidden and requires solving optional puzzles to access.
