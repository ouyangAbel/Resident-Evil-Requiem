ACT I — ECHOES OF THE PAST (CHAPTERS I–III)

The opening act of the Resident Evil Requiem mission list sets the tone for the entire game. Grace Ashcroft arrives at the abandoned Rookwood Estate, and the missions here blend exploration with resource gathering. The pacing is deliberate, giving you time to learn the mechanics while ramping up the tension.

01
Chapter I — Mission

ARRIVAL AT ROOKWOOD ESTATE

Objective: Enter the estate grounds, locate the main entrance, and investigate the security office.

Reward: Security Band Mk.1, Estate Map

This introductory mission teaches you the core movement and investigation mechanics. Explore the guardhouse thoroughly before heading inside — the shotgun shells and herb on the desk are easy to miss. The Security Band you receive here is your primary tool for the rest of the game, so familiarize yourself with its interface early.

Chapter I — Mission

THE WESTERN WING INVESTIGATION

Objective: Search the western wing for clues about Rookwood's experiments. Obtain the Library Key.

Reward: Library Key, First Lore Fragment (optional)

The western wing introduces locked doors and basic puzzle mechanics. The Library Key is mandatory, but the Lore Fragment hidden behind the painting puzzle in the study is optional — and critical for the secret ending. Search every room carefully before progressing.

Chapter II — Mission

ECHOES IN THE LIBRARY

Objective: Solve the library puzzle, access the underground passage, and retrieve Rookwood's journal.

Reward: Rookwood's Journal, Handgun Upgrade Part

The library puzzle is one of the first real brain-teasers. Arrange the books in the order shown on the stained glass window above the fireplace. Rookwood's journal entries provide essential backstory and clue you into the locations of several side missions.

Chapter III — Mission

THE ESTATE CELLAR — BLOATED LYCAN ENCOUNTER

Objective: Survive the cellar, defeat the Bloated Lycan, and escape to the grounds.

Reward: Shotgun, Cellar Safe Code

The first mandatory boss fight in the Resident Evil Requiem mission list. Stock up on shotgun shells and herbs before entering the cellar. The Bloated Lycan is weak to fire — use any Incendiary Rounds you have been saving. After the fight, grab the shotgun from the weapon rack. This is also where Leon's storyline begins to intertwine with Grace's.

ACT II — DESCENT INTO DARKNESS (CHAPTERS IV–VI)

Act II raises the stakes considerably. The missions become more complex, resources grow scarce, and the narrative takes several unexpected turns. This section of the Resident Evil Requiem mission list is where completionists need to pay close attention, as several missable objectives appear for the first time.

05
Chapter IV — Mission

GRACE'S HOSPITAL ESCAPE

Objective: Navigate Grace Ashcroft through St. Damien Hospital, evade HUNK, and locate the evidence locker.

Reward: Evidence Locker Keycard, Hospital Map, Medical Supplies

This is a stealth-heavy mission. HUNK patrols the corridors in a set pattern, and getting caught triggers a difficult chase sequence. Study his patrol routes from the security cameras before moving. The evidence locker contains a critical item for one of the game's multiple endings.

06
Chapter V — Mission

LEON'S DIRT BIKE PURSUIT

Objective: Chase Victor Gideon through the industrial district. Survive the motorcycle boss fight.

Reward: Leon's Combat Knife upgrade, Industrial District Key

A high-octane change of pace. This mission plays out as an action set-piece with Leon on a dirt bike. The Victor Gideon boss fight happens mid-chase and requires quick reflexes. Keep your health above 50% before triggering the encounter, as Gideon's attacks hit hard during the vehicle segments.

07
Chapter VI — Mission

THE ABOMINATION — LABORATORY SHOWDOWN

Objective: Infiltrate the underground laboratory and defeat the Abomination.

Reward: Magnum, Second Lore Fragment, Lab Access Codes

The hardest boss fight in Act II. The Abomination has three distinct phases, each requiring different tactics. Phase one is straightforward — shoot the glowing weak points. Phase two introduces acid pools you must dodge. Phase three is a damage race where the arena shrinks over time. Bring maximum ammunition and at least three First Aid Meds. The Lore Fragment drops after the fight but only if you destroyed all three weak points in phase one.

ACT III — THE FINAL RECKONING (CHAPTERS VII–XII)

The final act is where every thread of the Resident Evil Requiem mission list converges. Missions here are longer, more dangerous, and carry the highest narrative stakes. Two of the six Lore Fragments required for the secret ending are found in this act, and both are permanently missable if you advance past certain checkpoints.

08
Chapter VII — Mission

THE DIMENSIONAL RIFT

Objective: Survive the dimensional shift and navigate the warped version of the estate.

Reward: Rift Stabilizer, Dimensional Map

The dimensional rift transforms familiar environments into nightmarish versions of themselves. Map layouts are inverted, and new paths open while old ones close. The Rift Stabilizer you receive is used to anchor yourself during these shifts and becomes a key mechanic for the rest of the game.

Chapter VIII — Mission

SHADOW STALKER AMBUSH

Objective: Defeat the Shadow Stalker mini-boss and secure the catacombs entrance.

Reward: Third Lore Fragment, Catacombs Key

The Shadow Stalker is a fast, aggressive mini-boss that teleports between shadows. Use flash grenades to stun it and create openings. The Third Lore Fragment is found in the crypt behind the catacombs entrance — search the sarcophagus on the right side after the fight.

10
Chapter IX — Mission

THE FORGOTTEN CITY EXPLORATION

Objective: Explore the Forgotten City, collect all five city seals, and unlock the Temple Gate.

Reward: Temple Gate Key, Fourth Lore Fragment, Forgotten King encounter (optional)

The longest single mission in the game. The Forgotten City is an open explorable area with five city seals scattered across its districts. Each seal is guarded by a unique puzzle or mini-encounter. The optional Forgotten King boss rewards a powerful weapon upgrade, but requires significant resources to defeat. Budget at least 90 minutes for this mission on a first playthrough.

Chapter X–XI — Mission

THE FINAL DESCENT — AMALGAM BATTLE

Objective: Descend into the abyss, survive the gauntlet, and defeat the Amalgam.

Reward: Fifth Lore Fragment, Abyssal Weapon

Widely considered the hardest mission in the Resident Evil Requiem mission list. The Amalgam boss fight is a brutal multi-phase encounter with extremely tight resource economy. Fully upgrade your weapons before attempting this mission. The Fifth Lore Fragment is missable — you must examine the altar on the left side of the arena before the final phase begins. Once the phase starts, the altar becomes inaccessible permanently.

MISSABLE MISSIONS AND WARNINGS

Several missions in the Resident Evil Requiem mission list can be permanently missed if you advance past certain story checkpoints. Here are the key warnings every player should know:

  • The Gardener's Diary (Chapter I): Must be completed before entering the main hall. Once you trigger the cutscene with the butler, the garden gate locks permanently.
  • Rookwood's Secret Safe (Chapter II): The safe combination is found in the greenhouse. If you leave the estate grounds before finding it, the greenhouse becomes inaccessible.
  • The Nurse's Confession (Chapter IV): This side mission only triggers if you examined the nurse's station before encountering HUNK. It provides crucial context for the controversial Leon nurse scene.
  • Abyssal Altar (Chapter XI): As mentioned, the Lore Fragment here is missable during the Amalgam boss fight. Examine the altar on the left side before the final phase.
  • The Final Recording (Chapter XII): Available only if you have collected all five previous Lore Fragments. The recording appears in the final room before the last boss encounter.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM MISSION LIST

Q1

How many main missions are in Resident Evil Requiem?

There are 16 main missions spread across the game's 12 chapters. Some chapters contain a single mission, while others have two or three shorter objectives that flow together. The full Resident Evil Requiem mission list includes both main and side content for a total of 25 unique missions.

Q2

How many side missions are there?

There are 9 side missions in total. Six of them reward Lore Fragments, which are essential for the secret ending. The other three provide weapon upgrades, key items, and additional lore.

Q3

Can I replay missed missions?

After completing the game, Chapter Select becomes available from the main menu. You can replay any chapter to complete missed side missions. However, some missable objectives require starting from an earlier chapter checkpoint, as your actions in previous chapters affect later mission availability.

Q4

Which missions are required for the secret ending?

All six Lore Fragment side missions must be completed to unlock the secret ending. Additionally, you need to make specific dialogue choices in Chapters III, VI, and XII. See our All Endings Explained guide for exact requirements.

Q5

What is the hardest mission in the game?

Most players consider The Final Descent (Chapter XI) to be the hardest mission. The Amalgam boss fight has a brutal damage output and the resource economy is extremely tight. Fully upgrade your weapons and stock maximum supplies before attempting it.

Q6

How long does it take to complete every mission?

A first playthrough completing all main and side missions takes approximately 18–25 hours. Speedrunners focusing only on mandatory missions can finish in under 3 hours. A completionist run with all collectibles and secrets can exceed 30 hours.

The main missions deliver intense set-pieces and boss encounters, while the side missions add meaningful depth to the lore and directly impact which ending you receive.

Whether you are chasing the secret ending through all six Lore Fragment missions or simply working through the story at your own pace, this guide covers every objective you will encounter.
