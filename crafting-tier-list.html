RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CRAFTING TIER LIST: EVERY RECIPE RANKED FROM BEST TO WORST

AUGUST 01, 2026 • 18 MIN READ

SHARE

Crafting is one of the most powerful systems in Resident Evil Requiem, but not every recipe is created equal. With limited materials scattered across Rookwood Estate and Grace Hospital, knowing which recipes to prioritize — and which to skip entirely — can mean the difference between a smooth run and a frustrating grind. This crafting tier list ranks every recipe from S-tier must-craft to D-tier never-craft, based on material efficiency, DPS value, and situational utility across all difficulties.

Whether you're a first-time player looking to conserve resources or a Hardcore veteran optimizing every inventory slot, this guide will help you make smarter crafting decisions. We've tested every recipe across Standard, Hardcore, and Professional difficulties to bring you the definitive ranking. For a full breakdown of crafting basics and material locations, check out our complete crafting guide.

How We Ranked Each Recipe: S-tier = essential, craft immediately. A-tier = excellent value, craft often. B-tier = solid but situational. C-tier = niche use, craft sparingly. D-tier = waste of materials, skip entirely. Rankings account for material cost, output value, inventory efficiency, and difficulty scaling.

S-TIER: ESSENTIAL RECIPES (CRAFT IMMEDIATELY)

These recipes represent the absolute best value in the game. They provide maximum output for minimal material cost and should be your default crafting priority whenever you visit a typewriter bench.

1. Handgun Ammo (Gunpowder)

Materials: 2x Gunpowder → 15 Handgun Ammo
Why S-tier: Handgun ammo is the backbone of your entire arsenal. The standard handgun is available from Chapter 1 and remains viable through the entire campaign. Crafting 15 rounds from just 2 gunpowder is the most material-efficient recipe in the game. On Professional difficulty where ammo drops are scarce, this recipe becomes even more critical. Always craft when you have spare gunpowder.

2. First Aid Med (Green Herb)

Materials: 1x Green Herb → 1x First Aid Med
Why S-tier: Healing is non-negotiable, and the 1:1 herb-to-heal ratio is the most efficient healing recipe in the game. Unlike combining herbs for partial heals, a single green herb crafts into a full First Aid Med that restores 100% HP. Never waste green herbs on mixed combinations when you can craft full heals instead.

3. Shotgun Ammo (Gunpowder + Gunpowder)

Materials: 3x Gunpowder → 8 Shotgun Ammo
Why S-tier: The shotgun is your primary crowd-control weapon, and 8 shells from 3 gunpowder is exceptional value. Shotgun ammo drops are rare in later chapters, making crafting essential for boss fights and enemy clusters. The damage-per-material ratio is among the highest of any recipe.

Pro Tip: On Hardcore and Professional difficulties, prioritize shotgun ammo crafting before boss encounters. The Forgotten King and Final Boss fights reward heavy shotgun damage, and natural ammo drops won't cover the full fight.

A-TIER: EXCELLENT VALUE (CRAFT OFTEN)

These recipes offer strong value and should be crafted regularly, though they may not be as universally essential as S-tier options.

4. Enhanced Handgun Ammo (High-Grade Gunpowder)

Materials: 1x High-Grade Gunpowder → 10 Enhanced Handgun Ammo
Why A-tier: Enhanced handgun ammo deals roughly 40% more damage than standard rounds, making it devastating against armored enemies and boss weakpoints. The material cost is higher, but the DPS increase justifies the investment. Save high-grade gunpowder for boss fights and Professional difficulty encounters.

5. Explosive (Gunpowder + Scrap Metal)

Materials: 1x Gunpowder + 1x Scrap Metal → 1x Explosive
Why A-tier: Explosives deal massive AoE damage and can instantly kill groups of weaker enemies. They're also required for breaking cracked walls to access secret areas. The dual-purpose utility — combat and exploration — makes this recipe extremely valuable. Always keep 2-3 explosives in your inventory for hidden wall breaches.

6. Grenade (Gunpowder + Scrap Metal + Sulfur)

Materials: 1x Gunpowder + 1x Scrap Metal + 1x Sulfur → 1x Grenade
Why A-tier: Grenades deal even more damage than explosives and have a larger blast radius. The additional sulfur cost is the only thing keeping this from S-tier. Use grenades for emergency crowd control or to stagger bosses for free damage windows. The stun effect on larger enemies is invaluable on higher difficulties.

7. Mixed Herb (Green + Red)

Materials: 1x Green Herb + 1x Red Herb → 1x Enhanced First Aid Med
Why A-tier: While the green herb alone heals fully, the red herb combination provides a temporary defense boost for 30 seconds after healing. This defensive buff reduces incoming damage by 15%, making it the superior choice for boss fights and difficult encounters. The red herb has no other use, so combining it is always optimal.

B-TIER: SOLID BUT SITUATIONAL (CRAFT WHEN NEEDED)

These recipes have clear use cases but aren't universally valuable. Craft them when the situation demands it, but don't prioritize them over higher-tier options.

8. Rifle Ammo (High-Grade Gunpowder + Scrap Metal)

Materials: 2x High-Grade Gunpowder + 1x Scrap Metal → 6 Rifle Ammo
Why B-tier: The rifle is a powerful but niche weapon. Its slow fire rate and limited ammo capacity mean you won't use it as often as the handgun or shotgun. However, when you do need rifle ammo — particularly for long-range enemy weakpoints — this recipe delivers. The high material cost makes it a luxury rather than a staple.

9. Flash Grenade (Sulfur + Scrap Metal)

Materials: 1x Sulfur + 1x Scrap Metal → 1x Flash Grenade
Why B-tier: Flash grenades stun enemies and are particularly effective against Lycans and other fast-moving threats. However, the perfect dodge mechanic often provides similar stun windows without consuming materials. Craft flash grenades for group encounters where dodging isn't practical, or when facing the Shadow Stalker boss.

10. Mixed Herb (Green + Blue)

Materials: 1x Green Herb + 1x Blue Herb → 1x Antidote + Partial Heal
Why B-tier: Poison is a status effect that appears primarily in Chapters 7-9 and certain boss encounters. The blue herb alone cures poison, but combining it with green provides healing simultaneously. Only craft this when actively poisoned — otherwise, save blue herbs for standalone antidote use.

11. Magnum Ammo (High-Grade Gunpowder x2)

Materials: 2x High-Grade Gunpowder → 4 Magnum Ammo
Why B-tier: The magnum is the most powerful handgun in the game, but its ammo is extremely rare and the material cost is steep. Four rounds from two high-grade gunpowder feels underwhelming compared to other recipes. Only craft magnum ammo for the final boss fight or when you have a surplus of high-grade gunpowder.

Difficulty Note: On Standard difficulty, B-tier recipes feel more valuable because materials are plentiful. On Professional, every material counts — stick to S and A-tier recipes unless a specific situation demands otherwise.

C-TIER: NICHE USE (CRAFT SPARINGLY)

These recipes have very specific applications and are rarely worth the material investment. Craft them only when you have a clear, immediate need.

12. Enhanced Shotgun Ammo (High-Grade Gunpowder + Scrap)

Materials: 2x High-Grade Gunpowder + 1x Scrap Metal → 5 Enhanced Shotgun Ammo
Why C-tier: Enhanced shotgun shells deal bonus damage, but the material cost is nearly double the standard recipe for only a 25% damage increase. The efficiency loss is significant. Only craft these for the Final Boss fight on Professional difficulty where every point of damage matters.

13. Scrap Metal Combo (Scrap Metal x3)

Materials: 3x Scrap Metal → 1x Reinforced Explosive
Why C-tier: Reinforced explosives deal 50% more damage than standard explosives, but the triple scrap cost is steep. Standard explosives already kill most enemy groups, making the upgrade redundant. Save scrap metal for standard explosives and grenades instead.

14. Sulfur Combo (Sulfur x2 + Gunpowder)

Materials: 2x Sulfur + 1x Gunpowder → 1x Incendiary Grenade
Why C-tier: Incendiary grenades deal damage over time, which sounds useful but is outclassed by the instant damage of regular grenades. The DoT effect is too slow for most encounters, and the double sulfur cost is hard to justify. Skip this recipe unless you're facing enemies weak to fire.

D-TIER: SKIP ENTIRELY (WASTE OF MATERIALS)

These recipes offer poor value and should almost always be avoided. The material cost outweighs the benefit in virtually every situation.

15. Low-Grade Gunpowder (Scrap + Sulfur)

Materials: 1x Scrap Metal + 1x Sulfur → 1x Low-Grade Gunpowder
Why D-tier: This recipe converts two useful materials into a low-grade component that's less valuable than its inputs. Low-grade gunpowder is used in basic handgun ammo recipes, but you can find gunpowder directly throughout the game. Never craft low-grade gunpowder — it's a net loss of resources.

16. Mixed Herb (Green + Green + Red)

Materials: 2x Green Herb + 1x Red Herb → 1x Max Heal + Defense Buff
Why D-tier: While the defense buff is nice, using two green herbs for a single heal is wasteful. You'd be better off crafting two separate First Aid Meds from each green herb and saving the red herb for a mixed combo. The triple-herb cost is excessive for the output.

17. Scrap Metal (Scrap x2)

Materials: 2x Scrap Metal → 1x Reinforced Scrap
Why D-tier: Reinforced scrap is used in only two recipes in the entire game, both of which are C-tier or below. The conversion rate is poor, and scrap metal is better saved for explosives and grenades. This recipe exists to trap new players into wasting materials.

CRAFTING STRATEGY BY DIFFICULTY

Your crafting priorities should shift depending on which difficulty you're playing. Here's how to adapt your strategy:

Standard Difficulty

Materials are plentiful and enemies are forgiving. You can afford to craft B-tier and even C-tier recipes without major consequences. Focus on S-tier recipes for efficiency, but don't stress over optimal crafting. This is the best difficulty to experiment with different recipes and learn the system.

Hardcore Difficulty

Material drops are reduced by approximately 30%, and enemies hit harder. Stick to S-tier and A-tier recipes exclusively. Only craft B-tier recipes when facing a specific challenge (like a boss fight). Healing becomes more important — always keep at least 2 First Aid Meds crafted and ready.

Professional Difficulty

Every material counts. Drops are reduced by 50% compared to Standard, and enemies can kill you in just a few hits. Craft ONLY S-tier recipes unless a boss fight demands otherwise. Never craft C-tier or D-tier recipes — the material waste is too severe. Prioritize handgun ammo and healing above all else.

New Game Plus Tip: In NG+, material drops are even scarcer, but you carry over crafted items. Stock up on S-tier crafts before finishing your first playthrough to give yourself a head start on subsequent runs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the best crafting recipe in Resident Evil Requiem?

The handgun ammo recipe (2 Gunpowder → 15 rounds) is the best recipe in the game due to its unmatched material efficiency and universal utility. Handgun ammo is used throughout the entire campaign and is effective against every enemy type.

Should I craft healing items or buy them from the Merchant?

Crafting First Aid Meds from green herbs is always more cost-effective than buying them from the Merchant. Herbs are free and abundant, while the Merchant charges premium prices for healing items. Save your Lei for weapon upgrades and treasure maps instead.

Is it worth crafting explosives for secret areas?

Absolutely. Explosives are required to break cracked walls in several chapters, and these hidden areas contain valuable materials, weapon parts, and lore items. Always keep at least 2 explosives in your inventory when exploring new areas.

What happens if I craft a D-tier recipe by mistake?

Don't worry — crafting mistakes happen. The materials are gone, but you'll find more as you progress. The key is to learn from the mistake and avoid repeating it. On Standard difficulty, the impact is minimal. On Professional, it can be more punishing, but the game provides enough materials to recover.

Can I un-craft items to recover materials?

No, crafting is permanent in Resident Evil Requiem. Once you craft an item, the materials are consumed and cannot be recovered. This is why choosing the right recipes matters — especially on higher difficulties where materials are scarce.

Do crafting recipes change in New Game Plus?

The recipes themselves remain the same in NG+, but material drops become scarcer. The tier list rankings still apply — S-tier recipes remain the best choice. The main difference is that you'll need to be even more selective about what you craft.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Mastering the crafting system is one of the most impactful skills you can develop in Resident Evil Requiem. By focusing on S-tier and A-tier recipes, you'll maximize your resource efficiency and ensure you're always prepared for whatever Rookwood Estate throws at you. Remember: craft handgun ammo and healing items first, save high-grade materials for boss fights, and never waste resources on D-tier recipes.
