RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM MERCHANT GUIDE: ALL SHOP LOCATIONS, BEST BUYS & TREASURE VALUES

UPDATED JUNE 2026 • 18 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

The Merchant is one of the most important NPCs in Resident Evil Requiem. Scattered across safe rooms throughout Rookwood Estate, Grace Hospital, and the Forgotten City, the Merchant sells weapons, upgrades, supplies, and key items that can make or break your survival. Knowing when to buy, what to skip, and how to maximize your Peseta income is the difference between a smooth run and a desperate struggle.

This complete Resident Evil Requiem merchant guide covers every merchant location, a full price list for weapons and upgrades, treasure combination values, and expert buying strategies for every chapter. Whether you are on your first Standard run or pushing through Professional difficulty, this guide ensures every Peseta counts.

ALL MERCHANT LOCATIONS BY CHAPTER

The Merchant appears in safe rooms marked with a green flame on your map. He is always accessible between encounters and offers a growing inventory as you progress.

Chapter II
ROOKWOOD ESTATE BASEMENT
Location: Estate Basement Safe Room

First appearance. Limited inventory: SG-09 R ammo, Green Herbs, First Aid Meds, and basic handgun upgrades. Sell all small treasures here to fund your first weapon upgrade. The Red9 is available for purchase (12,000P) — buy it if you prefer raw damage over the SG-09 R's accuracy.

Chapter III
ROOKWOOD ESTATE LIBRARY
Location: Library Back Room Safe Room

Expanded inventory adds SR M1903 (bolt-action rifle) for 28,000P and shotgun shells. Purchase the rifle if you can afford it — long-range precision becomes essential in Chapter IV. W-870 shotgun available for 22,000P if not already found in Chapter II.

Chapter V
GRACE HOSPITAL ENTRANCE
Location: Hospital Reception Safe Room

Major inventory upgrade. Semi-Auto Rifle available for 48,000P — the most important purchase in the game. Also sells the Blacktail handgun (32,000P), First Aid Sprays, and mid-tier weapon upgrades. Start combining treasures before selling from this point onward.

Chapter VI
HOSPITAL UNDERGROUND LAB
Location: Laboratory Safe Room

Adds TMP SMG for 18,000P and TMP Stock (8,000P). Rifle and shotgun upgrade tiers unlock. Defense items and Flash Grenades become available. Sell combined treasures here to fund Semi-Auto Rifle upgrades.

Chapter VIII
THE CRIMSON WING
Location: Dimensional Hub Safe Room

Late-game inventory. Magnum ammo becomes available. Striker shotgun for 52,000P. High-tier upgrades for all weapon categories. Sell excess herbs and ammo you are not using to fund final weapon upgrades.

Chapter X
THE FORGOTTEN CITY
Location: City Center Safe Room

Full inventory. Broken Butterfly Magnum available for 78,000P (if missed from the Chapter IX optional boss). All exclusive weapon upgrades unlock once base stats are maxed. This is your last chance to buy supplies before the endgame gauntlet.

Chapter XII
FINAL DESCENT
Location: Pre-Arena Safe Room

Final merchant visit before the endgame. Full stock of all items. Sell everything you do not need for the final boss — excess herbs, unused weapon ammo, and spare treasures. Spend every last Peseta on Magnum ammo and First Aid Sprays.

WHAT TO BUY: PRIORITY LIST

Currency is limited, so spending priorities matter. Here is the optimal buying strategy ranked from most to least important:

PRIORITY 1

Semi-Auto Rifle (Chapter V — 48,000P)

The single most important purchase. The Semi-Auto Rifle is your primary weapon for the rest of the game. If you are short on Pesetas, sell all treasures (even uncombined ones) to afford this weapon.

PRIORITY 2

Rifle Power Upgrades (All Levels)

Every power upgrade reduces the number of shots per kill, saving ammo across the entire game. The compounding savings far exceed the Peseta cost.

PRIORITY 3

Shotgun Power Upgrades

Your shotgun is the crowd control weapon. Power upgrades ensure stagger on the first shot, preventing enemies from closing distance.

PRIORITY 4

Handgun Upgrades (Blacktail Recommended)

Upgrade your preferred handgun after the rifle and shotgun are invested. The Blacktail is the best all-around handgun, but the SG-09 R with its Exclusive upgrade becomes competitive.

PRIORITY 5

Capacity Upgrades (Strategic Timing)

Buy Capacity upgrades right before boss fights for the free magazine refill. This saves ammo and provides a combat boost at the perfect moment.

LOW PRIORITY

Supplies (Herbs, Ammo, First Aid)

Only buy supplies when critically low. The game's adaptive difficulty provides more drops when you are struggling. Buying ammo at full price is rarely efficient compared to upgrading your weapons.

WHAT TO SELL & WHEN

Knowing what to sell is just as important as knowing what to buy.

  • Sell immediately: Individual small treasures (Small Pearl, Gold Ring) unless you have their combination partner
  • Hold and combine: All gemstones (Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire) — always wait for their matching mount
  • Sell excess ammo: If you have 60+ handgun rounds and are not using them, sell 20-30 to fund upgrades
  • Sell excess herbs: If you have 4+ Green Herbs, sell 1-2. You rarely need more than 3 combined healing items
  • Never sell: Weapon parts, key items, or unique treasures (Elegant Crown, Royal Crown components)

Chapter XII Clearance Sale: Before the final boss, sell everything you will not use: excess ammo for secondary weapons, spare herbs, and any remaining treasures. Convert every Peseta into Magnum ammo and First Aid Sprays. There is nothing left to save for.

MONEY-MAKING TIPS

  • Break everything. Crates, barrels, vases, and destructible objects contain Pesetas and treasures. Many players miss hidden crates behind furniture.
  • Complete side missions. The 9 side missions reward 3,000-8,000 Pesetas each. See our Mission List for details.
  • Revisit cleared rooms. Some treasures only appear after certain story triggers. Return to earlier chapters via Chapter Select to collect missed treasures.
  • Use the knife on crates. Melee attacks on breakable objects cost zero ammo. Knife every crate you see.
  • Sell at the right time. Sell treasures at the Merchant immediately before purchasing upgrades. This ensures you always have maximum buying power when it counts.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM MERCHANT

Q1

Where is the Merchant in Resident Evil Requiem?

The Merchant appears in safe rooms throughout the game. He is first encountered in Chapter II at the Rookwood Estate basement and appears in every subsequent chapter's safe room. His location is marked with a green flame icon on your map.

Q2

What should I buy first from the Merchant?

Prioritize the Semi-Auto Rifle (Chapter V, 48,000P), then invest in Rifle Power upgrades, then Shotgun Power upgrades. Buy the Semi-Auto Rifle even if you must sell all your treasures to afford it — it is the best investment in the game.

Q3

How do I get more Pesetas?

Collect and sell treasures (combine before selling for higher value), complete side missions for bonus rewards, break all destructible objects, and sell excess ammo and herbs you do not need. Avoid buying unnecessary supplies to preserve your budget for weapon upgrades.

Q4

Which treasures should I combine before selling?

Always combine: Ruby + Gold Ring (10,000P), Emerald + Gold Ingot (30,000P), Sapphire + Ornate Necklace (32,000P). The best combination is Ruby + Emerald + Gold Ingot = Jeweled Ingot (50,000P). Never sell individual gemstones when a combination partner is available.

Q5

Should I buy weapons or find them in the world?

Several weapons are available for free: the W-870 shotgun is found in Chapter II's estate armory, and the Broken Butterfly drops from the Chapter IX optional boss. Always check for free pickups before purchasing. Buy only the Semi-Auto Rifle and Blacktail, as these have no free alternatives.

Q6

Does the Merchant carry over to New Game Plus?

Yes. The Merchant appears at all the same locations in NG+ with his full inventory available from the start. All your weapon upgrades carry over, so you only need to buy new ammo and supplies. Use NG+ runs to fully upgrade weapons you missed on your first playthrough.

FINAL THOUGHTS

The Merchant is your lifeline in Resident Evil Requiem, but only if you use him wisely. Prioritize weapon upgrades over supplies, combine every treasure before selling, and time your Capacity purchases before boss fights for free refills. A smart economy strategy turns a resource-starved nightmare into a manageable survival experience.

Remember the core principle: upgrades are permanent investments, while supplies are temporary consumption. Every Peseta spent on a weapon upgrade pays dividends for the rest of the game. Every Peseta spent on ammo is gone after a single magazine. Spend accordingly.
