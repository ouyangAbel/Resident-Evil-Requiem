Resident Evil Requiem is one of the most lore-dense entries in franchise history, scattering its story across 47 documents, 12 chapters of cutscenes, audio logs, environmental details, and hidden files. Piecing together the complete chronological timeline requires detective work — many documents are undated, many cutscenes reference events obliquely, and the game deliberately obscures the order of key revelations to preserve its mysteries.

This guide assembles the definitive Resident Evil Requiem lore timeline, from the earliest historical events referenced in documents to the game's final moments in 2026. Every dated entry, every document reference, every cutscene implication has been cross-referenced and placed in chronological order. Whether you are a lore veteran seeking to fill gaps or a newcomer who wants the full picture before diving into NG+, this is your comprehensive reference.

HOW THIS TIMELINE WAS BUILT: This timeline combines in-game document dates, cutscene dialogue, environmental storytelling, developer interviews, and logical inference. Where dates are explicitly stated in documents, those are used. Where dates are implied by context (e.g., a document referencing "the Spencer Mansion incident" must be post-1998), the earliest possible date is given. Confirmed dates from developer commentary are marked with an asterisk (*).

The roots of RE Requiem's conflict stretch back to the founding of the Umbrella Corporation. Multiple documents in the game — particularly the Rookwood Estate journals and Umbrella Reconstruction files — reference this era extensively. Understanding these early events is essential for grasping why the Rookwood Estate exists and what Lord Rookwood's true purpose was.

The George Trevor Connection

One of the most fascinating revelations in RE Requiem's lore is that the Rookwood Estate was designed by George Trevor — the same architect who designed the Spencer Mansion in the Arklay Mountains. This is not a coincidence. The Trevor family had deep ties to Spencer's eugenics research, and George Trevor was specifically chosen because his architectural style incorporated hidden passages, secret laboratories, and reinforced underground chambers perfect for containing bioweapons research.

A document found in Chapter 4 (the Architecture Blueprint) shows that the Rookwood Estate's underground tunnels were designed to the same specifications as the Spencer Mansion's secret labs. This means Spencer and Rookwood were planning the estate as a backup research site from the beginning — a contingency in case the Arklay facility was compromised. The 1998 Spencer Mansion incident proved this paranoia justified.

ERA 2: THE COMPOUND 11 PROGRAM (1998–2015)

After the Spencer Mansion disaster and Umbrella's official dissolution in 2003, the Compound 11 program did not end — it went deeper underground. This era covers the secret continuation of Umbrella's research through shell companies and private facilities, culminating in the creation of the Convergence network.

The Grace Ashcroft Origin

The most significant revelation in this era is the true origin of Grace Ashcroft. She is not merely a CDC operative who was experimented on — she is the first successful Compound 11 human, born in 2014 from a genetically engineered embryo. The documents reveal that Convergence Biotech raised Grace within a carefully controlled foster environment, monitoring her development through staged "routine medical checkups" that were actually data collection sessions.

By the time Grace was recruited by what she believed was the CDC (actually a Convergence front organization) at age 22, she had no knowledge of her true nature. Her "CDC training" was a cover story designed to place her in the field where her Compound 11 enhancements would activate under stress — which is exactly what happens during the events of RE Requiem. The timeline confirms that Grace's entire life was engineered to produce the perfect Compound 11 host.

Leon's Path to the Rookwood Estate

The timeline reveals that Leon's involvement was not random. His government file (Chapter 7) shows that he had been tracking Umbrella successor organizations for three years before the game's events. His handler's briefing in the Prologue explicitly states that intelligence placed "Umbrella-level biotech activity" at the Rookwood Estate — information that Convergence may have deliberately leaked to draw Leon into a trap. The question of whether Leon was sent to stop the conspiracy or to become another test subject for Compound 11 is one of the game's most disturbing implications.

FRANCHISE CONNECTIONS: RE REQUIEM IN THE RE UNIVERSE

RE Requiem does not exist in isolation — it is deeply connected to every previous Resident Evil game. Here is how the timeline integrates with the wider franchise:

Connection to Resident Evil 1 (1998)

The Rookwood Estate was designed by George Trevor as a backup to the Spencer Mansion labs. The 1998 incident directly caused Rookwood to cut ties with Umbrella, accelerating the Compound 11 program's independence.

Connection to Resident Evil 2 Remake (1998/2022)

Chapter 8's dimensional rift deposits Leon in a corrupted RPD building. The reversed audio clips from RE2 Remake are not random — they represent the franchise's timeline fracturing under Compound 11's reality-warping effects.

Connection to Resident Evil 4 (2004/2023)

The Merchant's cryptic references to "Umbrella's grandchildren" in RE4 take on new meaning in light of RE Requiem's revelations about Convergence Biotech. The Merchant may have been aware of the Compound 11 program all along.

Connection to Resident Evil 5 (2009)

Dr. Isaacs is confirmed to be the same researcher mentioned in RE5's files. Compound 11 is explicitly described as "Uroboros refined" — the next generation of Wesker's forced evolution concept. RE Requiem is the thematic conclusion to Wesker's original vision.

Connection to Resident Evil 7 & Village (2017/2021)

The mold-based bioweapons in RE7 and Village represent an alternative evolutionary path to Compound 11 — biological vs. genetic modification. Documents in RE Requiem suggest Convergence monitored both programs, learning from each to refine Compound 11.

FAQ: RE REQUIEM LORE TIMELINE

Q1

How long is the Resident Evil Requiem timeline?

The complete RE Requiem lore timeline spans 59 years, from Umbrella's founding in 1967 to the game's events in 2026. This includes 28 years of Umbrella-era history (1967–2003), 12 years of the secret Compound 11 program (2003–2015), and 11 years of Convergence Network operations (2015–2026).

Q2

Is Dr. Isaacs the same character from Resident Evil 5?

Yes. Dr. James Isaacs is the Umbrella researcher first mentioned in RE5's laboratory files. RE Requiem confirms he survived the RE5 incident and continued his research for another 17 years, self-administering Compound 11 variants that kept him alive until age 89. His character arc from RE5 to RE Requiem represents the full evolution of Umbrella's ideology.

Q3

How does Grace Ashcroft fit into the timeline?

Grace is the first successful Compound 11 human, born in 2014 from a genetically engineered embryo. She was raised in a controlled foster environment, monitored through fake medical checkups, and recruited by a Convergence front organization at age 22. Her entire life was designed to produce the perfect Compound 11 host — making her both the program's greatest achievement and its greatest victim.

Q4

What is the "Umbrella Restitution Project"?

Referenced in the Prologue briefing, the Umbrella Restitution Project was the 2004 initiative by former Umbrella executives to restructure the corporation under shell companies. This is the origin of Convergence Biotech and the broader network that funded Compound 11 research for two decades. It represents Umbrella's corporate resurrection — the idea that the corporation was never truly destroyed, only reorganized.

Q5

Are there any timeline contradictions between documents?

Yes — intentionally. Several documents contain conflicting dates and details, reflecting the unreliable nature of classified biotech records. The most notable contradiction involves Dr. Isaacs' age: his medical log states he was born in 1936 (making him 90 in 2026), but a 2006 audio log references him as "sixty-nine years old" (implying a 1937 birth year). This one-year discrepancy has fueled fan theories about whether there are two Dr. Isaacs — a father and son — or whether the records were deliberately falsified.

Q6

What does PROJECT UMBR-2 mean for the timeline?

The 3-frame hidden text in the True Ending — "PROJECT UMBR-2 — INITIATED — 2026" — suggests that the events of RE Requiem were merely the first phase of a larger plan. "UMBR-2" appears to be the sequel to the original Umbrella program, using Compound 11 as its foundation. If this timeline continues, future games could explore Compound 11's deployment on a global scale.

FINAL THOUGHTS: THE WEIGHT OF HISTORY

The Resident Evil Requiem lore timeline is more than a collection of dates — it is a chronicle of humanity's repeated failure to learn from its bioweapons past. From Umbrella's founding in 1967 to Convergence Biotech's Compound 11 in 2026, the same pattern recurs: brilliant scientists convinced they are saving humanity, corporate structures that enable unchecked experimentation, and innocent people — like Grace Ashcroft — who pay the price for others' ambitions.

What makes RE Requiem's timeline unique in the franchise is its insistence that history is inescapable. Leon cannot escape the shadow of Raccoon City. Grace cannot escape her engineered origins. The dimensional rift in Chapter 8 is the ultimate expression of this theme: the franchise's past literally breaking into its present, forcing both characters and players to confront what came before.

The timeline ends in 2026 with the tease of PROJECT UMBR-2, but the story it tells is one that began 59 years earlier. Whether RE Requiem is the end of the Umbrella saga or merely the beginning of its next chapter, one thing is certain: the lore timeline of Resident Evil is far from complete.
