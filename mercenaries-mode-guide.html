RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM MERCENARIES MODE GUIDE: CHARACTERS, STAGES, SCORING & REWARDS

JULY 31, 2026 • 22 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Resident Evil Requiem's Mercenaries mode is one of the most addictive bonus modes in the entire franchise — a score-attack arcade experience that strips away the survival horror tension and replaces it with pure, adrenaline-fueled combat. Originally introduced in Resident Evil 3 and refined through RE4, RE5, and RE6, the Mercenaries concept returns in RE Requiem with its most ambitious iteration yet.

Whether you're chasing high scores, unlocking bonus content, or simply want to sharpen your combat skills, this guide covers everything you need to know. We break down all 6 playable characters, analyze every 8 stages, explain the combo scoring system, and share expert strategies to push your scores into the S-rank territory.

WHAT IS MERCENARIES MODE?

Mercenaries mode is a standalone score-attack mode separate from the main campaign. You select a character and stage, then fight through waves of enemies within a time limit. The goal is simple: kill enemies, build combos, and rack up the highest score possible before time runs out.

Unlike the main game's resource management focus, Mercenaries gives you generous ammo drops and encourages aggressive, stylish play. Chaining kills without taking damage builds a combo multiplier that dramatically increases your score. The mode is pure arcade fun — but mastering it requires deep knowledge of enemy patterns, stage layouts, and character abilities.

Unlock Requirement: Mercenaries mode becomes available after completing the main story on any difficulty. Once unlocked, it persists across all save files and New Game Plus runs.

CHARACTER ROSTER & ABILITIES

RE Requiem launches with 6 playable characters in Mercenaries mode, each with unique weapons, special abilities, and combat roles. Understanding each character's strengths is essential for maximizing your score potential on different stages.

LEON S. KENNEDY
BALANCED / STARTER CHARACTER

Leon is the most versatile character and the easiest for newcomers. His loadout includes a pistol, shotgun, and knife combo. His special ability "Combat Instinct" briefly slows time after a perfect dodge, giving you free hits on nearby enemies. Leon excels in sustained combos and has forgiving stats across the board.

ADA WONG
HIGH SCORING / GRAPPLE SPECIALIST

Ada is the top-tier choice for score chasers. Her grapple gun lets her zip across the map and extend combo windows by pulling enemies together. Her crossbow deals massive damage per shot. Ada's low health is offset by her mobility — she can evade almost any attack and reposition instantly.

HUNK
TANK / MELEE SPECIALIST

Unlocked by achieving S+ rank on Professional difficulty, HUNK is a melee powerhouse. His "Reaper" ability instakills standard enemies with his knife and grants temporary invincibility on kill. HUNK has the highest health pool but limited ranged options, making him challenging but devastating in skilled hands.

JILL VALENTINE
CROWD CONTROL / TACTICAL

Jill brings gadgets to the fight. Her "Lockdown Mine" deploys an area-of-effect explosive that stuns groups of enemies, creating combo opportunities. Her utility pistol and grenade launcher provide solid ranged damage. Jill is ideal for players who prefer controlled, tactical engagements over aggressive rushing.

CHRIS REDFIELD
POWER / SHOTGUN SPECIALIST

Chris is the brute-force option. His "Magnum Cannon" ability fires a devastating single shot that pierces through multiple enemies. His high stagger power lets him interrupt enemy attacks easily. Chris has slow movement but dominates close-quarters combat with his upgraded shotgun.

CLAIRE REDFIELD
VERSATILE / SUBMACHINE GUN

Claire bridges the gap between Leon and Chris. Her submachine gun provides rapid fire for crowd control, while her "Adrenaline Rush" ability temporarily boosts fire rate and reload speed. She's a solid all-rounder who performs well on every stage type.

Pro Tip: Start with Leon to learn stage layouts and enemy spawns. Once comfortable, switch to Ada for score records — her grapple combo potential is unmatched for high-score runs.

SCORING SYSTEM & COMBO MECHANICS

Understanding the scoring system is crucial for Mercenaries success. Your final score is calculated from three components: kill points, combo multiplier, and time bonus.

Kill Points

Each enemy type awards a base score on kill. Standard enemies give 100 points, elite variants award 500, and boss-type enemies grant 2,000. Headshots add a 50% bonus, and environmental kills (explosive barrels, electrical traps) add 100% bonus.

Combo Multiplier

The combo system is the heart of Mercenaries scoring. Every kill within 3 seconds of the previous one increases your combo counter. The multiplier scales as follows:

Combo Count Multiplier Strategy Tip
0-9 kills1xFocus on survival, not combos
10-24 kills1.5xStart chaining consistently
25-49 kills2xUse environmental kills to maintain chain
50-74 kills3xActivate character abilities for crowd clear
75+ kills5xMaximum score potential — don't get hit!

Time Bonus

Completing a stage awards time bonus points based on remaining seconds. Each remaining second adds 100 points to your total. Fast clears with high combos are the key to S-rank scores.

SCORING TIP

NEVER BREAK YOUR COMBO

A 50-kill combo at 3x multiplier earns 15,000 points from kills alone. The same kills without combo earn only 5,000. If your combo is high, prioritize safety over aggression — a dead mercenary scores zero. Use defensive items and evasion to preserve your chain.

STAGE GUIDES

Mercenaries mode features 8 unique stages, each adapted from campaign locations with modified enemy placements and time limits. Here is a breakdown of each stage and strategies for high scores.

STAGE 01

ROOKWOOD ESTATE GROUNDS

Time Limit: 3:00 | Enemy Count: 45 | Best Character: Leon or Jill

An open outdoor area with multiple enemy spawn points. The key is to patrol the perimeter and use explosive barrels scattered around the mansion. Central fountain area is a death trap — avoid being surrounded. Jill's mines work wonders here for crowd control.

STAGE 02

GRACE HOSPITAL CORRIDORS

Time Limit: 2:30 | Enemy Count: 35 | Best Character: Chris or Claire

Tight corridors favor shotguns and SMGs. Enemies burst through walls and doors — listen for audio cues. Chris dominates here with his cannon ability clearing entire hallways. Save your special ability for the elite Molded spawn at 1:30.

STAGE 03

ABOMINATION LABORATORY

Time Limit: 4:00 | Enemy Count: 60 | Best Character: Ada or HUNK

The largest stage with vertical elements. Ada's grapple lets her traverse quickly between floors. The lab equipment explodes when shot — chain these for combo preservation. Boss-type Amalgam spawns at 2:00 — focus fire immediately.

STAGE 04

FORGOTTEN CITY RUINS

Time Limit: 3:30 | Enemy Count: 50 | Best Character: Leon or Ada

Urban ruins with multiple levels and sniping positions. Enemies drop from above — watch the rooftops. Use narrow alleys to funnel enemies into single-file, then chain headshots. Time your special ability for the 2:00 swarm event.

STAGE 05

CELLAR CATACOMBS

Time Limit: 2:45 | Enemy Count: 40 | Best Character: Chris

Underground tunnels with very limited visibility. Enemies lurk in alcoves — clear methodically. Chris's high stagger and cannon ability dominate these tight spaces. Bring a flashlight for hidden item caches that grant score boosts.

STAGE 06

DIMENSIONAL RIFT ARENA

Time Limit: 3:00 | Enemy Count: 55 | Best Character: HUNK or Ada

A surreal arena that shifts gravity periodically. Enemies teleport in — there's no predictable spawn pattern. HUNK's instakill ability is perfect here since enemies materialize at close range. Save your ultimate for the elite wave at 1:45.

STAGE 07

MERCHANT'S HIDEOUT

Time Limit: 3:15 | Enemy Count: 48 | Best Character: Claire or Jill

A shop-like environment with crates and counters. Treasures scattered around grant score multipliers when collected. Jill's area denial excels here — place mines at chokepoints. The Merchant himself occasionally appears and drops bonus items.

STAGE 08

THE FINAL DESCENT

Time Limit: 5:00 | Enemy Count: 75 | Best Character: Ada

The ultimate test. A gauntlet combining elements from all previous stages with escalating difficulty. Ada's combo-extending grapple is essential for maintaining multiplier through the final swarm. Every ability cooldown matters — time them for elite waves at 2:00 and 4:00.

ADVANCED STRATEGIES FOR HIGH SCORES

1. Environmental Kill Chains

Explosive barrels and electrical hazards don't just deal damage — they extend your combo timer. When your combo is at risk of breaking, shoot a barrel near enemies to refresh the chain. Each environmental kill resets the 3-second combo window.

2. Defensive Item Timing

Grace items and flash grenades aren't just defensive tools — they're combo preservatives. When surrounded, a flash round stuns all nearby enemies, giving you free kills without breaking your chain. Always carry at least 2 defensive items.

3. Ability Cooldown Management

Each character's special ability has a cooldown. Use abilities strategically for elite waves rather than wasting them on single enemies. Ada's grapple, for example, can pull 5+ enemies together for a massive combo spike.

4. Audio Cue Mastery

Enemy spawn sounds are directional and distinct. Elite enemies have a unique audio tell — a deep growl before they appear. Learn these sounds to prepare for tough encounters and maintain your combo through awareness rather than reaction.

5. The "No Damage" Bonus

Completing a stage without taking damage awards a 2x score bonus to your final total. This is the single biggest score multiplier in the game. Practice defensively — perfect dodges and positioning are more valuable than aggressive play for record scores.

Score Record Tip: The world record scores always combine a 50+ kill combo (3x multiplier) with a no-damage clear (2x multiplier) and maximum time bonus. That's a 6x total multiplier on your base kill points — the difference between A-rank and S-rank.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

How do I unlock HUNK for Mercenaries mode?

HUNK unlocks by achieving S+ rank on Professional difficulty in the main campaign. This requires a sub-3-hour clear, fewer than 5 Ink Ribbons used, and keeping your partner alive. Once unlocked, HUNK is available in Mercenaries immediately.

Q2

Do Mercenaries scores carry over between characters?

No. Each character has separate stage scores and records. However, cumulative rewards (like total score unlocks) count across all characters.

Q3

What is the best stage for farming high scores?

Abomination Laboratory (Stage 3) is generally considered the best for score farming due to its high enemy count and environmental hazards. With Ada, you can easily score 150,000+ points here.

Q4

Can I play Mercenaries co-op with a friend?

Yes! Mercenaries mode supports 2-player online co-op. Combo counters are shared between players, making coordinated play extremely rewarding. Use the in-game lobby system or direct invite to play together.

Q5

Does difficulty affect Mercenaries mode?

No. Mercenaries has its own fixed difficulty scaling based on stage selection. However, your character unlocks and abilities from the main campaign do carry over — so progressing through harder campaign difficulties indirectly improves your Mercenaries performance.

Q6

What is the Golden Egg Launcher?

The Golden Egg Launcher is a secret weapon unlocked by reaching 1 million cumulative Mercenaries score. It fires explosive golden eggs and is one of the strongest weapons in campaign mode — essentially a reward for Mercenaries mastery.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem's Mercenaries mode is a fantastic bonus that adds dozens of hours of replayability beyond the main campaign. Whether you're chasing S-rank scores, unlocking bonus content, or just want to unleash pure combat chaos, there is something here for everyone.

The mode also serves as excellent combat training. Skills you develop in Mercenaries — perfect dodges, combo timing, ability management — directly translate to campaign performance. Many players report that practicing in Mercenaries made their Professional difficulty runs significantly smoother.
