RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM TROPHIES & ACHIEVEMENTS GUIDE

Platinum Trophy Overview: Resident Evil Requiem features 52 trophies total (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 11 Silver, 38 Bronze). Estimated platinum time: 40-60 hours across 2-3 playthroughs. No online trophies. All difficulty trophies stack.

Resident Evil Requiem offers a challenging but rewarding trophy list for completionists. With 52 trophies spanning story progression, combat mastery, collectibles, and hidden challenges, earning the platinum requires thorough exploration and multiple playthroughs. This guide covers every trophy, highlights missable ones, and provides an optimized roadmap to 100% completion.

PLATINUM ROADMAP

To efficiently earn every trophy, we recommend a structured 2-3 playthrough approach. Planning ahead will save you significant time and prevent frustrating replays.

PLAYTHROUGH 1

Story + Full Completion (Standard Difficulty)

Play on Standard difficulty while collecting everything. Use our Safe Codes Guide for all 13 safes, find all 6 Lore Fragments for the secret ending, collect all 30 Files, and complete every side mission. This playthrough earns roughly 70% of all trophies. Take your time and explore thoroughly.

PLAYTHROUGH 2

Nightmare Difficulty Run

Complete the game on Nightmare difficulty with your fully upgraded weapons from Playthrough 1. Nightmare unlocks the Hardcore and Standard difficulty trophies automatically. Focus on survival and efficient progression.

PLAYTHROUGH 3 (Optional)

Speed Run & Cleanup

Only needed if you missed the speed run trophy or specific combat challenges. With endgame weapons, a sub-4-hour run is very achievable. Clean up any remaining miscellaneous trophies.

MISSABLE TROPHIES WARNING

Critical: Several trophies can be permanently missed if you pass certain story checkpoints. The most important are: Lore Master (all 6 Lore Fragments), Shadow Vault (Chapter VIII only), No Stone Unturned (all collectibles in one playthrough), and Truth Seeker (secret ending). Follow a collectible guide from the start of your first playthrough.

TROPHY FAQ

Q1

Do difficulty trophies stack?

Yes. Completing the game on Nightmare awards all three difficulty trophies (Standard, Hardcore, Nightmare). You do not need separate playthroughs for each.

Q2

Can I earn trophies in New Game+?

Yes, most trophies work in New Game+. However, the Speed Demon trophy (sub-4-hour completion) must be done in a fresh save file, not New Game+.

Q3

What is the hardest trophy?

Most players find Nightmare Conqueror and Minimalist (handgun/knife only run) to be the hardest. Nightmare tests your resource management, while Minimalist requires mastering enemy patterns without powerful weapons.

Q4

Can I use Chapter Select for missed collectibles?

Yes, Chapter Select unlocks after your first playthrough. However, for the No Stone Unturned trophy, all collectibles must be found in a single continuous playthrough. Chapter Select does not count toward this trophy.

Q5

How many playthroughs minimum for platinum?

Two playthroughs is the minimum: one on Standard for full completion, one on Nightmare for difficulty trophies. If you miss the speed run trophy, a third quick run is needed.

FINAL THOUGHTS

The trophy list in Resident Evil Requiem is one of the most balanced in the series. It rewards thorough exploration, combat mastery, and multiple playthroughs without requiring extreme challenges or frustrating RNG. By following this roadmap and using our companion guides, you can efficiently earn the platinum trophy while experiencing everything the game has to offer.

Start with a thorough Standard playthrough collecting everything, then take on Nightmare with your upgraded arsenal. The most important trophies to track from the beginning are the collectible-related ones — especially the 6 Lore Fragments and the Shadow Vault puzzle in Chapter VIII. Miss these, and you will need another full playthrough.
