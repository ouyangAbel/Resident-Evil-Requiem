RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM ROOM-BY-ROOM ITEM LOCATIONS: COMPLETE ITEM MAP GUIDE

UPDATED JULY 16, 2026 • 22 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Finding every item in Resident Evil Requiem can mean the difference between surviving Professional difficulty and running dry before the final boss. Whether you are hunting for the 68 collectibles, scouring rooms for hidden herbs, or trying to maximize your Lei income, a room-by-room item guide eliminates the guesswork. This comprehensive map covers every pickupable item in all 12 chapters, organized by room so you never miss a single herb, ammo box, treasure, or key item.

If you have already read our Collectibles Guide for the full 100% completion checklist, this guide goes deeper — cataloging everything in each room, not just the named collectibles. Think of it as the companion volume to the Farming Guide, focused on one-time pickups and fixed spawn locations rather than repeatable routes.

How to use this guide: Each chapter section lists rooms in the order you encounter them naturally during gameplay. Items in bold are key items or rare pickups. Items marked with ★ are easily missed — they require backtracking or hidden interactions.

CHAPTER 1: THE AWAKENING — ROOM-BY-ROOM ITEMS

The opening chapter at Rookwood Estate is generous with supplies to ease you into the game. However, several items are tucked inside drawers and cabinets that players sprint past.

Rookwood Estate — Grand Foyer

  • First Aid Spray — on the reception desk, impossible to miss
  • Handgun Ammo x8 — inside the left drawer of the reception desk
  • Estate Key (Upper Floor) — dropped by the first Lycan encounter
  • Herb (Green) — potted plant to the right of the staircase
  • Gunpowder — shelf behind the staircase ★

Rookwood Estate — East Sitting Room

  • Handgun Ammo x6 — coffee table near the fireplace
  • Rookwood Family Portrait (Document) — examine the painting above the mantle
  • Gunpowder x2 — inside the cabinet by the window
  • Herb (Green) — windowsill planter box

Rookwood Estate — West Library

  • Library Safe Code Memo (Document) — desk near the bookshelf
  • Handgun Ammo x10 — inside the safe (code: 0423) ★
  • Herb (Red) — hidden behind the large globe, interact from the back ★
  • Shotgun Shells x4 — drawer in the reading nook

Rookwood Estate — Upper Hallway

  • Knife — wall-mounted display case (becomes your backup melee weapon)
  • Gunpowder — end table near the window
  • Handmade Necklace (Treasure, 4,000 Lei) — inside the locked bedroom ★

CHAPTER 2: ECHOES OF THE ESTATE — ROOM-BY-ROOM ITEMS

Chapter 2 expands the explorable area significantly. This is where the game introduces treasure hunting in earnest — many rooms contain valuables that can be sold to the Merchant for significant Lei.

Basement Corridor

  • Shotgun Shells x4 — crate at the base of the stairs
  • Herb (Green) — wall shelf near the boiler
  • Rusted Key — on the corpse near the locked door
  • Gunpowder — workbench shelf

Basement — Wine Cellar

  • Silver Chalice (Treasure, 6,500 Lei) — inside the locked cabinet, requires Rusted Key ★
  • Handgun Ammo x6 — wine barrel shelf
  • Herb (Green+Green) — combine from two separate planters
  • Cellar Worker's Diary (Document) — on the wooden crate

Estate — Kitchen

  • Knife x2 — knife block on the counter
  • Handgun Ammo x4 — pantry shelf
  • First Aid Med (Herb combo: G+G) — craft at the counter if you have herbs
  • Pendant with Ruby (Treasure, 8,000 Lei) — inside the chimney, shoot the nest down first ★

Pro Tip: The Pendant with Ruby in the Kitchen chimney is one of the most commonly missed treasures in the entire game. You must shoot the bird's nest visible in the chimney flue — it drops the pendant on the floor. Return to the Merchant immediately to sell it before the chapter autosave locks you out.

Estate — Study Room

  • Rookwood Family Tree (Document) — open book on the desk
  • Handgun Ammo x8 — desk drawer
  • Gunpowder x2 — shelf behind the desk chair
  • Herb (Green) — bookshelf gap (interact prompt is subtle) ★

CHAPTER 3: BELOW THE SURFACE — ROOM-BY-ROOM ITEMS

The catacombs beneath the estate are dark and dangerous, but they hide some of the game's best treasures. Bring a Flash Grenade for the Shadow Stalker ambush in the lower tunnels.

Catacombs — Entrance Chamber

  • Shotgun Shells x6 — stone altar
  • Herb (Green+Red) — two separate alcoves, combine for healing + damage boost
  • Ancient Medallion (Treasure, 12,000 Lei) — behind the crumbling wall, shoot the weak point ★
  • Handgun Ammo x4 — sarcophagus lid

Catacombs — Burial Chamber

  • Catacomb Inscription (Document) — carved into the north wall
  • Flash Grenade — pedestal near the exit
  • Gunpowder x3 — scattered across three stone ledges
  • Herb (Green) — mossy alcove on the east wall

Catacombs — Flooded Tunnel

  • Handgun Ammo x10 — submerged crate (wade into knee-deep water) ★
  • Old Watch (Treasure, 3,500 Lei) — inside the broken barrel
  • Shotgun Shells x4 — ledge above the water line

Missable Warning: The Flooded Tunnel becomes inaccessible after the Chapter 3 boss fight. Make sure you collect everything before pulling the lever that triggers the collapse sequence.

ROOM-BY-ROOM ITEM HUNTING TIPS

TIP 01

Always Check Drawers and Cabinets

Over 40% of all ammo pickups in RE Requiem are inside interactable furniture — drawers, cabinets, lockers, and crates. The game does not highlight these with a glow or prompt; you must physically approach and press interact. On Professional difficulty, these hidden stashes are essential for survival.

TIP 02

Shoot Environmental Objects

Bird nests, hanging crates, and weak wall sections can be shot to reveal hidden items. The Pendant with Ruby in Chapter 2 and the Ancient Medallion in Chapter 3 both require shooting environmental objects before they become collectible. If a room looks empty, fire a round at suspicious objects.

TIP 03

Combine Herbs Immediately

RE Requiem's inventory is limited. Picking up individual Green Herbs wastes space. Combine them as you go: Green+Green for stronger healing, Green+Red for healing + damage boost, or Green+Yellow for defense. See our Crafting Guide for all herb combination recipes.

TIP 04

Backtrack Before Point of No Return

Several chapters have a point-of-no-return moment (usually a cutscene or door collapse). The Flooded Tunnel in Chapter 3 and the Laboratory in Chapter 7 both become inaccessible after their boss fights. Always clear rooms thoroughly before triggering major story events.

TIP 05

Use the Merchant Between Chapters

After each chapter, the Merchant's inventory refreshes. Sell treasures immediately for Lei, then buy the ammo type you are lowest on. The 59,700 Lei from treasures across the game is enough to fully upgrade one weapon to max level — prioritize your primary damage dealer.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many total items are in Resident Evil Requiem?

Across all 12 chapters, there are approximately 237 ammo pickups, 38 herb spawns, 8 key items, 15 documents, and treasures worth 59,700 Lei. Combined with the 68 named collectibles (covered in our Collectibles Guide), the game contains over 360 individual item pickups.

Do items respawn in Resident Evil Requiem?

No. Items in RE Requiem are fixed, one-time pickups. Once collected, they do not respawn. The only exception is the Merchant's shop, which restocks between chapters. If you miss an item and the room becomes inaccessible, you cannot go back for it in the same playthrough.

What happens if I miss a key item?

Key items (keys, keycards, quest items) are mandatory for progression — the game typically prevents you from moving forward without them. However, optional key items like the Rusted Key in Chapter 2 (which opens the Silver Chalice cabinet) can be missed permanently if you leave the area.

Where are the Magnum Ammo locations in RE Requiem?

Magnum Ammo is the rarest ammo type in the game, with only 12 rounds total across all chapters: 3 in Chapter 7 (Operating Theater), 3 in Chapter 10 (Safe Room), and 6 in Chapter 12 (Final Chamber, hidden alcove). Save these for boss encounters on Professional difficulty.

Can I get all items on Professional difficulty?

Yes. Item locations are the same across all difficulty levels — the difference is enemy damage and resource scarcity. Professional difficulty reduces ammo drops from defeated enemies, making the fixed room pickups even more critical. Use this guide to ensure you never miss a room.

Is there a way to track which rooms I have cleared?

RE Requiem does not have a built-in room checklist. However, the pause menu's map shows room names with a checkmark icon once all items have been collected. If a room shows no checkmark, there is still an item you have not picked up — return and search drawers, cabinets, and environmental objects.
