COLLECTIBLES OVERVIEW

Resident Evil Requiem contains 68 total collectibles spread across its 12 chapters. These are divided into four categories, each with distinct visual indicators and rewards. Unlike previous RE titles, many collectibles are permanently missable because key areas become inaccessible after certain chapters. Use this guide alongside your playthrough to avoid missing anything.

TypeCountVisual CueReward for All
Files24Glowing white document on desk/shelfComplete story archive in Extras menu
Lore Fragments12Purple-glowing crystalline shardUnlocks the Hidden/True Ending
Weapon Parts18Blue-glowing weapon caseFull upgrade potential for all weapons
Mr. Raccoon Figures14Small raccoon statue (must destroy)Unlimited Knife (NG+) + Pest Control trophy
TOTAL68

Collectibles appear on your in-game map as icons once you are within 15 meters. Files show as document icons, Lore Fragments as diamond icons, Weapon Parts as gear icons. Mr. Raccoons do not appear on the map at all -- you must find them by sight or by the faint jingling sound they emit when nearby.

IMPORTANT: Missable Content Warning

Chapters 1-4 (Rookwood Estate) and Chapters 5-6 (Grace Hospital) are one-time areas. Once you progress past Chapter 6, you cannot return to either location. Make sure to collect everything in these areas during your first visit. Chapter 7 onward takes place in the Forgotten City, which allows limited backtracking until Chapter 11.

ACT I: CHAPTERS 1-4 -- ROOKWOOD ESTATE

The Rookwood Estate is densely packed with collectibles. Explore every room thoroughly before triggering the Chapter 4 boss fight -- the estate collapses after you defeat the Bloated Lycan and becomes permanently inaccessible.

Chapter 1: The Awakening

FILE
Rookwood Estate Welcome Letter
Main Hall -- Reception Desk

On the reception desk immediately after entering the estate. Cannot be missed as it sits directly in your path.

FILE
Staff Duty Roster
East Wing -- Servants' Quarters, First Room

On a small desk in the first bedroom of the servants' quarters. After obtaining the Rusty Key from the kitchen, head east through the dining hall.

LORE FRAGMENT #1
The Patriarch's Journal
West Wing -- Study, Hidden Safe Behind Painting

In the study on the second floor. Interact with the crooked painting to reveal a wall safe. The combination is 4-2-1 (found on the back of the Family Portrait in the main hall).

MR. RACCOON #1
Kitchen Counter Raccoon
Kitchen -- Behind Stacked Crates, Southwest Corner

In the kitchen, look behind the stacked wooden crates in the southwest corner. The raccoon sits on the counter. Destroy it with your knife to save ammo.

Chapter 2: Echoes of the Estate

FILE
Gardener's Diary (Part 1)
Greenhouse -- Workbench

In the greenhouse accessible from the garden path. On the workbench near the Herb planters.

FILE
Groundskeeper's Incident Report
Servants' Quarters Corridor -- Bulletin Board

Pinned to the bulletin board in the long corridor connecting the servants' quarters to the main house. Easy to miss if you sprint through this area.

FILE
Letter from Lady Rookwood
Master Bedroom -- Nightstand

On the nightstand in the master bedroom (second floor, west wing). Requires the Ornate Key obtained from the library puzzle.

WEAPON PART
Pistol Suppressor
Library -- Locked Display Case

In a locked glass display case in the library. Use the Display Case Key found in the desk drawer of the study to open it. Reduces noise and prevents attracting nearby enemies.

WEAPON PART
Shotgun Extended Tube
Basement Storage -- Behind Breakable Wall

In the basement storage area, a cracked wall section can be destroyed with a shotgun blast or one frag grenade. Behind it: the extended tube, increasing shotgun capacity from 4 to 6 shells.

LORE FRAGMENT #2
The Rookwood Bloodline
Family Crypt -- Behind the Central Sarcophagus

In the family crypt beneath the chapel. Push the central sarcophagus aside (co-op required, or use a grenade solo) to reveal a hidden alcove with the fragment.

MR. RACCOON #2
Chapel Raccoon
Chapel -- Upper Balcony, On the Organ

On the upper balcony of the chapel, sitting on the organ. Climb the spiral staircase on the left side of the chapel to reach it.

Chapter 3: Below the Surface

FILE
Mining Operation Log
Estate Cellar -- Foreman's Office

On the desk inside the foreman's office in the cellar. The office is locked; the key is on a hook just outside the door.

FILE
Geological Survey Report
Tunnel Section B -- Abandoned Cart

Inside an overturned mining cart in Tunnel Section B. Look for the cart near the second lantern post along the tracks.

WEAPON PART
Rifle Scope (2x)
Cellar Armory -- Weapon Rack

In the cellar armory (requires the Armory Key from the foreman's office). On the wall-mounted weapon rack. Attaches to the Semi-Auto Rifle for improved long-range accuracy.

WEAPON PART
Magnum Speed Loader
Hidden Alcove -- After First Lycan Encounter

After defeating the first Lycan in the tunnels, check the alcove behind where it spawns. The speed loader reduces Magnum reload time by 40%.

LORE FRAGMENT #3
The Excavation Discovery
Deep Shaft -- Bottom Level, Glowing Crevice

At the bottom of the deep shaft after the elevator ride, search the glowing purple crevice on the eastern wall. Requires the flashlight to spot the glow.

MR. RACCOON #3
Mine Cart Raccoon
Tunnel Section C -- Inside Empty Mine Cart

In Tunnel Section C, one of the stationary mine carts contains a Mr. Raccoon. It is the third cart on the right side after entering Section C.

On the first workstation you encounter upon entering the underground lab beneath the estate.

FILE
Experiment Log #47
Lab B-Wing -- Observation Room

Inside the observation room overlooking the test chamber. On a clipboard next to the window. Contains the armory code: 1-9-8-7.

FILE
Evacuation Order
Lab Control Room -- Central Terminal Wall

Pinned next to the central terminal in the control room. Collect this before triggering the boss fight -- the room becomes inaccessible after.

WEAPON PART
Assault Rifle Stabilizer

In the armory locker (code: 1-9-8-7 from Experiment Log #47). Reduces recoil by 30% for the Assault Rifle.

WEAPON PART

Under the staircase in B-Wing storage. Increases knife damage by 25% and adds a bleed effect to all knife attacks.

LORE FRAGMENT #4
The Crimson Project

Inside the shattered incubation tank in the main lab chamber. Crawl through the broken glass carefully; the fragment glows at the bottom of the tank.

MR. RACCOON #4
Lab Break Room Raccoon
Underground Lab -- Staff Break Room, Microwave

Inside the microwave in the staff break room. Open the microwave door and destroy the raccoon inside -- one of the more creative placements in the game.

ACT II: CHAPTERS 5-6 -- GRACE HOSPITAL

You play as Grace Ashcroft during this section. Grace has different movement mechanics and cannot access Leon's equipment. All collectibles found here carry over to the shared inventory once the characters reunite in Chapter 7.

Chapter 5: Betrayal in the Dark

FILE
Hospital Admission Record
Grace Hospital -- Front Desk

On the front desk of the hospital lobby immediately after the opening cutscene. Cannot be missed.

FILE
Nurse's Confession Note
Second Floor -- Nurses' Station, Drawer

Inside a drawer at the nurses' station on the second floor. The drawer is initially locked; use the Nurses' Station Key from the pharmacy.

WEAPON PART
Grace's Pistol Laser Sight
Security Office -- Locked Cabinet

In the security office behind a locked cabinet (code: 0-4-1-5 from the security guard's note in the locker room). Adds a laser sight for improved hip-fire accuracy.

WEAPON PART
SMG Extended Magazine
Morgue -- Drawer #7

In the morgue, open drawer #7. Increases SMG capacity from 30 to 45 rounds. Watch for the jump-scare enemy that bursts from drawer #3 when you enter the room.

LORE FRAGMENT #5
Patient Zero Documentation
Isolation Ward -- Quarantine Room 3

In Quarantine Room 3 of the isolation ward. The door requires the Quarantine Access Card from the chief physician's office. Inside, examine the body on the bed to find the fragment.

MR. RACCOON #5
Hospital Rooftop Raccoon
Hospital Rooftop -- On Top of HVAC Unit

On top of the large HVAC unit on the hospital rooftop. Accessible after the Chapter 5 boss encounter. Climb onto the smaller unit first, then jump across.

Chapter 6: The Living Laboratory

FILE

On the desk in Victor Gideon's private office in the secret basement lab. Reveals key story details about the Rookwood experiments.

WEAPON PART

Behind the retinal scanner door in the secret lab armory. Use the severed hand from the specimen storage to bypass the scanner. Reduces grenade launcher recoil by 50%.

On the top shelf of the supply closet in B2. Shoot it down with a pistol round. Precision bolts deal 2x headshot damage and travel faster than standard bolts.

LORE FRAGMENT #6

Under the main console in the observatory room overlooking the dimensional gate prototype. Crouch and look beneath the panel to spot the purple glow.

MR. RACCOON #6

Secret Lab B2 -- Specimen Storage, Jar Display

Inside a large specimen jar on the central display in the specimen storage room. Shoot the jar to break it and then destroy the raccoon.

MR. RACCOON #7
Parking Garage Raccoon
Hospital Parking Garage -- Inside Abandoned Ambulance

In the parking garage exit route, inside the back of an abandoned ambulance. Check before triggering the Leon dirt bike chase sequence.

In the barricaded house in the residential district (third house on the left). On the kitchen table. Contains the police evidence locker code: 7-2-3-9. Be ready for a Crimson Lycan ambush when you exit.

WEAPON PART

In the abandoned weapons shop near the central plaza. Behind the counter in a locked case (shoot the lock). Tightens shotgun spread for better effective range.

At the base of the central fountain statue, tucked behind a loose stone. Look for a faint purple shimmer from the statue's shadow side.

Climb to the top of the clock tower. The raccoon sits behind the western clock face. You will need to squeeze through a narrow gap to reach it.

ACT IV: CHAPTERS 10-12 -- FINAL DESCENT

The final act is linear with no backtracking. Every collectible in these chapters is on the critical path, but some are hidden in side rooms during intense combat sequences. Stay alert.

Embedded in a shattered monitor on the observation deck overlooking the gate. The purple crystal is visibly protruding from the broken screen.

On the altar at the entrance to the final boss arena. This is the last File in the game.

After defeating the final boss, before triggering the ending cutscene, the rift core glows purple. Walk to the edge of the platform and interact with the crystal formation. MR. RACCOON #14
Final Raccoon

Behind a collapsed pillar in the final arena (south side). Visible during Phase 1 and Phase 2. Destroy it before Phase 3, as the pillar is completely destroyed in the final phase.

ALL 12 LORE FRAGMENTS FOR HIDDEN ENDING

Collecting all 12 Lore Fragments is the only way to unlock the Hidden Ending (True Ending / Requiem Ending). This ending reveals the Rookwood family's full backstory, the origin of the dimensional rift, and a post-credits scene that hints at a sequel. You must collect all 12 before entering the final boss arena in Chapter 12 -- once you enter, missing fragments cannot be retrieved.

Hidden Ending Trigger Checklist

1. Collect all 12 Lore Fragments before entering Chapter 12's final boss arena.
2. Defeat the final boss.
3. After the boss is defeated, interact with the glowing purple crystal formation at the Rift Core.
4. The Hidden Ending cutscene will play instead of the standard ending.
5. You also unlock the "Requiem Aeternam" achievement/trophy and 10,000 Requiem Points.

PRO TIPS FOR EFFICIENT COLLECTION

1. Use Headphones for Mr. Raccoons

Every Mr. Raccoon emits a faint jingling/chime sound when you are within 10 meters. This audio cue is the only reliable way to find them on a blind playthrough, since they do not appear on the in-game map. Playing with headphones makes the directional audio much clearer.

2. Clear Each Area Before Boss Fights

Make it a habit to sweep every room in a chapter before triggering the boss encounter. Many chapters lock you out of exploration once the boss fight begins. If the game gives you a "Point of No Return" warning, stop and backtrack immediately.

3. Lore Fragments Glow Under Flashlight

When your flashlight beam hits a Lore Fragment, the purple glow intensifies noticeably. In dark areas like the catacombs and underground lab, sweep your flashlight across walls and corners to spot hidden fragments.

4. Save Ammo for Mr. Raccoons -- Use Your Knife

On Professional difficulty, ammo is extremely scarce. Use your knife to destroy Mr. Raccoon figures instead of wasting bullets. Most raccoons are stationary and can be reached without alerting enemies.

5. New Game Plus Carryover Rules

Collectible progress does not fully carry over to NG+. You keep your weapon parts and their upgrades, but Files, Lore Fragments, and Mr. Raccoons reset. If you are hunting the Hidden Ending, complete the Lore Fragment collection in a single playthrough. NG+ is best used for Mr. Raccoon cleanup runs on Assisted difficulty.
