RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM DIFFICULTY GUIDE: STANDARD VS HARDCORE VS PROFESSIONAL

UPDATED JUNE 2026 • 20 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Choosing the right difficulty in Resident Evil Requiem can make or break your first experience with the game. Unlike many modern titles, RE Requiem does not allow you to change difficulty mid-playthrough — the choice you make at the start stays with you for the entire run. This means understanding exactly what each difficulty level changes is critical before you commit.

This guide breaks down every difference between Standard, Hardcore, and Professional difficulties in RE Requiem, including enemy damage multipliers, item scarcity, unlock requirements, and expert advice on which difficulty suits your playstyle. We also cover the ranking system, S+ rank requirements, and strategies for conquering each tier.

DIFFICULTY LEVELS OVERVIEW

RE Requiem offers three clearly distinct difficulty tiers. Each modifies enemy stats, item availability, save mechanics, and even enemy placements.

DIFFICULTY 1
STANDARD — THE BALANCED EXPERIENCE
Recommended for: First-time players • Survival horror newcomers

Standard is the baseline difficulty that the game was designed around. Enemies deal moderate damage, items appear at designed frequencies, and auto-save checkpoints are generous. Standard is the perfect way to experience the story, explore environments, and learn enemy patterns without excessive frustration.

DIFFICULTY 2
HARDCORE — FOR SEASONED SURVIVORS
Recommended for: RE veterans • Players seeking a challenge

Hardcore significantly ramps up the difficulty. Enemy damage is doubled, item drops are reduced by 40%, new enemy variants appear in unexpected locations, and auto-save checkpoints are much rarer. Enemies require more hits to stagger, and some puzzle solutions are randomized.

DIFFICULTY 3
PROFESSIONAL — THE ULTIMATE TEST
Recommended for: Completionists • S+ rank seekers

Professional is RE Requiem at its most extreme. Enemy damage is 3.5x Standard, items are 60% scarcer, and the biggest change: you can only save at typewriters with Ink Ribbons (very limited supply) and your partner has permadeath. Professional is required for the highest-tier unlocks.

STANDARD DIFFICULTY GUIDE

Standard is the most accessible experience, but that does not mean it is easy. Enemies still hit hard enough that reckless play will kill you. Here is what you need to know:

What to Expect on Standard

  • Generous ammo and herb drops — you can afford to experiment with different weapons without worrying about ammo economy.
  • Frequent auto-saves — the game saves at every major room transition, key item pickup, and boss checkpoint. You will rarely lose more than 5-10 minutes of progress.
  • Predictable enemy patterns — enemies spawn at fixed locations with consistent behavior. Once you learn a room, you can plan your approach.
  • Full puzzle solutions — safe codes, lock combinations, and environmental puzzles follow the same solutions every playthrough.

Standard Strategy: Use your first Standard run to explore thoroughly, find all collectibles, learn enemy attack patterns, and stockpile resources. Every herb, bullet, and upgrade you find on Standard makes your future Hardcore or Professional runs easier through New Game Plus.

HARDCORE DIFFICULTY GUIDE

Hardcore is the recommended difficulty for anyone who has completed a Resident Evil game before. It transforms RE Requiem into a genuine resource management survival horror experience.

Hardcore-Specific Changes

  • New enemy variants: Crimson Molded appear in Hardcore and above. These are faster, more aggressive versions of standard enemies with unique attack patterns.
  • Item scarcity changes strategy: With 40% fewer drops, you cannot afford to be wasteful. Prioritize handgun ammo crafting over shotgun or magnum rounds.
  • Reduced auto-saves mean checkpoint planning: You may go 20-30 minutes between saves. Plan your exploration around save room locations.

Hardcore Pro Tip: Always carry at least one Defense Item. On Hardcore, grab attacks can one-shot you from full health at certain points in the game. Defense Items auto-counter grabs and save your life.

Hardcore Survival Checklist

  • Craft ammo at every workbench visit — materials do not carry over between chapters
  • Prioritize Green+Red herb combos for full heals
  • Explore every room thoroughly before moving on — hidden items are more critical
  • Save special ammo (incendiary, acid) exclusively for boss fights
  • Use the knife for environmental objects to conserve ammo

PROFESSIONAL DIFFICULTY GUIDE

Professional is a beast unto itself. This difficulty is designed for the most dedicated players and requires near-perfect play to complete.

The Ink Ribbon System

On Professional, you cannot save freely. Manual saves require Ink Ribbons, and there are approximately 15 Ink Ribbons scattered across the entire game. You must choose wisely when to save.

  • Save before every boss fight — boss arenas are the highest-risk moments
  • Save after major item acquisitions — losing progress after finding a rare weapon part is devastating
  • Never save in safe rooms — safe rooms are resupply points
  • Keep at least 1 Ink Ribbon in reserve — for emergencies

Partner Permadeath Management

Your AI partner can die permanently on Professional. If they fall in battle, they do not return until the next chapter starts. This means you lose partner synergy mechanics (co-op door breaking, dual puzzles), enemy aggro doubles on you, and no partner healing or backup fire.

Professional Survival Tip: Equip your partner with a defensive item loadout. Give them a shotgun with limited ammo and prioritize keeping their health topped up. A dead partner makes an already brutal difficulty nearly impossible.

S+ Rank Requirements

Professional features a ranking system. To achieve S+ rank (required for HUNK unlock), you must:

  • Complete the game in under 3 hours
  • Use 5 or fewer Ink Ribbons
  • Open no bonus weapon crates (Rocket Launcher disables S+ tracking)
  • Keep your partner alive for all chapters
  • Collect at least 75% of Lore Fragments

S+ Strategy: Do not attempt S+ on your first Professional run. First complete Professional at any rank to unlock the Infinite Rocket Launcher and bonus weapons. Then use a New Game Plus Professional run with infinite ammo SMG for your S+ attempt. The SMG does not disable ranking.

EXPERT TIPS FOR EACH DIFFICULTY

TIP 1

Standard: Complete Everything

Treat Standard as your preparatory run. Find all 14 weapon parts, all 50 Lore Fragments, and all 12 hidden photographs. Every collectible carries over to NG+ and contributes to unlock progression.

TIP 2

Hardcore: Master the Perfect Dodge

On Hardcore, the Perfect Dodge is not optional — it is essential. The 2.0x damage multiplier means you cannot trade hits with enemies. Practice on standard enemies before reaching boss fights.

TIP 3

Professional: Use Every Tool

Professional demands that you use every resource available. Flash rounds stun even bosses. Defense items counter otherwise lethal grabs. The knife can destroy environmental obstacles to reveal hidden items.

TIP 4

All Difficulties: New Game Plus Stacking

Each NG+ run stacks progress from all previous runs. Completing Professional on NG+ after a Hardcore NG+ run gives you access to both the Chicago Sweeper and any weapons you unlocked from Standard.

TIP 5

Infinite Ammo Strategy

The fastest path to infinite ammo: complete Standard, complete Hardcore (unlock Chicago Sweeper), then purchase infinite SMG ammo (10,000 RP). Use the infinite Chicago Sweeper on Professional to trivialize most combat encounters.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

Can I change difficulty mid-game in RE Requiem?

No. RE Requiem locks your difficulty choice at the start of a playthrough. You must start a new game or New Game Plus run to change difficulty.

Q2

Does enemy placement change between difficulties?

Yes. Hardcore introduces new enemy variants in existing locations. Professional adds elite enemy versions and additional enemy encounters.

Q3

What is the biggest difference between Hardcore and Professional?

The Ink Ribbon save system and partner permadeath. Hardcore is manageable with careful play. Professional changes core mechanics that demand a completely different approach to risk assessment.

Q4

Can I get the secret ending on any difficulty?

Yes. The secret ending is tied to Lore Fragment collection, not difficulty. You can unlock it on Standard.

Q5

How many Ink Ribbons are in a Professional playthrough?

Approximately 15 Ink Ribbons are available. Plan to use no more than 12, keeping 3 as emergency reserves.

Q6

Do bonus weapons disable Professional S+ rank?

Only the Rocket Launcher disables S+ rank tracking. Infinite ammo SMGs, handguns, and the Samurai Edge do not affect S+ eligibility.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem difficulty design rewards preparation and punishes complacency. Whether you choose Standard for the story-first experience or dive straight into Hardcore for the classic survival horror challenge, knowing exactly what each difficulty changes is the key to a satisfying playthrough.

Our recommended approach: Standard for story and collection, then Hardcore NG+ for skill development, then Professional NG+ for unlocks, and finally Professional S+ NG+ for mastery. Four playthroughs, approximately 25-30 hours total, and you will have experienced everything RE Requiem has to offer across every difficulty tier.
