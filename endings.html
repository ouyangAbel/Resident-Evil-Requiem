RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM ALL ENDINGS EXPLAINED: SECRETS, HIDDEN ENDING & LORE

Resident Evil Requiem features multiple endings that significantly impact the story's conclusion and have major implications for the franchise's future. This guide covers every ending, the requirements to unlock each one, and a deep analysis of what they mean for the characters and lore.

There are three main endings: the Default Ending, the Redemption Ending, and the hidden Requiem Ending. Each requires different choices and completion criteria.

ENDING 1: THE DEFAULT ENDING

DEFAULT

THE COST OF SURVIVAL

In the Default Ending, Leon survives the final confrontation and escapes the facility. However, the victory is bittersweet. The final cutscene shows Leon sitting alone in a helicopter, staring at his hands as the camera slowly pulls back to reveal the burning ruins of the research complex below.

The implication is clear: Leon has survived, but at what cost? The final shot mirrors the opening scene, creating a sense of cyclical tragedy. Leon's expression suggests deep trauma and uncertainty about the future.

How to unlock: Complete the game on any difficulty without meeting the specific requirements for the other endings.

LORE CONNECTION

The Default Ending's helicopter scene directly references the ending of the original Resident Evil 4, creating a deliberate parallel between Leon's first and potentially final major mission.

ENDING 2: THE REDEMPTION ENDING

GOOD ENDING

REDEMPTION

The Redemption Ending is a more hopeful conclusion. In this version, Leon not only survives but makes a conscious choice to walk away from the fight. The final scene shows him arriving at a small coastal town, implied to be the location referenced in earlier documents about his "retirement plan."

A key difference from the Default Ending: Leon is shown smiling for the first time in the game. The camera lingers on a photo on his desk showing him with the original S.T.A.R.S. members, suggesting he's finally found peace with his past.

How to unlock: Complete the following requirements during your playthrough:

  • Find and read all 12 "Personal Letters" hidden throughout the game
  • Complete the optional side mission "Lost Memories" in Chapter 7
  • Make the "Mercy" choice during the confrontation with the final boss (press L2 when prompted)
  • Have at least 80% completion of collectibles
LORE CONNECTION

The coastal town in the Redemption Ending is implied to be the same location where Jill Valentine retired after the events of RE5. This suggests Leon and Jill may have reconnected, though this is never explicitly confirmed.

ENDING 3: THE REQUIEM ENDING (SECRET)

SECRET ENDING

THE REQUIEM

The Requiem Ending is the most difficult to unlock and contains the most significant revelations. In this version, the final confrontation takes a dramatically different turn. Instead of defeating the final boss through combat, Leon discovers the truth behind the entire conspiracy and makes a sacrificial choice.

The ending reveals that the events of Requiem were orchestrated by a shadow organization seeking to create the "perfect bioweapon" — and Leon himself was the experiment all along. The final scene shows Leon activating the facility's self-destruct sequence and staying behind to ensure its complete destruction.

However, the post-credits scene reveals a twist: a figure matching Leon's description is seen walking away from the explosion's aftermath, heavily bandaged but alive. The camera focuses on his eyes — which flash with a familiar amber glow, hinting at residual Plaga infection from RE4.

How to unlock:

  • Complete the game on Hardcore difficulty or higher
  • Find all 30 "Shadow Files" (hidden documents detailing the conspiracy)
  • Complete the secret puzzle in Chapter 12 (requires combining 3 key items found earlier)
  • Defeat the final boss without using any healing items
  • Make the "Sacrifice" choice during the final prompt (press R2 when prompted)
  • Have 100% map completion

Important Note: The Requiem Ending cannot be achieved on your first playthrough unless you use a guide. The Shadow Files are extremely well-hidden, and some are only accessible through specific story paths. We recommend completing the Redemption Ending first, then using New Game+ to hunt for the secret ending.

WHAT THE ENDINGS MEAN FOR THE FRANCHISE

The three endings of Requiem suggest different futures for the Resident Evil franchise:

If the Default Ending is Canon

Leon continues as an active agent, potentially appearing in future games. However, his psychological state suggests he may take on a more mentorship role rather than front-line combat.

If the Redemption Ending is Canon

Leon retires from active duty. This would allow Capcom to focus on newer protagonists (like Rose Winters from Village) while keeping Leon available for cameos or advisory roles.

If the Requiem Ending is Canon

This opens the most possibilities. Leon survives but is fundamentally changed by the Plaga remnants in his system. This could set up a new storyline where Leon struggles with his own biology while uncovering deeper conspiracies.

DEVELOPER HINT

In a post-launch interview, director Koshi Nakanishi stated: "The true ending of Requiem is the one players earn through dedication. It's our love letter to the fans who have followed Leon's journey from the very beginning." This strongly suggests the Requiem Ending is the intended canonical conclusion.

POST-CREDITS SCENE BREAKDOWN

The post-credits scene (available in all endings but most detailed in the Requiem Ending) contains several important details:

  • The amber glow in Leon's eyes matches the visual language used for Plaga infections throughout the series, suggesting Leon still carries remnants of the parasite from RE4.
  • The bandaged figure's gait matches Leon's signature walking animation, confirming his identity beyond reasonable doubt.
  • The location appears to be a port city, potentially setting up the next game's setting.
  • A brief audio cue during the credits features a distorted version of Ada Wong's theme music, suggesting she may have played a role in Leon's survival.

TROUBLESHOOTING: ENDINGS NOT TRIGGERING

If you're having trouble unlocking a specific ending, check the following:

  • Choices are permanent within a single playthrough. You cannot save-scum to see different endings — you need to replay from the relevant chapter.
  • New Game+ carries over your collectible progress, making it easier to reach 100% completion for the Requiem Ending.
  • Difficulty affects availability. The Requiem Ending requires Hardcore or higher. Lower difficulties will always default to the Redemption or Default endings even if you meet other requirements.
  • The Chapter 12 puzzle requires items from Chapters 4, 8, and 11. If you missed any, you'll need to replay from the earliest missed chapter.

WHICH ENDING SHOULD YOU GET FIRST?

We recommend the following order:

  1. First playthrough: Default Ending (experience the story naturally)
  2. Second playthrough: Redemption Ending (explore more thoroughly)
  3. Third playthrough: Requiem Ending (complete mastery of the game)

This progression allows you to appreciate each ending's emotional weight without the pressure of perfect completion on your first run.
