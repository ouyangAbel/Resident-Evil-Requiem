ACT I: THE ESTATE (CHAPTERS I-III)

The opening act of Resident Evil Requiem sets the tone for everything that follows. You arrive at a remote countryside estate under the cover of dusk, and within minutes the atmosphere of creeping dread takes hold. Act I is primarily about exploration, puzzle solving, and learning the survival mechanics that will carry you through the rest of the game. Resources are relatively generous here, giving you room to experiment before the difficulty ramps up.

01
Introduction • 30-45 min
ARRIVAL AT DUSK

Chapter I serves as the game's opening sequence and tutorial. You arrive at the Ashford estate as the sun sets, and the chapter walks you through basic movement, inventory management, and light puzzle solving. The mood is quiet and unsettling rather than action-packed, with environmental storytelling doing most of the heavy lifting.

Key objectives: Explore the estate grounds, find the Front Door Key in the gardener's shed, solve the Gate Lock Puzzle (the combination is found on the note in the mailbox: 4-7-2), and enter the main mansion. Listen for audio cues throughout — the game uses sound design to foreshadow threats long before you encounter them.

Why it matters: Chapter I establishes the narrative framework. Pay attention to the documents and environmental details here; many of them become relevant in Act III when the story comes full circle.

02
Deep Exploration • 60-90 min
ECHOES OF THE ESTATE

This is the longest chapter in Act I and the game's most exploration-heavy sequence. The mansion is vast, with multiple wings, locked rooms, and interconnected puzzles that require you to backtrack and think laterally. Chapter II is where the game rewards patient, methodical players who check every corner.

Key objectives: Obtain the Library Key from the master bedroom, solve the Clock Tower Puzzle (set the hands to 11:45), collect the three Medallions (located in the library safe, the wine cellar, and the attic), and open the sealed passage in the basement. You will also find the Handgun in the study desk drawer during this chapter.

Pro tip: The attic contains the Wooden Box treasure, which sells for a high price at the Duke's shop later. Do not miss the hidden room behind the bookshelf in the library — it contains a weapon upgrade part.

Why it matters: Chapter II teaches you the resource loop that defines the entire game: explore thoroughly, solve puzzles to unlock shortcuts, and manage your inventory space carefully.

Bloated Lycan boss fight walkthrough — Chapter III

03
First Boss • 45-60 min
BELOW THE SURFACE

The underground passage beneath the mansion leads to a network of tunnels that once served as smuggling routes. Chapter III is tense and linear, with limited resources and some of the game's most claustrophobic encounters. It culminates in the first real boss fight of the Resident Evil Requiem chapters list.

Key objectives: Navigate the tunnel system by following red wall markings, drain the flooded chamber by solving the Water Valve Puzzle (right, left, center), find the Tunnel Key, and defeat the chapter boss.

Boss: The Bloated Lycan. This swollen creature guards the tunnel exit. It attacks with sweeping claws and ranged acid vomit. Maintain medium distance, dodge the acid sideways, and fire during recovery windows. Explosive canisters near the walls deal massive damage. Approximately 20 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun blasts on Standard difficulty.

Why it matters: This chapter transitions the game from exploration into combat survival. The Bloated Lycan teaches you boss-phase thinking that will be essential for every major encounter ahead.

ACT II: BETRAYAL AND THE LABORATORY (CHAPTERS IV-VI)

Act II raises the stakes dramatically. An ally's betrayal shifts the narrative into high gear, the estate transforms into a combat arena, and the underground laboratory reveals the scientific horror beneath the surface. If Act I taught you how to survive, Act II teaches you how to fight.

04
Mansion Revisited • 50-70 min
THE CRIMSON WING

Returning to the mansion reveals that everything has changed. New, more aggressive enemy variants patrol the halls, and previously visited areas now contain fresh threats and new item placements. The Crimson Wing introduces the Crimson Lycan, a faster, deadlier variant that can leap across rooms in a single bound.

Key objectives: Collect the Shotgun from the newly accessible armory, survive the three-wave Crimson Lycan ambush in the main hall, obtain the Garden Key, navigate the garden maze (right, left, straight through hedge gap, left at statue, right at fountain), and reach the greenhouse.

Why it matters: The Shotgun becomes your primary weapon from this point forward. The garden maze contains the Emerald Brooch, one of the most valuable treasures in the game.

05
Narrative Turning Point • 40-55 min
BETRAYAL IN THE DARK

This is the game's most dramatic chapter. An ally reveals their true intentions, and the story pivots from survival horror to desperate escape. Gameplay shifts to a more urgent, linear pace as the estate begins to collapse around you. The Resident Evil Requiem chapters list reaches its emotional peak here.

Key objectives: Survive the betrayal cutscene (earlier choices affect dialogue), escape the collapsing wine cellar within 3 minutes on Standard difficulty, find the Emergency Keycard on a dead soldier near the collapsed archway, and reach the elevator to the underground laboratory.

Pro tip: During the timed escape, do not fight enemies. Sprint through debris and follow the lit torches. At the fork, take the left path. The right is a dead end but contains a Gold Ingot treasure worth grabbing if you have time.

Why it matters: This chapter permanently changes the game's tone. The trust you placed in earlier characters is shattered, and every NPC encounter afterward carries new tension.

06
Major Boss Fight • 60-80 min
THE LIVING LABORATORY

The underground laboratory is the game's most complex area. Interconnected rooms, security systems, and disturbing environmental storytelling make this chapter a methodical exploration challenge. Two critical puzzles gate your progress, and the chapter ends with the first major boss encounter on the Resident Evil Requiem chapters list.

Key objectives: Restore the power system by finding three fuses in the break room, operating theater, and storage room. Access the Research Wing with Security Clearance B. Solve the Specimen Tank Puzzle (align tubes: Red-Blue-Green-Yellow). Obtain the Master Keycard from the high-security vault.

Boss: The Abomination. A grotesque fusion of multiple test subjects with three distinct phases. Phase 1: target two glowing weak points on the torso with shotgun blasts. Phase 2: it grows extra arms; dodge the ground slam shockwave and fire during recovery. Phase 3: lure it near explosive canisters that spawn around the arena.

Why it matters: This is the game's difficulty spike. The Abomination demands everything you have learned: resource management, pattern recognition, environmental awareness, and precise aiming.

ACT III: THE REQUIEM AND BEYOND (CHAPTERS VII-XII)

The final six chapters take the game into entirely new territory. Dimensional rifts, nightmarish alternate realities, and the collapse of the barrier between worlds escalate the horror to cosmic levels. Act III is the longest portion of the Resident Evil Requiem chapters list, and it contains the game's most memorable set pieces.

07
New World • 50-65 min
THE REQUIEM BEGINS

Emerging from the laboratory, you find yourself in a decaying cathedral town that exists in a dimension overlapping with reality. The atmosphere shifts from scientific horror to supernatural dread, and the game's visual palette changes dramatically.

Key objectives: Explore the cathedral town, obtain the Cathedral Key from the bell tower, solve the Stained Glass Puzzle (Angel, Chalice, Skull, Cross from left to right), collect three Altar Stones, and open the path to the dimensional rift.

Why it matters: Chapter VII is the first time the Semi-Auto Rifle becomes available. It also introduces the Lore Fragments collectibles required for the secret ending.

08
Mirror Dimension • 55-70 min
DIMENSIONS APART

Stepping through the rift transports you to a nightmarish mirror version of the estate from Chapters I through III. The layout is inverted, gravity behaves strangely, and new enemy types phase in and out of visibility. This chapter will disorient even veteran players who memorized the original estate layout.

Key objectives: Navigate the inverted mirror-estate, defeat the Shadow Stalker mini-boss (track by red glow when invisible, shotgun burst when materialized), find the Dimensional Anchor, and solve the Inversion Puzzle (pull levers 4-3-2-1 with 2-second pauses).

Boss: Shadow Stalker. This enemy phases between visible and invisible states. Two close-range shotgun blasts force it to retreat. Approximately 12 shotgun rounds total to defeat.

09
Open Exploration • 70-100 min
THE FORGOTTEN CITY

The Forgotten City is the game's largest explorable area and the longest chapter on the Resident Evil Requiem chapters list. It features interconnected streets, crumbling buildings, and numerous optional encounters. This is where completionists will spend the most time.

Key objectives: Map the city using the Cartographer's Notes, activate four City Beacons, defeat the optional Forgotten King boss, and collect all six Lore Fragments for the secret ending path.

Optional Boss: The Forgotten King. A towering armored figure wielding a massive halberd. Stay behind him. When he plants the halberd for a shockwave slam, use the rifle to target the weak point on his back. Rewards the Broken Butterfly Magnum, one of the strongest weapons in the game.

Why it matters: This is the only chapter with true open-world exploration. The six Lore Fragments scattered throughout the city are absolutely required if you want to unlock the secret ending.

10
Combat Gauntlet • 45-60 min
CONVERGENCE

The dimensional barriers collapse. Both worlds merge into a single nightmarish landscape. Chapter X is a gauntlet of combat encounters with minimal exploration, building intensity toward the finale.

Key objectives: Fight through three consecutive arena encounters, protect the NPC ally during the 4-minute bridge defense sequence, and reach the Convergence Point.

Pro tip: Stock up on shotgun ammo and grenades before the bridge defense. Focus fire on Crimson Lycans first. They deal the most damage to your NPC ally, and losing the ally triggers a game over.

11
Penultimate Chapter • 50-65 min
THE FINAL DESCENT

This chapter descends into the heart of the dimensional rift. The environment is surreal and constantly shifting. It contains what many players consider the hardest fight in the entire Resident Evil Requiem chapters list.

Key objectives: Navigate the shifting corridors (follow the red light to avoid dead ends), defeat the Amalgam boss in the central chamber, recover the Requiem Key, and make final preparations before the endgame.

Boss: The Amalgam. A horrific fusion of every major enemy type. It cycles through forms: Lycan Form (fast melee, use rifle), Abomination Form (slow tentacle attacks, use pillars for cover), Shadow Form (phases in and out, shotgun when visible), and Final Form at 25 percent HP that combines all three. Use the explosive canisters that respawn every 30 seconds in the Final Form phase.

12
Finale • 40-60 min
REQUIEM

The final chapter of the Resident Evil Requiem chapters list delivers a multi-phase boss encounter that mirrors the entire game arc, escalating in intensity and requiring different weapon strategies for each phase. The ending you receive depends on your choices throughout the game and whether you collected all six Lore Fragments.

Pre-fight checklist: Full health, maximum ammunition for every weapon type, at least 3 First Aid Meds, and all weapons fully upgraded. There is no shop or storage access once the chapter begins.

TIPS FOR NAVIGATING THE RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CHAPTERS LIST

Here are some general strategies that apply across all 12 chapters:

  • Save often. The game has generous save room placement, but some boss arenas lock you out of backtracking. Save before entering any area that looks like a boss arena.
  • Explore before you advance. Many chapters lock previous areas once you pass a story trigger. Clear every room before pushing forward.
  • Manage ammunition types. Do not rely on a single weapon. Spread your resources across handgun, shotgun, and rifle ammo to avoid running dry during boss fights.
  • Collect Lore Fragments. Starting from Chapter VII, grab every Lore Fragment you find. There are exactly six, and they are required for the secret ending.
  • Use Chapter Select for cleanup. After your first playthrough, use Chapter Select to mop up missed collectibles and treasures.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM CHAPTERS

Q1

How many chapters are in Resident Evil Requiem?

Resident Evil Requiem has 12 chapters divided into three acts. Act I covers the estate exploration, Act II introduces betrayal and the laboratory, and Act III escalates into dimensional horror and the final confrontation.

Q2

How long does it take to beat all 12 chapters?

A first playthrough on Standard difficulty takes approximately 12 to 15 hours. Completionist runs with all collectibles and secrets can take 25 to 30 hours. Speedrunners have completed the game in under 3 hours.

Q3

Which chapter is the hardest?

Most players find Chapter XI (The Final Descent) to be the most challenging due to the Amalgam boss fight and the extremely tight resource economy. Chapter VI is a close second because of The Abomination's multiple phases.

Q4

Can I replay specific chapters?

Yes. After completing the game you unlock Chapter Select from the main menu. Your inventory carries over between chapter replays, making it easier to grab missed collectibles.

Q5

Which chapters have boss fights?

Four chapters have mandatory boss fights: Chapter III (The Bloated Lycan), Chapter VI (The Abomination), Chapter XI (The Amalgam), and Chapter XII (Final Boss). Chapter VIII has the Shadow Stalker mini-boss, and Chapter IX has the optional Forgotten King boss.

FINAL THOUGHTS

The Resident Evil Requiem chapters list represents one of the most carefully structured campaigns in franchise history. Each of the 12 chapters builds upon the last, introducing new mechanics, environments, and narrative twists that keep the experience fresh from the opening moments to the final credits. Whether you are drawn to the claustrophobic tunnels of Act I, the high-stakes betrayal of Act II, or the dimensional madness of Act III, every chapter earns its place in the lineup.
