RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM FARMING GUIDE: BEST LEI, AMMO & ITEM FARMING SPOTS PER CHAPTER

UPDATED JULY 2026 • 22 MIN READ

Farming Essentials: Resource management is the backbone of survival in Resident Evil Requiem. Knowing where to farm Lei, ammunition, herbs, and upgrade materials can mean the difference between dominating every encounter and limping through the campaign with an empty inventory. This guide covers every reliable farming location across all 12 chapters, with strategies tailored to each difficulty level.

Few things feel worse in Resident Evil Requiem than facing a boss with three handgun bullets and a half-eaten Green Herb. Yet this is exactly what happens to players who don't farm efficiently between chapters. Capcom designed Requiem's economy to be tight but fair — there are always enough resources hidden in the environment if you know where to look. In this guide, we cover every high-yield farming spot in the game, ranked by efficiency, risk level, and suitability for each difficulty tier. Whether you're grinding for the Professional S+ run or just want breathing room on Standard, this guide has you covered.

UNDERSTANDING THE FARMING ECONOMY

Before diving into locations, it's important to understand how RE Requiem handles resource distribution. Unlike earlier Resident Evil titles where item spawns were almost entirely fixed, Requiem uses a semi-dynamic loot system with several key mechanics:

  • Fixed spawns: Key items, treasures, weapon parts, and collectibles always appear in the same locations on every playthrough.
  • Semi-random crates: Wooden crates and barrels contain randomized drops from a chapter-specific loot pool. These can include Lei, handgun ammo, shotgun shells, herbs, and gunpowder.
  • Enemy drops: Most enemies have a chance to drop Lei, ammo, or materials on death. Drop rates scale with difficulty — Professional offers more valuable drops but less quantity overall.
  • Respawn mechanics: Crates and certain enemies respawn when you leave and re-enter an area (typically triggered by a loading screen or chapter transition on NG+). Treasure items and collectibles do NOT respawn.
  • Merchant recycling: Selling unwanted items to the Merchant is a core part of the economy. Treasure combinations multiply value significantly — consult our Merchant Guide for optimal treasure pairing strategies.

Key Insight: The most efficient farming in RE Requiem is not grinding — it's knowing which crates to break and which enemies to kill on your natural path. A 10-minute detour for three crates is rarely worth it. A 30-second crate cluster on your way to the next objective always is.

Detailed Lei Routes

Rookwood Estate Cellar (Chapter IV): The most accessible early-game Lei farm. After defeating the Bloated Lycan boss, the cellar opens with 6 crates along the eastern corridor and 2 treasure chests in the wine storage room. Each crate drops 800–2,000 Lei on Standard. Break everything in the cellar (3 minutes), save at the workbench, then reload the save. Crates respawn on reload. This single spot can fully fund your mid-game weapon upgrades.

Dimensional Rift Marketplace (Chapter VIII): Once you enter the dimensional rift, the floating marketplace area contains 12 breakable pots and crates scattered across three interconnected platforms. Each destroyed container drops 1,000–3,000 Lei on Standard. The Shadow Wraiths in this zone also drop 500–1,500 Lei each. Clear the market fully, use the dimensional anchor to warp back to the entrance, then run it again — the containers respawn each time you warp.

NG+ Rookwood Estate (Chapter I NG+): The absolute best Lei farm in the game, but requires New Game Plus. All crates in the estate spawn with NG+-scaled loot tables. Combined with the Requiem Points you've already accumulated (which boost rare drop rates), a single full clear of Chapter I on NG+ can yield 30,000+ Lei in under 6 minutes. This is how speedrunners fund infinite rocket launcher runs on Professional.

Pro Tip: Ammo crafting at workbenches is more efficient than farming raw drops. Prioritize gunpowder pickups and save them until you know exactly which ammo type you need. On Professional difficulty, gunpowder is scarcer — craft only what you'll use in the next encounter.

Ammo Crafting vs. Farming

RE Requiem allows you to craft ammunition at any workbench using gunpowder and base materials. The crafting system is covered in depth in our Crafting Guide, but the farming-relevant insight is this: gunpowder (Large) converts to 8 handgun rounds or 4 shotgun shells. A single gunpowder pickup is worth more than 4 crate-smashing runs on average. Farm gunpowder, not bullets.

The best gunpowder farming route is in Chapter VII's Laboratory: after clearing the Abomination boss, the ammunition wing contains 3 gunpowder (Large) and 2 gunpowder (Small) pickups that respawn when you leave and re-enter the wing. This single loop takes 2 minutes and yields enough crafting material for 30+ handgun rounds or 12+ shotgun shells.

Herb Combination Priority: On Hardcore and Professional, always combine Green+Red for full heals. Save Blue herbs exclusively for boss fights — the temporary defense boost reduces damage taken by 40% and can prevent one-hit kills. The G+R+B mix is reserved for the final boss only.

Dimensional Garden (Chapter VIII): This is the single best herb farming location in the game. After unlocking the dimensional rift hub, the garden area contains 6 herb plants that fully regenerate every time you warp away and return. A 90-second loop (warp to garden → harvest all 6 → warp to market → warp back) yields 3 Green, 2 Red, and 1 Blue herb each cycle. In 15 minutes of focused farming, you can stockpile enough herbs for the entire endgame.

UPGRADE MATERIAL FARMING

Weapon upgrade materials — specifically Weapon Parts and Modification Kits — are the rarest resources in RE Requiem. While most are fixed collectibles (see our Collectibles Guide), some can be farmed repeatedly:

  • Silver Cog (Weapon Part): Dropped by Abominations in Chapter VII's laboratory. 15% drop rate. Farm the three Abominations in the containment wing, save, reload, repeat. Each kill takes ~30 seconds with a fully upgraded shotgun.
  • Gold Cog (Elite Weapon Part): Guaranteed drop from Shadow Stalkers in Chapter IX. However, Shadow Stalkers do not respawn — you get one Gold Cog per NG+ cycle. This is the primary bottleneck for fully maxing weapons.
  • Modification Kit: Only two exist per playthrough: one from the Chapter VI safe and one as an S-rank reward. They do NOT drop from enemies and are NOT farmable. Choose your modification targets carefully.
  • Gunpowder (Large): 100% drop from Bloated Lycan variants in Chapter IV and Chapter X. Three spawn in Chapter X's Convergence zone and respawn on area re-entry. This is the most efficient material farm in the game.

PROFESSIONAL DIFFICULTY FARMING STRATEGY

Professional difficulty changes everything. Enemy HP doubles, item drops are reduced by approximately 40%, and Ink Ribbons limit your saving. Your farming approach must adapt:

PROFESSIONAL FARMING DOCTRINE
THE MINIMALIST APPROACH
On Professional, every bullet spent farming must return more value than it cost. If a farming route costs 10 handgun rounds to clear and only yields 8 rounds in drops, it's a net loss. Stick to crate-only routes (no combat required), use the knife on lone enemies to save ammo, and never farm a location with more than 3 enemies unless you have a clear ammo surplus.
  1. Prioritize crate-only routes: Chapter IV Cellar, Chapter V Hospital Lobby, and Chapter VIII Dimensional Rift Marketplace all have crate clusters that require zero combat to reach on repeat visits.
  2. Knife everything you can: A single zombie takes 6–8 knife slashes to kill. If you lure one enemy at a time, the knife is a zero-cost farming tool. Practice the Perfect Dodge → knife counter for ammo-free kills.
  3. Save before every farming session: Ink Ribbons are scarce, but one ribbon spent before a 20-minute farming run is worth it. If you die mid-farm, you lose everything. The Dimensional Garden and Rookwood Cellar both have save rooms adjacent to the farming zones.
  4. Use the Bolt Thrower: On Professional, the Bolt Thrower is S-tier for farming because bolts are retrievable. Clear crate zones with bolts, recover your ammo, repeat. It's slower than a shotgun but costs zero resources.

OPTIMAL FARMING ROUTES BY CHAPTER

Here is the most efficient farming route for each chapter where farming is viable. Follow these on your first playthrough to maintain a healthy resource buffer throughout the campaign:

CHAPTER I
ROOKWOOD ESTATE — FIRST PASS
Break every crate in the Estate (there are 14 total). Focus on the kitchen pantry (4 crates) and the main hall (6 crates). You cannot return to the Estate after Chapter I on a first playthrough, so be thorough. Expected yield: ~3,000 Lei, 8–10 handgun rounds.
CHAPTER II–III
FOREST & GARDEN LOOP
After restoring power to the Estate, the forest path opens with 7 crates and 5 herb plants. Run the path from the Estate gate to the cabin and back. Crates yield 400–1,200 Lei each. Expected yield per loop: ~4,500 Lei, 4 Green herbs, 1 Blue herb.
CHAPTER IV
CELLAR CRATE FARM
The most efficient early-game Lei farm. After the Bloated Lycan boss, the cellar has 8 crates and 2 treasure chests. Save at the workbench, break everything, reload. Each run: 8,000–12,000 Lei in 3 minutes. Repeat as needed to fully upgrade your handgun and shotgun before Chapter V.
CHAPTER VIII
DIMENSIONAL RIFT HUB
The best mid-game farming zone. 12 crates in the marketplace (Lei + ammo), 6 herb plants in the garden, and 3 Shadow Wraiths that drop 500–1,500 Lei each. Warp between zones to reset spawns. Full cycle takes 6–8 minutes and yields 15,000+ Lei, 20–30 ammo rounds, and 6 herbs.
CHAPTER X
FORGOTTEN CITY TREASURY
The highest raw Lei yield in the game, but requires clearing 8 enemies. The treasury has 3 Elite treasure chests (5,000–8,000 Lei each) and 10 crates. Total yield: ~25,000 Lei per clear. Save before entering — the Shadow Wraiths in this zone hit hard.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do crates and enemies respawn in Resident Evil Requiem?

Yes, most crates, barrels, and standard enemies respawn when you leave and re-enter an area. The trigger varies by location: some require a save-and-reload, others need a chapter transition, and the Dimensional Rift zones reset when you use the warp anchors. Treasure items, key items, collectibles, and boss enemies never respawn.

What is the fastest way to farm Lei in RE Requiem?

The Chapter IV Rookwood Cellar is the fastest Lei farm accessible on a first playthrough (3 minutes for 8,000–12,000 Lei). On New Game Plus, the Chapter I Estate clear is even faster (6 minutes for 30,000+ Lei) thanks to NG+ loot scaling. The absolute best is the Chapter X Forgotten City Treasury (25,000 Lei per clear), but the combat risk makes it less efficient for pure speed.

Which difficulty is best for farming resources?

Standard difficulty provides the best balance of drop quantity and risk-free farming. Hardcore reduces drop quantities but not dramatically — it's still viable. Professional reduces drops by roughly 40% and doubles enemy HP, making combat-based farming a net resource loss. On Professional, stick exclusively to crate-only routes and knife kills.

Can I farm upgrade materials infinitely?

Partially. Silver Cogs (basic Weapon Parts) can be farmed from Abominations in Chapter VII with a 15% drop rate per kill. Gunpowder (Large) can be farmed from Bloated Lycans in Chapter X. However, Gold Cogs (elite parts) and Modification Kits are strictly limited — 1 Gold Cog per NG+ cycle and only 2 Modification Kits per playthrough. Plan your upgrades around these bottlenecks.

Is it worth farming in the early game (Chapters I–III)?

On Standard, yes — breaking every crate in Chapters I–III yields roughly 8,000 Lei and enough ammo to carry you comfortably through the first boss encounters. On Professional, early-game farming is less productive because crates drop fewer items. Focus on knife kills and perfect dodges instead, and save your farming for Chapter IV when the cellar opens.

Does the Bolt Thrower strategy work on all difficulties?

Yes. The Bolt Thrower is highly recommended for farming on Professional because bolts can be recovered from enemy corpses. It takes approximately 3–4 body shots or 1 headshot to kill a standard Infected on Professional with the Bolt Thrower. For crate-only farming routes, any weapon works — you don't need to fight anything.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Farming in Resident Evil Requiem is not mandatory — skilled players can clear the entire campaign on Professional with the resources the game provides naturally. However, for players pursuing S+ ranks, 100% completion, or simply a more comfortable survival horror experience, knowing these farming routes transforms the game's economy from punishing to generous. The key is efficiency: farm on your path, prioritize crate-only routes, and never spend more resources farming than you gain back. With the strategies in this guide, you'll never face a boss fight with an empty magazine again.
