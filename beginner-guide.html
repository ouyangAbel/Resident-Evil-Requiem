RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM BEGINNER GUIDE: 15 ESSENTIAL TIPS FOR NEW PLAYERS

JUNE 3, 2026  •  15 MIN READ
SHARE THIS

Resident Evil Requiem is one of the most ambitious entries in the franchise, blending classic survival horror tension with modern gameplay mechanics. Whether you're a veteran returning to the series or a newcomer experiencing Resident Evil for the first time, this guide will help you survive the nightmare.

Below are 15 essential tips that cover everything from combat basics to advanced resource management. Bookmark this page and refer back to it as you progress through the game.

  • Combat Fundamentals
  • Resource Management
  • Exploration & Secrets

COMBAT FUNDAMENTALS

TIP 01

AIM FOR THE HEAD, BUT KNOW WHEN TO RUN

Headshots deal significantly more damage and can stagger enemies, creating openings for follow-up attacks or escape. However, ammunition is scarce in Requiem. Not every encounter needs to be a fight — sometimes the smartest move is to dodge and conserve your resources for tougher enemies ahead.

TIP 02

MASTER THE DODGE MECHANIC

Requiem introduces an improved dodge system. Timing your dodge just before an enemy attack triggers a "Perfect Dodge," which slows time briefly and gives you a chance to counterattack. Practice this against basic enemies early — it becomes essential in boss fights.

TIP 03

USE THE ENVIRONMENT

Explosive barrels, electrical panels, and environmental traps can deal massive damage to groups of enemies. Learn to kite enemies toward these hazards before triggering them. This saves ammunition and can clear rooms efficiently.

TIP 04

KNOW YOUR WEAPONS

Different weapons serve different purposes:

  • Handguns — Reliable, moderate damage, good for general use
  • Shotguns — High damage at close range, excellent for crowd control
  • Rifles — Long range precision, ideal for picking off distant threats
  • Magnums — Extreme damage, rare ammo — save for bosses
TIP 05

DON'T FORGET THE KNIFE

Your combat knife has unlimited durability in Requiem and can be used to finish downed enemies, cut through certain obstacles, and even parry attacks in emergencies. It's your last resort — and sometimes your best friend.

RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

TIP 06

HERBS ARE YOUR LIFELINE

Green herbs restore health, red herbs boost the effect of other herbs when combined, and yellow herbs increase your maximum health. Always combine herbs in the optimal order:

  • Green + Green — Strong healing
  • Green + Red — Full heal
  • Green + Yellow — Heal + max HP increase
  • Green + Red + Yellow — Full heal + max HP increase (best combination)
TIP 07

MANAGE YOUR INVENTORY WISELY

Inventory space is limited. Prioritize:

  1. Weapons you actively use
  2. Ammunition for those weapons
  3. Healing items
  4. Key items (required for progression)

Use item boxes at save rooms to store excess items. Backtracking to retrieve something is better than running out of space mid-mission.

TIP 08

CRAFT AMMUNITION

Requiem features a crafting system. Collect gunpowder and components throughout the environment to craft ammunition at workbenches. Different combinations yield different ammo types — experiment to find what works best for your playstyle.

TIP 09

SAVE OFTEN, SAVE STRATEGICALLY

Use typewriters to save your game regularly. However, be mindful of ink ribbon supply on higher difficulties. Consider saving before:

  • Entering a new area
  • Attempting a boss fight
  • After finding rare items or completing puzzles
TIP 10

SELL TREASURE TO THE MERCHANT

Collect all treasure items (gems, artifacts, etc.) and sell them to the Merchant. Use the income to purchase weapon upgrades, additional supplies, and inventory expansions. Some treasures can be combined for higher value — check your inventory descriptions.

EXPLORATION & SECRETS

TIP 11

EXPLORE EVERYTHING

Requiem is packed with hidden items, secret rooms, and optional content. Check every corner, break every crate, and interact with objects that look suspicious. Many of the best weapons and upgrades are hidden off the beaten path.

TIP 12

READ ALL DOCUMENTS

Files and documents provide crucial information: safe codes, puzzle solutions, enemy weaknesses, and lore that enriches the story. Don't skip them — they often contain the answers you need to progress.

TIP 13

LISTEN TO AUDIO CUES

Sound design in Requiem is exceptional and gameplay-relevant. Enemy growls indicate nearby threats, music changes signal danger zones, and environmental sounds can hint at hidden passages. Play with headphones for the best experience.

BOSS STRATEGIES

TIP 14

LEARN BOSS PATTERNS

Every boss in Requiem follows identifiable attack patterns. Spend your first attempt learning these patterns rather than trying to deal maximum damage. Identify:

  • Attack wind-ups (telegraphed moves)
  • Recovery windows (safe moments to attack)
  • Weak points (glowing areas, exposed organs, etc.)
  • Phase transitions (bosses change behavior at health thresholds)
TIP 15

PREPARE BEFORE EACH BOSS FIGHT

Before entering a boss arena, make sure you have:

  • Full health (use herbs beforehand)
  • Maximum ammunition for your best weapons
  • At least 2-3 healing items
  • Ammo for your secondary weapon (backup plan)

Save your game before entering. If the fight goes badly, you can reload with your supplies intact.

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

Once you've mastered the basics, consider these advanced strategies:

  • Weapon Upgrade Priority — Upgrade damage first, then capacity, then reload speed. Damage upgrades make every shot count, which is critical when ammo is scarce.
  • New Game+ Benefits — After completing the game, you unlock New Game+ with access to all your weapons and upgrades. This is the best time to hunt for collectibles and complete challenges.
  • Difficulty Scaling — The Adaptive Difficulty system adjusts enemy behavior based on your performance. If you're doing well, enemies become more aggressive. Struggling players will find the game subtly easier.
  • Speedrun Potential — Many sections can be bypassed entirely by learning optimal routes. If you're aiming for completion times, study enemy placement and memorize key item locations.

Pro Tip: The Mercenaries mode unlocks after completing the main story. It's an excellent way to practice combat mechanics and earn bonus rewards for subsequent playthroughs.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem rewards patience, observation, and strategic thinking. Don't rush through areas. Take your time to explore, manage your resources carefully, and learn from each encounter. The game is designed to be challenging but fair — every death teaches you something.

Good luck, and remember: in the world of Resident Evil, survival is the ultimate victory.
