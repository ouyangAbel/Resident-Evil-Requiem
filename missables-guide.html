UNDERSTANDING MISSABLES IN RE REQUIEM

Before diving into the specific items, it helps to understand how missables work in Resident Evil Requiem. The game is divided into 12 chapters, each representing a distinct area. While you can freely explore within a chapter, advancing to the next chapter permanently locks you out of the previous one.

There are four categories of missables:

  • Collectibles — Documents, treasures, weapon parts, and key items hidden throughout the world. Once missed, these cannot be retrieved.
  • One-Time Events — Unique NPC interactions, scripted encounters, and environmental triggers that only occur at specific story moments.
  • Optional Bosses — Secret boss encounters that appear only under certain conditions. Miss the trigger and the fight is gone forever.
  • Point-of-No-Return Items — Items you can only grab before triggering a major story sequence. Once you trigger the sequence, the area changes and the items vanish.

Save Often: The best strategy against missables is maintaining multiple save files. Before entering a new chapter or triggering a major story event, save in a different slot. If you miss something, you can reload without losing significant progress.

CHAPTERS I-III: ROOKWOOD ESTATE

The early chapters in Rookwood Estate contain several missables that new players often overlook. The estate is large and interconnected, making it easy to miss a hidden room or optional encounter.

I
CHAPTER I • COLLECTIBLE
THE GARDENER'S DIARY

Location: Garden shed, accessible only during the daytime exploration segment.

Why it's missable: The garden shed is locked during nighttime segments. If you progress to the night phase without entering the shed, you cannot return to it later. This document reveals the code for a later puzzle and contributes to 100% document completion.

How to get it: After arriving at Rookwood Estate but before triggering the "Nightfall" story event, head to the east side of the garden. The shed is near the greenhouse. Search the desk inside to find the diary.

II
CHAPTER II • SECRET ENCOUNTER
THE WHISPERING SHADOW

Location: Second floor of the eastern wing, in the abandoned nursery.

Why it's missable: This optional encounter only appears if you explore the eastern wing after finding the nursery key but before confronting the chapter boss. If you trigger the boss fight first, the shadow entity disappears permanently.

How to get it: After obtaining the nursery key from the library, proceed to the second floor. Enter the nursery and examine the crib. The shadow appears and drops a unique accessory item. This encounter also unlocks a hidden lore document.

III
CHAPTER III • WEAPON PART
PROTOTYPE BARREL

Location: Hidden workshop beneath the estate cellar.

Why it's missable: The hidden workshop is only accessible during a brief window in Chapter III. After you activate the elevator to escape the cellar, the workshop entrance collapses and cannot be accessed again.

How to get it: In the cellar, find the cracked wall near the wine racks. Use a explosive barrel to blow it open, revealing the workshop. The prototype barrel upgrade for your shotgun is on the workbench.

Pro Tip: Chapter III has a point of no return when you use the elevator to escape. Before triggering the elevator cutscene, make sure you've fully explored the cellar and collected all items. After the elevator sequence, you cannot return to this area.

CHAPTERS IV-VI: GRACE HOSPITAL

Grace Hospital introduces more complex missables tied to NPC interactions and branching paths. Several items here require you to make specific choices at key moments.

IV
CHAPTER IV • NPC INTERACTION
DR. CHEN'S RESEARCH NOTES

Location: Research wing of Grace Hospital.

Why it's missable: Dr. Chen is an NPC you encounter early in Chapter IV. He gives you a side quest to retrieve his research notes. If you complete the chapter without finishing his quest, Dr. Chen dies during the hospital lockdown and the notes become inaccessible.

How to get it: When Dr. Chen asks you to find his notes, head to the B-2 laboratory. The notes are in a broken locker. Return them to Dr. Chen before triggering the lockdown sequence. He rewards you with a unique herb recipe.

V
CHAPTER V • OPTIONAL BOSS
THE FORGOTTEN KING

Location: Underground catacombs beneath Grace Hospital.

Why it's missable: This secret boss only appears if you collect three hidden relic fragments scattered throughout Chapters IV and V. If you enter the catacombs without all three fragments, the boss arena remains sealed.

How to get it: Fragment 1 is in Chapter IV's research wing (behind a locked door requiring the Level 3 keycard). Fragment 2 is in Chapter V's morgue (inside a body drawer). Fragment 3 is in Chapter V's chapel (on the altar). Bring all three to the catacomb entrance before leaving the hospital area.

VI
CHAPTER VI • ONE-TIME EVENT
THE PHONE CALL

Location: Hospital administrator's office.

Why it's missable: During the Leon boss fight in Chapter VI, you can find a ringing phone in the administrator's office. Answering it triggers a unique cutscene with a mysterious caller. If you defeat the boss without answering the phone, the call never happens again.

How to get it: After the first phase of the Leon boss fight, break through the wall to access the administrator's office. The phone is on the desk. Answer it to hear a cryptic message that foreshadows the Forgotten City arc.

CHAPTERS VII-IX: THE FORGOTTEN CITY

The Forgotten City arc contains the highest concentration of missables in the game. Multiple branching paths, time-sensitive events, and hidden bosses make this section particularly treacherous for completionists.

TIP 01

SAVE BEFORE THE CITY GATES

Chapter VII begins with a cinematic entering the Forgotten City. Once you pass through the massive stone gates, you cannot return to Grace Hospital. Make sure you have all Grace Hospital collectibles before this point. Create a separate save file here.

TIP 02

THE PLAGUE DOCTOR ENCOUNTER

In Chapter VIII, wearing the Plague Doctor Mask when entering the underground temple triggers a unique boss fight against the "Crimson Surgeon." Without the mask, the room is empty. The mask is found in the flooded district — search the sunken pharmacy on the east side of the district.

CHAPTERS X-XII: FINAL DESCENT

The final three chapters have fewer missables due to the linear nature of the endgame, but the ones that exist are critical. Missing any of these can lock you out of the true ending or the platinum trophy.

X
CHAPTER X • STORY BRANCH
SARAH'S FATE

Location: Abandoned research station.

Why it's missable: During Chapter X, you encounter Sarah trapped behind a collapsed wall. You can either spend time digging her out (delaying your progress) or leave her and continue immediately. If you leave her, she dies and you miss her unique ending scene and the "Savior" trophy.

How to get it: Choose to dig Sarah out. It takes about 5 minutes of gameplay, and you miss some combat encounters as a result. Sarah survives and appears in the final chapter, providing crucial assistance.

XI
CHAPTER XI • HIDDEN ITEM
THE FINAL RECORDING

Location: Deep within the dimensional rift.

Why it's missable: In Chapter XI, the dimensional rift area has a hidden alcove visible only when facing a specific direction. The alcove contains the final recording from the main villain, explaining the true nature of Requiem. If you progress through the rift without finding it, the recording is lost forever.

How to get it: In the rift area, look for a flickering wall texture on the northwest side. Walk through the illusion to find the alcove. Play the recording for crucial story context.

XII
CHAPTER XII • ENDING REQUIREMENT
THE TRUE ENDING

Location: Final boss arena.

Why it's missable: The true ending requires you to have collected all optional documents, saved Sarah in Chapter X, and defeated The Forgotten King in Chapter V. If any of these conditions are unmet, you get the standard ending instead. There is no way to replay just the final chapter.

How to get it: Follow this guide carefully to ensure all prerequisites are met. During the final boss, when prompted, choose to "Remember" rather than "Forget" to trigger the true ending sequence.

MISSABLE NPC INTERACTIONS

Several NPCs throughout the game offer unique interactions that cannot be replayed. Missing these does not block progression but does reduce your completion percentage and locks you out of optional content.

  • Dr. Chen (Chapter IV) — Side quest for research notes. Missable if chapter is completed without finishing his quest.
  • The Merchant (Chapter VI) — Offers a unique weapon for sale only during Chapter VI. After progressing, he relocates and the weapon is gone.
  • Sarah (Chapter X) — Rescue side quest affecting the ending. Missable if you choose to leave her.
  • Old Man Rivers (Chapter II) — Provides cryptic hints about hidden treasure locations. If you don't speak to him before Chapter III, he disappears.
  • The Ghost Nurse (Chapter V) — Appears only if you examine the nurse's station three times. Provides a key item for the hospital's secret room.

NPC Tip: When you meet a new NPC, always exhaust all dialogue options and check back with them after major story events. Many NPCs have multiple interaction points that trigger at different times.

FAQ: RESIDENT EVIL REQUIEM MISSABLES

What happens if you miss a collectible in Resident Evil Requiem?

Missed collectibles cannot be recovered in your current playthrough. Unlike some games that offer a chapter select or free play mode, Requiem locks you out of previous chapters permanently. You must either reload a previous save file or collect the item in a New Game+ run. This is why maintaining multiple save files is crucial.

Are there any missable trophies in RE Requiem?

Yes, several trophies are tied to optional content. The most notable missable trophies are "The Forgotten King" (defeat the secret boss in Chapter V), "Savior" (save Sarah in Chapter X), "True Ending" (meet all prerequisites), and "Crimson Surgeon" (defeat the optional boss in Chapter VIII). All of these are required for platinum.

Can you get 100% completion in one playthrough?

Yes, 100% completion in a single playthrough is achievable but requires careful planning and a guide. The game does not prevent you from collecting everything in one run, but the sheer number of missable items makes it extremely difficult without prior knowledge. New Game+ allows you to carry over progress, so if you miss something, your second run will be easier.

Is there a chapter select in Resident Evil Requiem?

No, Resident Evil Requiem does not feature a chapter select system. Once you advance from one chapter to the next, you cannot return to previous areas. The only way to backtrack is by loading a save file from the earlier chapter or starting a New Game+. This makes save file management essential for completionists.

What is the biggest missable in the game?

The biggest missable is the "True Ending" sequence. To unlock it, you must save Sarah in Chapter X, defeat The Forgotten King in Chapter V, and collect all hidden documents throughout the game. Missing any of these conditions locks you out of the true ending and the associated trophy. The true ending also provides the most complete story resolution.

Do items from New Game+ carry over?

Weapons, upgrades, and collectibles found in previous playthroughs carry over to New Game+. However, documents and key items reset each playthrough. This means if you missed a document in your first run, you can collect it in New Game+ without needing to re-find treasures or weapon parts.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Resident Evil Requiem's missable content adds tremendous replay value but can be frustrating for completionists who want everything in one run. Use this guide as your chapter-by-chapter checklist, maintain multiple save files, and you'll never have to worry about permanently missing content again.

Remember: the game rewards thorough exploration and careful planning. Take your time in each chapter, talk to every NPC, and search every corner. The items you find off the beaten path are often the most valuable.
